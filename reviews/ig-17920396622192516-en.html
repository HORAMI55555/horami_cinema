The Silence

2019

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5Scare2/5

Make a sound and the swarm will find you.

Synopsis

When creatures that hunt by sound overrun the cities, deaf teenager Ally flees with her family. They can sign to each other in hiding, but the strangers they encounter have their own rules for survival.

My thoughts

The second I saw the premise I thought of A Quiet Place, and that’s exactly why I gave it a go. If you loved that film, this feels like spending the same kind of soundless nightmare with a different family. I really enjoyed it.

Cast

Kiernan Shipka, Stanley Tucci, Miranda Otto

Trivia

Tim Lebbon’s novel came out in 2015. If the film reminds you of A Quiet Place, the order is a neat surprise: this family was already keeping quiet on the printed page years before either film reached the screen.
