The End of the F***ing World

2017

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5

They run away together for entirely different reasons.

Synopsis

James thinks he might be a psychopath. Alyssa can't stand her life at home. They meet at school and take off together, each disastrously mistaken about what the other wants from the trip.

My thoughts

A non-horror pick. This one's so much fun. I watched it on Netflix, and if you've got Netflix, this is the one I'd recommend.

Cast

Alex Lawther, Jessica Barden

Trivia

The series adapts Charles Forsman's comic. Voiceovers sit beside the teenagers' clipped exchanges, so we hear the wildly different meanings they give the very same silence.
