The Deliverance

2024

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★2/5Scare2.5/5

Keeping a family together becomes a fight for survival.

Synopsis

Ebony moves her family into a new home hoping for a fresh start. When her children begin behaving in disturbing ways, outsiders question her care rather than the house itself. Domestic pressures and an increasingly sinister presence collide, leaving her struggling to protect the children while making anyone believe her.

My thoughts

Oh, an exorcism horror! Mostly I kept feeling sorry for the mother. The way poverty leaves her struggling with so little relief was painful. The possession story itself is fairly ordinary, so it was not especially exciting for me, but fine as a way to pass the time.

Cast

Andra Day, Glenn Close, Mo’Nique, Aunjanue Ellis-Taylor

Trivia

Lee Daniels told Netflix that he did not approach this only as horror in the conventional sense. The film spends substantial time with Ebony’s circumstances and family relationships alongside the haunting, with Andra Day, Glenn Close and Mo’Nique among the cast. A revealing viewing question is whose explanation people accept when something frightening happens. Paying attention to how others judge Ebony gives the domestic drama an additional weight before the supernatural threat takes over.
