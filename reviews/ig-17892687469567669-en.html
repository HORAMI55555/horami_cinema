何がジェーンに起こったか？

1962

Scare0/5

One sister still hears the applause. The other needs someone to hear her.

Synopsis

Former child star Jane shares a Hollywood mansion with her sister Blanche, once a film star and now dependent on a wheelchair. Jane controls the meals and the telephone. An old rivalry becomes terrifying when one sister controls every contact with the outside world.

My thoughts

I keep seeing this high on horror lists and I don't quite get it. I wasn't scared, but that ending did make me go, “Ohhh!”

Cast

Bette Davis, Joan Crawford

Trivia

Norma Koch designed the costumes, including the outfits Jane clings to from her childhood act. Her work won the Academy Award for black-and-white costume design, a category separate from colour films at the time.
