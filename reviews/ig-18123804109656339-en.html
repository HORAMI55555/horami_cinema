Mama

2013

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5Scare3/5

A haunting that leaves room for heartbreak.

Synopsis

Two young sisters who vanished after losing their parents are discovered alive in a woodland cabin. Their uncle and his girlfriend Annabel take them in, hoping to help them adjust to ordinary life. But the presence the girls call Mama may have followed them home.

My thoughts

This was my second watch. It has plenty of horror, but the story is so sad... A ghost grieving her lost child and children without their parents, longing for a mother figure, becoming dependent on each other. I actually cried! At a horror film! I’d recommend this one too, although the ending had me going, noooo, why?!

Cast

Jessica Chastain · Nikolaj Coster-Waldau · Megan Charpentier · Isabelle Nélisse

Trivia

Directed by Andy Muschietti, the film grew out of a short; the official extras explore that transition. Viewing tip: follow how Annabel’s relationship with the sisters changes alongside the haunting.
