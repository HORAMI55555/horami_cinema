Ginny & Georgia

2021

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5

A mother and daughter as close as friends—and secrets that cut deeper because of it.

Synopsis

Free-spirited Georgia moves to an affluent town with her 15-year-old daughter Ginny and son Austin. As Ginny navigates friendship and romance, her mother’s hidden past threatens their fresh start. A drama about the love and friction between a mother and daughter trying to begin again.

My thoughts

I wasn’t expecting much when I started, but this was so, so good. Some parts are really heavy and dark and hit hard, but it’s incredibly absorbing. It really made me appreciate how fortunate I am and how important family is. Apparently there are more seasons coming! I can’t wait! If you’re feeling emotionally up to it, I highly recommend this—please watch it. Personally, I found it harder to watch than 13 Reasons Why.

Cast

Brianne Howey, Antonia Gentry, Diesel La Torraca

Trivia

Netflix’s official recap notes that Ginny is 15 when the story begins—the same age Georgia was when she gave birth to her. Their age gap is a key to their relationship, set in affluent Wellsbury, Massachusetts. Viewing tip: watch the gap between what Georgia conceals to protect her daughter and what Ginny feels entitled to know. The same event can mean something different to each of them, revealing tension beneath the lively dialogue.
