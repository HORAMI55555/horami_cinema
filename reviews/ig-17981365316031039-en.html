Tin & Tina

2023

Innocent faith becomes a family’s nightmare.

Synopsis

After a traumatic miscarriage, a young couple adopts twins Tin and Tina from a convent. They hope to rebuild their family, but the children have an intense attachment to religious teachings. As the twins’ peculiar faith enters daily life, the household becomes increasingly uneasy and frightening.

My thoughts

Loved it—if you like Orphan, you’ll definitely like this. It feels like Orphan without the age deception, with religion and a Midsommar vibe added. The intense religious atmosphere was so well done. Really good; I’m satisfied. Please watch these two little psychopaths adopted from an orphanage. What feels fresh is that they aren’t aware of it and aren’t doing it deliberately, which is surprisingly rare in horror. No ghosts appear.

Cast

Milena Smit, Jaime Lorente, Anastasia Russo

Trivia

Netflix classifies this Spanish horror film as set in the 1980s, and its official synopsis places the twins’ religious obsession at the center of the family’s disturbance. Viewing tip: follow the different ways adults interpret the children’s behavior. Judging it only by whether there is malicious intent may leave you even more uneasy. Notice how literally religious language is taken, and consider why the twins’ innocence can feel frightening.
