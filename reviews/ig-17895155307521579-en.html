Sound of Freedom

2023

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5

One rescue leads to another child still missing.

Synopsis

Investigating child trafficking, agent Tim learns that a rescued boy’s sister is still missing. His search for her takes him deeper into a dangerous criminal world beyond national borders. Inspired by the work of a real person, this suspense drama follows an effort to bring vulnerable children to safety.

My thoughts

This was such a shock. Knowing it was based on real events made me wonder how things like this can be happening around the world. It also got me thinking more broadly about exploitation and forced labor, and whether some cheap clothes could have hidden circumstances behind them. A disturbing film, but one I think is worth seeing.

Cast

Jim Caviezel · Bill Camp · Mira Sorvino

Trivia

Its US release drew attention for a pay-it-forward ticket program: viewers could buy tickets for other people. It gave audiences a direct way to turn “someone else should see this” into a gifted cinema visit.
