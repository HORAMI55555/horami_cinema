Jailbirds

2019

Even these walls can't stop a conversation.

Synopsis

Inside California's Sacramento County Jail, incarcerated women find their own ways to communicate, maintain friendships and pursue relationships.

My thoughts

I feel like I've watched every Netflix prison series now. Jailbirds is an actual documentary, and that realness set it apart from the others. Loved it.

Cast

Yasmin Sundermeyer and Rebecca Temme (as themselves)

Trivia

When participants mention the bowl, they mean the toilet. Conversations through the plumbing reveal a network of relationships you couldn't guess from the cell doors alone.
