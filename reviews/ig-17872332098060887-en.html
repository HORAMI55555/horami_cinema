In a village without night, you can see the horror perfectly.

Synopsis

Grieving Dani joins her boyfriend and his friends on a trip to a Swedish midsummer festival. They are welcomed with flowers and white linen. The longer they stay, the less they understand what their hosts expect of a guest.

My thoughts

First the gore wrecked me, then I had no idea what I’d just watched. I want my time back.

Cast

Florence Pugh, Jack Reynor

Trivia

After Hereditary, Ari Aster set his next horror story at a sunlit festival. Let your eyes leave the foreground during a conversation and look for villagers moving together in the distance. Being able to see everything is half the discomfort.
