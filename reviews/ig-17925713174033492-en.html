The guilty

2021

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5

He has a caller's voice. That's his entire view of the crime.

Synopsis

On emergency dispatch duty, Joe receives a call from a woman who seems to have been abducted. She can barely speak in front of the person beside her. He tries to locate her by listening to what she can safely say.

My thoughts

I loved this. I was on edge the whole time, and suddenly it was over. I didn't get bored once. It starts with a 911 call, and I loved how much could unfold entirely on that end of the line. That setup felt so fresh to me.

Cast

Jake Gyllenhaal, Riley Keough, Peter Sarsgaard

Trivia

This remake moves the Danish thriller to Los Angeles. Riley Keough and other off-screen actors have to convey the other end of each call through breath and hesitation, without a single reaction shot.
