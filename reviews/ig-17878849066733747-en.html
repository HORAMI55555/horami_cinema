The Boy

2016

Scare2/5

Babysitting rules still apply when the child is porcelain.

Synopsis

Greta takes a nanny job at an English estate. Her charge is Brahms, a doll his parents treat as a living child. Left in charge, she skips a few instructions and starts noticing objects and sounds where they shouldn't be.

My thoughts

A bit under the radar, and one I really want to recommend. It looks like one kind of horror, then goes somewhere else. The final stretch is so good! The beginning might be a little scary if horror isn't usually your thing.

Cast

Lauren Cohan, Rupert Evans, Jim Norton

Trivia

The written rules cover everything from food to reading aloud. They're a clever prop: we learn the house's routine quickly enough to keep count ourselves when Greta starts breaking it.
