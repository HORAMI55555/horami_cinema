Orphan

2009

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5

How can a little ribbon make me this uneasy?

Synopsis

After losing a daughter, Kate and John adopt Esther, a polite young artist they meet at an orphanage. As accidents start happening around the children, Kate begins to suspect their new daughter. Convincing John is another matter.

My thoughts

Fourth time watching Orphan and I could honestly go again. This is one for the psychological horror history books. A couple takes in a little girl and something about her is seriously off. I was so excited when I heard a second film was coming. For me it’s the psychological side that earns four stars.

Cast

Vera Farmiga, Isabelle Fuhrman, Peter Sarsgaard

Trivia

Esther’s ribbons go all the way back to the audition. Fuhrman showed up in an old-fashioned dress with ribbons tied around her neck and wrists. She had worked out that unmistakable silhouette before she even had the part.
