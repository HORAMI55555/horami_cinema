The Notebook

2004

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★5/5

He starts their story again, right from the beginning.

Synopsis

In a care home, an older man reads to a woman from a worn notebook. Its pages return to the summer Noah and Allie met, and to a love their families and different circumstances made difficult to sustain.

My thoughts

Oh, this is lovely. I'm crying. Modern romances are great too, but there's something about a simple love story like this that gets straight to me.

Cast

Ryan Gosling, Rachel McAdams, Gena Rowlands

Trivia

Gena Rowlands, who plays the older Allie, was director Nick Cassavetes's mother. The reading scenes carry another family connection behind the camera: a son watching his mother's face.
