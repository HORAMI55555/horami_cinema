Lamb

2021

Scare0.5/5

Their new family crosses a boundary nature remembers.

Synopsis

On an isolated Icelandic farm, María and Ingvar discover a strange newborn in their sheep barn. They welcome the child as their own, finding renewed joy in their quiet life. But beneath that happiness, an unsettling presence from the natural world begins to make itself felt.

My thoughts

I was late getting to this, but I finally watched it. It was pretty well known, so I expected full-on animal horror with some psycho animal attacking people, but it was completely different. If you go in expecting horror, it can end with you still confused and going, “??” Roughly speaking, it has a Midsommar-like atmosphere with a little Greek mythology and strange animals mixed in. The ending had me going, “Wait, seriously?” People wanting a conventional horror movie might not feel very satisfied. It may suit people who want something to think about.

Cast

Noomi Rapace, Hilmir Snær Guðnason, Björn Hlynur Haraldsson

Trivia

This was Valdimar Jóhannsson’s first feature. According to the official press notes, the crew numbered only thirty-five, and Noomi Rapace had never worked with such a small team in such isolated surroundings. The director wanted María to combine coldness with care. Rapace’s performance in Icelandic and the crew’s work turning unusual ideas into physical reality help give the quiet farm its uncanny conviction.
