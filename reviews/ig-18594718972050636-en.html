Lucy

2014

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5

What happens when human limits disappear?

Synopsis

Living in Taiwan, Lucy is forced to carry a mysterious substance inside her body. When it leaks, her senses, thinking and physical abilities begin changing at extraordinary speed. Seeking to understand the transformation, she reaches out to a researcher. This science-fiction action film imagines what might happen if human capabilities expanded far beyond their familiar limits.

My thoughts

This one is famous!!! The idea of unlocking 100 percent of the brain’s capacity is such an interesting sci-fi setup. I kept thinking, oh, so THAT’S where it goes next! It was entertaining and kept my eyes on the screen. Recommend!

Trivia
