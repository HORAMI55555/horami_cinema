The Purge

2013

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5Scare2/5

One night a year, the law looks away.

Synopsis

In a future America, nearly all crime is legal for one twelve-hour period each year. The Sandin family plans to wait it out behind their home’s expensive security system. When a stranger is allowed inside, the danger outside turns toward them. Their supposedly secure home becomes a trap.

My thoughts

Such an interesting idea!! One night when even murder is legal? Terrifying. The people who want to kill seem thrilled, while everyone else has to be absolutely terrified. I got annoyed by some decisions in the second half—why would you do that?!—but I still had fun. The concept is really good.

Cast

Ethan Hawke, Lena Headey, Max Burkholder

Trivia

James DeMonaco has traced part of the idea to a road-rage incident involving him and his wife. Something she said afterward helped spark the premise of a night without legal consequences.
