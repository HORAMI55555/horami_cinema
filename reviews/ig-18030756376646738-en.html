Every new chapter brings another death.

Synopsis

Eight university students who love horror fiction conceal a fatal prank. An anonymous writer then threatens to publish a horror novel starring them, with one student dying whenever a chapter appears. As suspicion fractures the group, they must fight to survive their own unfolding story.

My thoughts

This is so much like Scary Stories, haha. It’s about a story being written into a book without their control, and the people named in it die in that order, in the ways it describes!! Good for killing time!

Cast

Veki Velilla, Álvaro Mel, Priscilla Delgado

Trivia

Netflix categorizes this Spanish horror film as a book adaptation. Its book club consists of eight university students who meet weekly; the stalker threatens to publish a novel about them on social media. Viewing tip: track not only suspicious behavior but also who knows the story’s information and who is reading it. Horror fans become unwilling characters themselves, combining a hunt for the killer with the dread of another chapter appearing.
