The F**k-It List

2020

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★2/5

His perfect future collapses. His honest regrets go viral.

Synopsis

Straight-A student Brett seems set for an elite college until a prank wrecks his carefully planned future. He posts a list of everything he missed while trying to be perfect, and it strikes a chord with teenagers everywhere. Sudden attention forces him to ask what he wants beyond other people’s expectations.

My thoughts

Another American high-school story! This one’s about a guy who’s followed the perfect path, then loses his mind the moment he gets into good colleges and ruins his life. It’s refreshing and funny.

Cast

Eli Brown, Madison Iseman, Marcus Scribner

Trivia

Eli Brown leads as Brett, alongside Madison Iseman as Kayla and Marcus Scribner as his friend Clint. Jerry O’Connell and Natalie Zea join the adult cast, and director Michael Duggan also produced. Awesomeness Films is the studio behind the project. Its central reversal captures a distinctly online coming-of-age story: Brett’s own words connect with his peers more powerfully than the college acceptances that once defined his success.
