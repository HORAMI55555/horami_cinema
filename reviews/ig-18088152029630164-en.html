A Quiet Place: Day One

2024

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5Scare2/5

When the city falls silent, what keeps you going?

Synopsis

Samira’s visit to New York becomes a nightmare when creatures that hunt by sound attack. With her cat Frodo and an unexpected companion, Eric, she makes her way through a suddenly silent city. As everyone struggles to survive, the journey also becomes a question of what matters most to her.

My thoughts

Finally got around to this! Another entry in the A Quiet Place series. It uses the same setup, but this time the main character already knows she doesn’t have long to live. Putting her in this world creates such an unusual emotional balance. An easy way to spend more time in the A Quiet Place universe without overthinking it. Maybe fewer absolutely terrifying jump scares than the earlier films!

Cast

Lupita Nyong’o · Joseph Quinn

Trivia

Michael Sarnoski wrote and directed this installment. John Krasinski contributed to the story and served as a producer. Viewing tip: notice how the two leads communicate without relying on speech.
