Smile

2022

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5Scare3/5

One smile can shatter everything.

Synopsis

After witnessing a traumatic incident involving a patient, psychiatrist Rose experiences terrifying phenomena she cannot explain. The disturbances increasingly take over her life and strain her relationships. To escape, she must confront her own painful past.

My thoughts

Yay! I’d wanted to see this for so long, so I’m happy I finally did! It’s similar to It Follows: some evil thing keeps showing up as different people, which is fun. A smile really can look sinister, and having a counselor as the main character worked well too. There are frequent jump scares, so I wouldn’t recommend it if you can’t handle those. Otherwise, I recommend it! For a horror movie it isn’t that scary—just fun!

Cast

Sosie Bacon, Jessie T. Usher, Kyle Gallner

Trivia

Parker Finn wrote and directed the film. Paramount’s official synopsis emphasizes Rose’s need to confront her painful past alongside the supernatural events. Viewing tip: observe how a normally reassuring smile can become disturbing in a different context. A doctor who supports patients must now explain her own experiences to others. Following that reversal can sustain the unease between the shocks.
