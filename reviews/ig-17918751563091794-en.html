She’s live on camera. She’s also locked out.

Synopsis

Alice makes her living livestreaming. Locked out of her account, she finds a woman with her exact face performing for her regular viewers. Everyone seems happy to carry on watching.

My thoughts

I watched this because it ranked high on an American horror list, but I didn’t find it scary. It reminded me a little of another social-media horror film. Someone hijacks her account and starts streaming with her face. I’m still not sure what to make of it!

Cast

Madeline Brewer

Trivia

Screenwriter Isa Mazzei had worked as a cam performer. Notice the routine Alice builds around a camera angle or a familiar viewer. By the time the double takes over, you know exactly how much work went into the identity on screen.
