Scary Stories to Tell in the Dark

2019

Scare2/5

The book is writing about you.

Synopsis

On Halloween, Stella and her friends find a book of scary stories in an old mansion. New words appear on its pages, casting the friends in tales of their own.

My thoughts

That was fun! It felt like a new kind of story. Didn't scare me, but the actual story was interesting enough. Even people who struggle with horror might only be half dead afterward!

Cast

Zoe Colletti, Michael Garza, Gabriel Rush

Trivia

Stephen Gammell's illustrations for the original books helped shape the creatures. Designs readers once saw as blotchy silhouettes on paper now have weight and movement.
