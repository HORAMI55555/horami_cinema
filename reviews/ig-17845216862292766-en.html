Scare2/5

They want to hang up. The caller died a year ago.

Synopsis

An unknown account joins a group video call under the name of a classmate who died after being humiliated online. Casual chatter turns into an interrogation as the friends' private messages and secrets start surfacing.

My thoughts

I'd really give this one a miss. Nothing scared me, and I couldn't make sense of it.

Cast

Shelley Hennig, Moses Storm, Renee Olstead

Trivia

Although the film unfolds on a laptop, the actors performed the conversations in long takes. Look at the quiet windows during an argument: someone listening on screen can be reacting in the same stretch of time as the person speaking.
