Rise of the Planet of the Apes

2011

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5

Humanity’s gift of intelligence becomes its greatest challenge.

Synopsis

An experimental drug gives chimpanzee Caesar extraordinary intelligence. Raised like family by researcher Will, he is eventually confined after a conflict with humans. Meeting other captive apes sets him on a path that will transform the relationship between humanity and his own kind.

My thoughts

Oh, I watched this one too! I started it thinking it probably wouldn’t be interesting, but it felt pretty realistic and had me nervous and on the edge of my seat. I had so much fun, haha. There’s no other movie that makes apes look this cool!!

Cast

Andy Serkis, James Franco

Trivia

Andy Serkis plays Caesar, with James Franco as his human guardian. The official synopsis emphasizes human-like emotions as well as the intelligence gained from the experimental drug. Viewing tip: follow whose gaze Caesar meets and where he looks for belonging. Comparing his life as a family member with his confinement gives the eventual uprising the shape of a personal search for dignity, alongside the large-scale action.
