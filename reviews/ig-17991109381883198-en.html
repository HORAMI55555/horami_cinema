Why does the girl in that haunted video have my face?

Synopsis

High schooler Kanon sees a video of a girl forced to wear an ox-head mask and locked inside an abandoned building. The girl looks exactly like her. Following the mystery to Tsubono Mineral Springs in Hokuriku, Kanon uncovers connections to twins and the terrifying customs of a place called Ox-Head Village.

My thoughts

I’d been curious because Koki, Kimutaku’s daughter, is in it, so I watched! For a horror fan it isn’t quite enough, but if you’re bad with horror and still want to try one, this should be enough!

Cast

Kōki, Riku Hagiwara, Fumiya Takahashi, Haruka Imou

Trivia

Kōki, made both her film debut and her debut as a lead here, taking on the additional challenge of playing two high-school-age sisters. Takashi Shimizu directed this third entry in the Village of Terror series after Howling Village and Suicide Forest Village. Following Kyushu and the forest around Mount Fuji, this chapter moves to Hokuriku locations including Tsubono Mineral Springs. Each film’s shift in geography brings a new place and a new central character into the series’ approach to fear.
