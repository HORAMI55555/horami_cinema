Julia(s)

2022

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3.5/5

A different moment. Another life worth living.

Synopsis

Julia dreams of becoming a pianist, but tiny accidents send her life down different paths. A dropped book, a chance meeting or a single decision can reshape love, family and ambition. Following several possible versions of one woman’s life, the film explores the happiness and losses that each path can hold.

My thoughts

This is lovely… It reminded me of Look Both Ways, one of my favourites. Just because something goes wrong doesn’t mean there isn’t a good life waiting down that other path. It’s amazing how one second can change who we meet and everything that follows. Even when you think it’s all over, it isn’t. A really worthwhile French film.

Cast

Lou de Laâge · Raphaël Personnaz · Isabelle Carré · Grégory Gadebois

Trivia

The different lives were not filmed one after another. Lou de Laâge sometimes switched between versions of Julia within the same day, using costumes, hair and her scene partners to reconnect with each life’s emotional history. It is a quietly demanding performance built from constant changes of perspective.
