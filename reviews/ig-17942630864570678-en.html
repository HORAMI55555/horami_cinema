Meru

2015

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5

Three climbers, an impossible wall, and the will to return.

Synopsis

Conrad Anker, Jimmy Chin and Renan Ozturk attempt the Shark’s Fin route on Mount Meru in the Himalayas. Facing severe conditions and major personal setbacks, the three climbers entrust their lives to one another’s skill and judgment in this documentary about ambition, resilience and trust.

My thoughts

I love Everest so much that I’ve watched all the movies, so I wanted to try Meru too—and it was wonderful. Each of them has such an intense personal story; it was a really good documentary. How can they keep climbing something that steep, where one fall would mean instant death? It’s incredible. Even if you know nothing about climbing, this will move you.

Cast

Conrad Anker, Jimmy Chin, Renan Ozturk

Trivia

The cliffside footage was filmed by Jimmy Chin and Renan Ozturk, who were also making the climb. Chin additionally co-directed with Elizabeth Chai Vasarhelyi. Because participants and camera operators overlap, the images offer the intimacy of being alongside the team as well as the scale of the mountain. The official credits also include climber and writer Jon Krakauer, bringing additional voices to a film built around the three men’s firsthand experience.
