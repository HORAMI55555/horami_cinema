The Autopsy of Jane Doe

2016

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5Scare4/5

No visible injuries. Then comes the first incision.

Synopsis

A father and son receive an unidentified woman's body at their morgue. Her skin looks untouched, yet every stage of their examination reveals another injury that shouldn't be possible.

My thoughts

Now that's proper horror. I get why it's famous for being scary. I'd steer clear unless you're a horror fan. The first half was so gory I could barely watch, but I loved the second half. The setup was new to me and the occult angle really hit the spot. If you can handle gore and want the real deal, watch this.

Cast

Brian Cox, Emile Hirsch, Olwen Kelly

Trivia

Jane Doe herself has an acting credit. Olwen Kelly's stillness makes it almost impossible not to watch for a flicker in her eyelids or a breath in her chest.
