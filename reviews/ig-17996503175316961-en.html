Barracuda Queens

2023

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★2/5

These privileged girls have an after-dark secret.

Synopsis

In an affluent Swedish neighborhood in 1995, a group of girls tries burglary to pay off an extravagant party bill. Their reckless plan brings an unexpected recruit and escalates into repeated thefts from wealthy neighbors. Their respectable daytime lives begin colliding with their nightly search for thrills.

My thoughts

I was so happy because dramas set in Sweden are so rare! It’s about a realistic group of four Swedish university girls committing loads of robberies. Somehow its simplicity made it feel so Swedish, and personally I loved that.

Cast

Alva Bratt, Tindra Monsen, Sandra Zubovic, Tea Stjärne, Sarah Gustafsson

Trivia

Netflix describes the story as loosely inspired by real events and places it in 1995, as the girl-power era begins. The name comes from the beach where the girls partied. Amina, whose home the original four plan to rob, becomes another member. Viewing tip: notice how appearing wealthy and respectable affects who attracts suspicion. Class shapes perception alongside the thrills of the crimes.
