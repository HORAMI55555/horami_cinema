Mufasa: The Lion King

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5

Before he became king, he was an orphan.

Synopsis

Rafiki tells Kiara the story of young Mufasa, an orphan who befriends Taka, heir to a royal bloodline. Their bond takes them on a journey with an unlikely group of companions in search of a new home. Long before he becomes the king we know, Mufasa must discover what kind of lion he wants to be.

My thoughts

I saw this at the cinema!!! The songs were so good, and the animals looked unbelievably real. I actually went in without knowing The Lion King’s story, and that was totally fine. Lions are just so impressive. I’ve seen almost all of Disney’s live-action-style remakes, and this was another really good one. Disney making a simple story this moving,, honestly incredible. Loved it!

Cast

Aaron Pierre, Kelvin Harrison Jr., Tiffany Boone (voices)

Trivia

The original songs were written by Hamilton creator Lin-Manuel Miranda, while Moonlight filmmaker Barry Jenkins directed—an unexpected creative pairing.
