The Boogeyman

2023

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★2/5Scare3/5

In a house full of grief, the darkness is watching.

Synopsis

After their mother’s death, teenager Sadie and her younger sister Sawyer struggle to connect with their grieving father. When a troubled man visits his therapy practice at home, he leaves something terrifying behind. The sisters must confront an entity that feeds on their family’s suffering.

My thoughts

I went in with high hopes because it was Stephen King, but it was no different from any other horror movie!! So predictable!! Just one jump scare after another, and the story wasn’t great either. Maybe this was a difficult story to turn into a movie. Anyway, it felt like watching a pretty average horror film.

Cast

Sophie Thatcher, Chris Messina, Vivien Lyra Blair

Trivia

Based on a Stephen King short story, the film is directed by Rob Savage and written by Scott Beck, Bryan Woods and Mark Heyman. The official synopsis describes an entity that attacks families and feeds on suffering. Viewing tip: alongside the darkness, watch how the sisters and their father communicate after losing their mother. The fear takes on another dimension when supposedly safe domestic spaces overlap with the distance created by unshared grief.
