Door Lock

2018

Scare3/5

A locked door should mean no one got in.

Synopsis

Living alone, Kyung-min notices that the cover on her digital lock is open. After hearing someone try the keypad at night, she begins looking at the tiniest changes in her flat as possible signs of an intruder.

My thoughts

That genuinely scared me. It gets under your skin, and even the little bit of gore was too much for me. Good movie. It's the kind of fear that feels real. For sheer scariness I'd give it three out of five.

Cast

Gong Hyo-jin, Kim Ye-won, Kim Sung-oh

Trivia

Inspired by Spain's Sleep Tight, the Korean film shifts the viewpoint to the woman whose home is being invaded. We notice disturbances alongside her, until even a familiar piece of furniture starts to look suspect.
