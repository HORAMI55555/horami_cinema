Senior Year

2022

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3.5/5

Seventeen at heart, thirty-seven and chasing prom queen.

Synopsis

A cheerleading accident leaves popular teenager Stephanie in a coma. Twenty years later, she wakes up at thirty-seven and returns to high school to finish the dreams she had at seventeen. School culture has completely changed, however, and chasing the prom-queen crown means navigating a new generation and making new friends.

My thoughts

Another one of my usual American high-school movies! Wow, this had me going, “Wait, THAT happens?!” The surprises were huge. But it was wonderful—kind of like 17 Again. The cheerleaders are cute.

Cast

Rebel Wilson, Zoë Chao, Sam Richardson

Trivia

The re-creation of Britney Spears’s “Crazy” video began as a brief script direction before fan Rebel Wilson pushed for a full tribute. Spears approved the music use and re-creation. The crew rebuilt the set and even researched the original lenses and camera angles; Wilson’s green top was custom-made, and the cast rehearsed for weeks. Her fall from a chair was an unplanned mistake that she simply worked into the performance.
