Gonjiam: Haunted Asylum

2018

Scare2/5

The viewer count climbs. Help doesn't.

Synopsis

An online crew livestreams an expedition into an abandoned psychiatric hospital. Some scares have been arranged for the viewers; soon even the people behind those tricks can't account for what's appearing on their feeds.

My thoughts

I watched because the hospital actually existed in Korea and it sounded intriguing. Afterwards I wanted my money back. Oof. It just kept trying to make me jump.

Cast

Wi Ha-jun, Park Ji-hyun, Park Sung-hoon

Trivia

Some of the crew's cameras point back at their faces. That lets the film give you a close view of someone's reaction before it offers a clear view of whatever is waiting at the end of the corridor.
