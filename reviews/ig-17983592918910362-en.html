Synopsis

Two years after M3GAN’s rampage, her technology has been repurposed to create a military AI called Amelia. When the new machine stops obeying humans, Gemma turns to an unlikely solution: bringing M3GAN back, faster and stronger. The former threat may now be their best chance of survival.

My thoughts

The second M3GAN film! Apparently its planned Japanese theatrical release was cancelled. After the first one, this had me thinking, wait, THAT’S where we’re going?! It actually made me happy. Maybe you can connect with machines emotionally too 🥺 The story is about as light as before, so it’s perfect for switching off, taking a break or watching with friends. Almost no scares for me this time—more of a fun action film!

Trivia

M3GAN’s animatronic hands worked almost too well: they moved so naturally that the team deliberately made them less fluid. After building more convincing hands, they had to put some of the robotic quality back in!
