Santa isn’t the only one coming this Christmas.

Synopsis

Sienna and her brother Jonathan are trying to rebuild their lives after surviving a massacre. As Christmas approaches, Art the Clown returns. The season’s lights and celebrations become the backdrop for another nightmare, threatening the fragile peace they have fought to recover.

My thoughts

I kept hearing about people fainting at screenings, so I had to see it!! Honestly, the gore was the main thing for me. The scares felt more like regular jump-scare horror, although Art’s sadistic energy was intense. Definitely a lot for horror beginners; if you have a high tolerance, you might enjoy it. I wasn’t that into all the gore… but did I still have fun? Kind of!

Cast

Lauren LaVera, David Howard Thornton, Elliott Fullam

Trivia

Damien Leone directs, writes and edits the film. His background as a special-effects artist is part of the creative DNA behind Art’s gruesome world.
