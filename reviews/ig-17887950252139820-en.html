The Cursed (Eight for Silver)

2021

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★2/5Scare3/5

Something terrible takes root on stolen land.

Synopsis

In nineteenth-century France, a Roma community is massacred during a land dispute. Nightmares, a missing child and brutal attacks follow. A pathologist arrives to investigate the violence spreading through the surrounding countryside, where the aftermath of human cruelty has taken a terrifying new form.

My thoughts

Okay, I was a bit lost with this one. It has that familiar period-horror setup: a community is persecuted, and then comes the curse. Some parts were hard to follow. If you love old-world horror, though, this might really work for you. Maybe don’t slaughter people and then act surprised when you’re cursed!

Cast

Boyd Holbrook, Kelly Reilly, Alistair Petrie

Trivia

It screened at Sundance as Eight for Silver before its US theatrical release as The Cursed. The release version also received changes to its score and visual effects.
