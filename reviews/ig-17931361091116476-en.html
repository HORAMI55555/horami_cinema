Notting Hill

1999

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5

The last person you’d expect in a little travel bookshop.

Synopsis

When Hollywood star Anna walks into William’s London travel bookshop, he hardly expects to see her again. A chance encounter soon leads to dinner with his friends. Inside, there might be room for an ordinary romance. Outside, everyone wants a piece of Anna’s private life.

My thoughts

A friend absolutely swore by this one, so I gave it a go. I loved hearing the British accents and spending time in London, and Julia Roberts is gorgeous. The romance itself felt pretty familiar, though. I wasn’t especially moved or swept off my feet.

Cast

Julia Roberts, Hugh Grant, Rhys Ifans

Trivia

Watch the market on Portobello Road. What looks like one continuous walk takes William through a whole cycle of seasons. On a rewatch, let your eyes drift to the stalls and passersby. The street tells you how much time he’s spent missing someone.
