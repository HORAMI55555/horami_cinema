Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5

Her dream college is one dance competition away.

Synopsis

Academically gifted but hopeless at dancing, Quinn decides to enter a dance competition to help secure a place at her dream college. She assembles an unlikely team to take on the school’s best dancers. Through rehearsals and setbacks, she discovers passion and romance beyond the application she originally wanted to improve.

My thoughts

So good! An American high-school movie that leaves you feeling happy and upbeat. It has musical elements too, so it’s fun to watch. And Sabrina Carpenter stars ✨ I loved it—if you like High School Musical, you’ll love this.

Cast

Sabrina Carpenter, Jordan Fisher, Liza Koshy

Trivia

Sabrina Carpenter is both the star and an executive producer, and Alicia Keys’s company also joined the production. Choreographer Aakomon Jones had worked on Pitch Perfect and Black Panther. Laura Terruso directed, with Toronto announced as the location where production would begin. Beyond its singer-star, the film brought together people with strong music and movement backgrounds, adding another reason to enjoy the team’s dance performances.
