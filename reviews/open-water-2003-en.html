Open Water

2003

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★2/5Scare1/5

The dive is over. The boat is gone.

Synopsis

On holiday, Daniel and Susan join a scuba-diving excursion. When they resurface, their boat has disappeared: a headcount error has left them stranded offshore. With no solid ground and no clear route to safety, the couple face mounting exhaustion, cold and uncertainty as they wait for someone to realise they are missing.

My thoughts

Wow, this really felt like an older film, and the image was grainy—but that actually gave it some atmosphere, haha. The story is extremely simple: a hellish stretch of time passes, and the ending leaves a seriously bitter taste. It is nice not to have to untangle a complicated plot, but I would not really recommend it unless you like ocean or shark horror. I often join sea excursions when travelling abroad, so imagining this happening to me was the part that felt scary.

Cast

Blanchard Ryan · Daniel Travis

Trivia

The sharks are real. In an interview, director Chris Kentis and producer Laura Lau described working with shark specialist Stuart Cove in the Bahamas. The actors wore protective chain mail and had their movements restricted for safety. The filmmakers also stressed the stranded characters’ viewpoint at the surface, rather than the graceful underwater view familiar from documentaries. Sudden movements and an inability to see what is below become part of the tension.
