The Swarm

2020

Scare3/5

Her family's livelihood develops a dangerous appetite.

Synopsis

A single mother raises edible locusts to support her family. With the business failing, she discovers the swarm responds to blood.

My thoughts

That was scary. Bugs plus horror plus a bit of gore! I can't stand either bugs or gore but I managed, so you probably can too. A genuinely different kind of horror, and hearing it in French added something to the atmosphere for me.

Cast

Suliane Brahim, Sofian Khammes, Marie Narbonne

Trivia

The French title is La Nuée. A single insect makes a tiny sound; a whole swarm can drown out the sounds of home. Listen for what grows louder outside the frame.
