Barbie

2023

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3.5/5

What happens when a perfect life stops fitting?

Synopsis

Barbie’s perfect routine in Barbie Land starts to go wrong. Seeking answers, she and Ken travel to the human world, which turns out to be nothing like they expected. Their colourful adventure becomes a confrontation with imposed ideals, uncomfortable contradictions and the possibility of choosing who to be.

My thoughts

A little late, but I finally watched! I expected something very fantasy-like, but they brought Barbie’s world into live action so cleverly. I can see why it became so popular. It feels fresh and is fun to watch. Seeing Los Angeles also made me personally excited! A good pick for some free time.

Cast

Margot Robbie, Ryan Gosling, America Ferrera

Trivia

The film’s world was created in part at Warner Bros. Studios Leavesden, with Greta Gerwig directing Margot Robbie and Ryan Gosling. A fun viewing angle is to treat Barbie Land’s homes as dollhouses rather than ordinary houses. Their openness and the way possessions are displayed become part of the pleasure. Once the story reaches the human world, compare the clutter, physical surroundings and distance between people. The contrast communicates through spaces as well as dialogue.
