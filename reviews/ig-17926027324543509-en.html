Pet Sematary

2019

Scare3/5

Be careful where you lay the dead to rest.

Synopsis

After moving to rural Maine, Louis discovers a children's pet cemetery in the woods. When the family cat dies, his neighbour Jud leads him to a second burial ground beyond it.

My thoughts

A somewhat fresh premise, though overall I'd say it was okay. The ending had me going, “Wait, that's where we're taking this?” It does deliver some horror.

Cast

Jason Clarke, Amy Seimetz, John Lithgow

Trivia

This 2019 adaptation takes its unusual title from Stephen King's novel. “Sematary” is the children's spelling on their handmade sign, an innocent detail at the entrance to a much less innocent place.
