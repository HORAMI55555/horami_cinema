Cocaine Godmother

2017

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3.5/5

Behind the power, everything has a price.

Synopsis

A crime drama inspired by Griselda Blanco, the drug trafficker known as the Godmother. As she gains power in the cocaine trade, ambition and family become inseparable from a world of money and violence. The film follows a life lived inside that dangerous system.

My thoughts

I watched this when it was available free on YouTube, and wow, it’s intense. Catherine Zeta-Jones gives such a commanding performance. Knowing it’s based on real events made it feel like a glimpse into a world completely different from mine... Sadness, madness, so many emotions mixed together. It left me thinking about how desperately people try to survive.

Cast

Catherine Zeta-Jones

Trivia

Although the story moves through New York, Miami and Colombia, over 90% was filmed in Vancouver. Director Guillermo Navarro explained that location choices and production design created the illusion of being elsewhere.
