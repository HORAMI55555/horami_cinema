A romantic escape turns into a fight to escape.

Synopsis

A rapper from Nalasopara and an influencer from South Bombay set off on a romantic getaway. Their different backgrounds become the least of their problems when a killer crocodile traps them in a fight for survival. Romance gives way to immediate danger, testing a relationship that was already bridging very different worlds.

My thoughts

Please watch this! What stayed with me wasn't just the horror or the filmmaking—it was how strongly it transported me to India. The long romantic setup and the sudden rap or ballad moments, no matter what was happening, had me reacting out loud the whole time. What even is this film?! I really liked how it wrapped up. Such an oddball within the horror genre.

Cast

Adarsh Gourav · Shanaya Kapoor · Amrutha Srinvasan

Trivia

Netflix categorizes the film as both a thriller and a romantic Bollywood movie. Viewing tip: treat the relationship setup as part of the experience. Its shift from romance into survival is a defining part of its identity.
