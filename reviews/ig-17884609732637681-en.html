Lucy

2014

Scare0/5

They made her a courier. They can't control what she's carrying.

Synopsis

A gang in Taipei forces Lucy to carry a drug inside her body. When the packet leaks, her senses and memory change, followed by her ability to influence the world around her. She seeks a scientist who might understand what's happening.

My thoughts

This isn't horror, but it's so entertaining. Give it a watch! Basically, it's about someone who suddenly develops superpowers.

Cast

Scarlett Johansson, Morgan Freeman, Choi Min-sik

Trivia

Luc Besson cuts to numbers marking Lucy's changing abilities. Instead of counting down an action hero's dwindling resources, the film counts up, as her reactions grow less and less recognizably human.
