Dumplin’

2018

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5

Who says beauty has to follow their rules?

Synopsis

Teenager Willowdean, whose mother is a former beauty queen, struggles with judgments about her body and tensions at home. She enters the pageant her mother runs as an act of protest. As others join her, music and friendship help turn that rebellion into a step toward defining her own worth.

My thoughts

Like Work It!, which I posted about just before this, it’s another American high-school movie where an insecure main character suddenly starts doing something completely unexpected! This one mainly focuses on appearance and feels similar to other Netflix movies of this kind. There’s a little Disney Channel vibe. Having Dove Cameron in it was amazing ✨ My only negatives were that the main character was so gloomy at first that it irritated me a little, and the ending also left me going, “Hmm…” (*'へ'*)

Cast

Danielle Macdonald, Jennifer Aniston, Dove Cameron

Trivia

The Dolly Parton music is more than a playlist of old hits. Parton and Linda Perry co-wrote six new songs, while familiar songs received new recordings with guest performers. Alongside singers including Sia and Miranda Lambert, cast members Danielle Macdonald and Jennifer Aniston also contributed vocals. Adapted from Julie Murphy’s novel, the film extends its heroine’s musical inspiration into a soundtrack that even brings its actors into the singing.
