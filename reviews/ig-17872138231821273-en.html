アメリカンホラーストーリー〜精神科病棟〜

2012

Scare1/5

She arrives to report a story. Inside, no one takes her word for it.

Synopsis

In 1964, reporter Lana investigates Briarcliff, the institution holding suspected killer Kit Walker. Inside Sister Jude's tightly controlled wards, the people entrusted with patients' care have their own secrets to protect.

My thoughts

The story felt fresh and kept me interested. It didn't feel like horror to me. I can see the right person getting completely hooked.

Cast

Jessica Lange, Sarah Paulson, Evan Peters

Trivia

American Horror Story reassigns familiar actors across its anthology seasons. Here, performers returning from the first season face one another as a nun, a reporter and a suspect, with an entirely different balance of power.
