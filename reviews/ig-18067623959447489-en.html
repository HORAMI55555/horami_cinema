Sinkhole

2021

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★2/5

A dream home. A terrifying drop.

Synopsis

After years of saving, Dong-won finally buys a home in Seoul. Following a housewarming celebration, a massive sinkhole swallows the entire building. Trapped underground, residents and guests have to work together as danger closes in. A dream of a fresh start turns into a desperate escape in this Korean disaster comedy.

My thoughts

The home collapsing had me going, what is happening?! But the story actually drew me in. Such a strange, entertaining setup—it felt like something different. A good pick when you want a fun watch, haha.

Cast

Cha Seung-won · Kim Sung-kyun · Lee Kwang-soo · Kim Hye-jun

Trivia

Directed by Kim Ji-hoon, the film screened in the Piazza Grande program at the 2021 Locarno Film Festival, according to the Korean Film Council. Its disaster spectacle is paired with humor rooted in the characters.
