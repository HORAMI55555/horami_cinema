Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★2/5

One city, one rainstorm, two very different weekends.

Synopsis

Gatsby accompanies girlfriend Ashleigh to Manhattan for a director interview. Their romantic weekend goes off schedule, sending each of them through the city with someone else.

My thoughts

I watched because people recommend it for a rainy day. I thought the woman was awful and the guy was pretty awful too. Then it just ended and I wasn't sure what to make of it. Was it deep?

Cast

Timothée Chalamet, Elle Fanning, Selena Gomez

Trivia

Splitting the day between Ashleigh's interview and Gatsby's wanderings gives the city two faces: a glamorous movie world for one, a place full of private memories for the other.
