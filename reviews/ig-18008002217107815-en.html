Fear Street Trilogy

2021

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5

1994. 1978. 1666. Follow the terror back to its beginning.

Synopsis

In 1994, a group of teenagers discovers that their town’s recurring atrocities may be linked by one curse. Their fight to survive leads back to a summer camp in 1978, then to 1666. This horror trilogy traces the origins of terror passed down through generations.

My thoughts

It’s been ages since I watched a horror movie this fun!!! Fantastic. It’s a trilogy that goes further back in time with each part, and the last one reaches the witch-hunt era where the curse began, which makes it so much fun. Religion × horror really is such a good combination. Ahh, this was great—I had a blast. Definitely recommend it. I feel like people who like Insidious would enjoy it.

Cast

Kiana Madeira, Olivia Scott Welch, Benjamin Flores Jr.

Trivia

Inspired by R. L. Stine’s book series, Leigh Janiak’s trilogy was released one week apart in July 2021. In a Netflix interview, the director described thinking of it as a hybrid of film and television. Moving backward from 1994 to 1978 and 1666 means later installments reshape the opening events. Viewing tip: notice how the style of horror changes across periods, and watch for actors returning in different roles.
