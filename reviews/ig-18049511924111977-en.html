Synopsis

Lost in a forest in western Ireland, Mina finds shelter with three strangers. But their refuge comes with a disturbing arrangement: mysterious beings watch them every night. Unable to find a way out of the woods, they must uncover what is keeping them there.

My thoughts

Ohhhh! This was so much fun!!! Definitely recommend ‼️ A forest escape game mixed with monster horror, but with a proper story and a final reversal that had me going, seriously?! Entertaining all the way through, never boring. I didn’t find it extremely scary, so I think people who aren’t great with horror could manage it with a few nervous moments. Really enjoyed this!!!

Cast

Dakota Fanning · Georgina Campbell · Olwen Fouéré · Oliver Finnegan

Trivia

M. Night Shyamalan produced the film, while his daughter Ishana Night Shyamalan wrote and directed it. Based on A. M. Shine’s novel, it brings father and daughter together in different filmmaking roles.
