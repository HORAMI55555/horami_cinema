Have a Song on Your Lips

2015

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5

Some feelings are easier to sing than to say.

Synopsis

Yuri Kashiwagi returns to the Goto Islands as a substitute music teacher and reluctantly takes charge of a school choir. She keeps her distance from both the students and the piano, while the teenagers hide troubles behind their everyday smiles. Preparing for a competition gives them a way to connect through voices when ordinary conversation feels impossible.

My thoughts

I saw this at the cinema when I was around middle-school age! I remember crying so much. It had that quintessential coming-of-age feeling, and I remember the story being really lovely.

Cast

Yui Aragaki · Fumino Kimura · Kenta Kiritani · Yuri Tsunematsu · Shota Shimoda

Trivia

The film adapts Eiichi Nakata's novel of the same name. Its distributor lists the English title as Have a Song on Your Lips, a useful title to use when looking for information in English.
