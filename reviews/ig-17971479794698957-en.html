Vivarium

2019

☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★ 3/5 · RecommendedScare 1/5

Their dream home has no way out.

Synopsis

A couple looking for a home is shown around an estate of identical houses. When they try to leave, every route leads them back to the same address. With no neighbors in sight, a baby is delivered to their doorstep. The promise of domestic life becomes a surreal form of confinement.

My thoughts

I’d been wanting to see this for ages, and finally did! Such an interesting setup: a couple goes house hunting, finds a nice-looking development, and then can’t get out. They’re stuck living there with nobody else around, until a random baby arrives for them to raise. The story is simple enough to enjoy without overthinking. The ending felt bitterly ironic and sad. To me, it seemed like an exaggerated metaphor for how hard motherhood can be.

Cast

Imogen Poots · Jesse Eisenberg · Jonathan Aris

Trivia

Lorcan Finnegan cited a David Attenborough documentary about cuckoos laying eggs in other birds’ nests, as well as Magritte’s paintings, among his inspirations. The unsettling suburb draws on both natural behavior and visual art.
