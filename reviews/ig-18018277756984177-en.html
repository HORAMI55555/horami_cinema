How to Lose a Guy in 10 Days

2003

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5

She needs him to leave. He needs her to fall in love.

Synopsis

For an article, journalist Andie plans to demonstrate the mistakes that make a man end a relationship within ten days. Advertising executive Ben has made the opposite wager: he can make a woman fall in love with him in the same time. Unaware of each other’s motives, they turn dating into an escalating battle of competing agendas.

My thoughts

I watched because it is known as a classic rom-com! Such a nice atmosphere from that era. The story is fairly straightforward romance, but the heroine is cute and it puts you in a brighter mood. Simply a good recommendation!

Cast

Kate Hudson, Matthew McConaughey, Kathryn Hahn

Trivia

Kate Hudson and Matthew McConaughey play characters with opposite professional goals but the same ten-day deadline. The official synopsis places that battle of wills at the centre. Because viewers know both plans, an awkward gesture becomes funny for more than one reason: we can see how it affects the other person’s strategy. Watch the shift between performing a role on a date and briefly dropping the act. Those small glimpses of sincerity make the changing relationship easier to follow.
