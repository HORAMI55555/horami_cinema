Burlesque

2010

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5

One voice. A chance to step into the spotlight.

Synopsis

Aspiring singer Ali arrives in Los Angeles and is drawn to Burlesque, a glamorous club run by Tess. After finding work there, she fights for an opportunity to perform while the venue faces financial trouble. Her voice becomes the means to pursue a new place in the world and a future onstage.

My thoughts

I found this on a top-ten list of uplifting films, and yes, absolutely! The music, the world and the heroine’s determination to work her way up are so good. It gave me a feeling similar to The Devil Wears Prada or The Intern. What a life to dream about!

Cast

Christina Aguilera, Cher, Stanley Tucci, Cam Gigandet

Trivia

Christina Aguilera plays Ali opposite Cher as Tess, making the meeting of two singers central to the club story presented by Sony. Watch Ali’s changing relationship to the stage: first something she looks at with longing, then a place where she can express herself. The same lights and costumes take on a different meaning depending on which side of the performance she occupies. The preparation and relationships surrounding the big numbers also reveal the work that makes the glamour possible.
