Mask Girl

2023

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3.5/5

Behind the mask, a life changes forever.

Synopsis

Office worker Kim Mo-mi feels insecure about her appearance but receives adoration while streaming in a mask at night. Her double life begins as a search for attention, then an unexpected incident transforms it. Other people’s desires and obsessions feed a tragedy stretching across decades.

My thoughts

Oooh, so much packed into just eight episodes! The main character changes each time and you gradually piece everything together. It turns into a prison story partway through, which was amazing. It wasn’t really horror, but it had plastic surgery, darkness, murder, lies, and finally love between parent and child. Very Korean. I like it!

Cast

Lee Han-byeol, Nana, Ko Hyun-jung, Ahn Jae-hong

Trivia

Lee Han-byeol, Nana and Ko Hyun-jung portray Kim Mo-mi at different stages of her life. Adapted from the webtoon of the same name, it is writer-director Kim Yong-hoon’s series debut. Viewing tip: notice the desires and wounds that persist as her face changes. The tension between seeing different people and recognizing one continuous life offers a way to think about judgments based on appearance.
