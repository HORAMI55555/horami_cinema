Her family’s interpreter finds a voice of her own.

Synopsis

Ruby is the only hearing member of her Deaf family. Between school and helping with the family fishing business, she regularly acts as their interpreter. Joining the choir opens up a love of singing—and a possible future in music. Following that possibility means confronting how much her family depends on her.

My thoughts

This was so, so good 😭 Being the only hearing person in your family comes with so many difficulties I hadn’t really thought about. Ruby’s struggles, her teacher’s care,, and that ending. I really recommend this for a quiet evening when you want something moving.

Cast

Emilia Jones, Marlee Matlin, Troy Kotsur, Daniel Durant

Trivia

Troy Kotsur won the supporting-actor Oscar for playing Ruby’s father, becoming the first Deaf male actor to win an Academy Award. CODA also won Best Picture.
