Never Have I Ever

2020

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5

First love and grief don't fit a class schedule.

Synopsis

After losing her father, teen Devi resolves to reinvent herself. Romance and friendship keep her busy, but grief follows her home, where her mother is struggling too.

My thoughts

So good! At first it seemed childish, and even the lead's name made me roll my eyes, but it went deeper and really got to me more than once. Their lives felt close to ours. That ending was so romantic I screamed. Half-hour episodes are perfect for a little every day.

Cast

Maitreyi Ramakrishnan, Poorna Jagannathan, Ramona Young

Trivia

Tennis player John McEnroe narrates Devi's life. A famously fiery competitor makes an inspired commentator on the split second before she loses her temper.
