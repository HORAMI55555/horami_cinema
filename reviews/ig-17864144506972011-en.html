Friday the 13th

2009

Scare3.5/5

Different reasons to visit the lake. One way back through the woods.

Synopsis

Looking for his missing sister, Clay crosses paths with a group staying near Crystal Lake. His search leads them into the path of a pursuer who knows the woods around the lake far better than any visitor.

My thoughts

It's one of those famous horror franchises that's been around for ages. I didn't find this very scary, though I didn't enjoy the bits of gore.

Cast

Jared Padalecki, Danielle Panabaker, Derek Mears

Trivia

This is the 2009 retelling, with Derek Mears behind Jason's mask. The way he moves through the grounds makes the lakeside feel like territory he patrols, rather than a place he happens to haunt.
