You're Beautiful

2009

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5

Her first job in a band? Stand in for her twin brother.

Synopsis

Training to become a nun, Go Mi-nyeo steps into her twin brother’s place in the idol band A.N.JELL. Sharing a home with her bandmates means staying in character long after the show ends.

My thoughts

I had such a drama hangover after this that I kept looking up Jang Keun-suk! She joins the group disguised as her twin brother, then one of the members catches on. What a great series.

Cast

Park Shin-hye, Jang Keun-suk, Lee Hong-gi, Jung Yong-hwa

Trivia

The fictional band spells its name A.N.JELL. Say it aloud and it sounds like “angel,” a nice echo of its stand-in singer’s convent background. During the group scenes, keep an eye on who looks at whom as the secret starts to slip.
