Look Both Ways

2022

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★5/5

Two possible futures, two ways to find yourself.

Synopsis

On the eve of graduation, Natalie takes a pregnancy test and the story splits into two lives: raising a child in Texas, or pursuing animation in Los Angeles. Through love, work and days that refuse to go to plan, both versions of Natalie search for happiness on their own terms.

My thoughts

This was soooo good. You really should watch it when you’re unsure about your path or wondering what to do with your life. Sometimes you think choosing wrong will ruin everything, or one failure will mean your life is over. This movie says that either way, you can still end up with a happy life. It’s genuinely so good. It was exactly what I needed right now and eased some of the pressure I feel about the future.

Cast

Lili Reinhart, Danny Ramirez, David Corenswet

Trivia

Director Wanuri Kahiu connected the split-future premise with her own experience. She recalled holding a pregnancy test and realizing that choosing to have a child would take her life far from what she had imagined. Lili Reinhart likewise saw the many possible routes through life—and the absence of one correct route to a destination—as central to the film. Their comments reflect its interest in the possibilities within different lives rather than ranking those lives as wins or losses.
