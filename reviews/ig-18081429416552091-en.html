Her nightmares are happening to someone else.

Synopsis

Madison begins experiencing horrifying visions that place her inside scenes of murder. As she discovers connections to real crimes, she has to ask why she is seeing them. Following the mystery of the killer draws her toward her own past, where an unexpected source of terror awaits.

My thoughts

This was fun!! There’s a properly developed story here. It gives you that roller-coaster feeling of a full-on horror film, like Insidious. So many moments had me going, THAT’S what’s happening?! Definitely recommend it, though it might be too much if horror isn’t your thing!

Cast

Annabelle Wallis · Maddie Hasson · George Young · Michole Briana White

Trivia

James Wan directs a screenplay by Akela Cooper, from a story credited to Wan, Ingrid Bisu and Cooper. It is a standalone original film rather than a sequel in an existing horror series.
