Synopsis

A grown Alice follows a white rabbit underground. The strange inhabitants insist she's the very Alice they've been waiting for.

My thoughts

One of my absolute favorites. You have to see it for this world alone. If you already love Alice, it'll hit even harder. Thank you, Disney.

Cast

Mia Wasikowska, Helena Bonham Carter, Johnny Depp

Trivia

Tim Burton's Alice has visited this land as a child. The creatures are staging a reunion while Alice believes she's meeting strangers, and that mismatch drives the story.
