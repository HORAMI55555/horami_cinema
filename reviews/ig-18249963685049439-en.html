Synopsis

First-class passenger Rose meets Jack, an artist traveling in steerage. As the distance between them shrinks, the liner continues across the Atlantic.

My thoughts

I realized I'd never posted Titanic and rushed to fix it. An absolute masterpiece. Surely everyone following this account has seen it?! Such a beautiful love story, and Rose and Jack are both so gorgeous. I love it.

Cast

Kate Winslet, Leonardo DiCaprio, Kathy Bates

Trivia

The lovers are fictional, but several fellow passengers were real. Molly Brown, who helps Jack dress for dinner, is based on Margaret Brown, a survivor of the sinking.
