Antebellum

2020

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★2.5/5Scare1.5/5

A nightmare that blurs past and present.

Synopsis

Successful writer Veronica’s life is set against images of a plantation ruled by the brutality of slavery. Seemingly separate worlds intersect as she confronts a terrifying reality. To find a way out, she must understand what is happening to her. The film builds its mystery around the disturbing relationship between past and present.

My thoughts

This was such a shocking watch... The scenes of slavery felt painfully real. Seeing the two strands come together and finally understanding what was happening was striking. Interesting, though some of the heartbreaking scenes really stayed with me.

Cast

Janelle Monáe · Jena Malone · Jack Huston · Gabourey Sidibe

Trivia

This is the first feature written and directed by Gerard Bush and Christopher Renz. Lionsgate’s announcement highlights their background in advertising work concerned with social justice.
