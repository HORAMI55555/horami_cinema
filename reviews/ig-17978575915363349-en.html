The Witch

2015

Scare2/5

Exiled to the edge of a forest, a family turns inward.

Synopsis

A family expelled from a colonial New England settlement farms at the forest's edge. When a baby vanishes, suspicion begins to settle on the eldest daughter.

My thoughts

The whole world reminded me of Midsommar: strange from start to finish. I didn't quite get the ending, but it was easier to follow than Midsommar. Apparently it draws on stories from when people really believed in witches. If that's your thing you'll love it. I had a pretty good time!

Cast

Anya Taylor-Joy, Ralph Ineson, Kate Dickie

Trivia

Robert Eggers drew on period accounts and folklore for the dialogue. Even the unfamiliar rhythms of the family's English help place the audience at a distance from our own time.
