Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★2.5/5

Synopsis

Londoner Bridget decides to turn over a new leaf and starts keeping a diary. Her plans soon become complicated by her charming boss Daniel and the initially unappealing Mark Darcy. Between work mishaps and romantic confusion, she tries to figure out what happiness might look like on her own terms.

My thoughts

I gave this famous older rom-com a watch. I especially enjoyed getting a feel for Britain back then!

Cast

Renée Zellweger · Hugh Grant · Colin Firth

Trivia

Renée Zellweger prepared by working incognito at London publisher Picador. Using another name, she answered phones, made copies and served coffee. Bridget’s publishing-office routine was something she had experienced herself before filming.
