Single’s Inferno

2021

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5

When do you get to ask your crush what they do?

Synopsis

Singles share an island in South Korea, fetching water, making meals and trying to get close to the person who’s caught their eye. Only those who choose each other can leave for a night together at a luxury hotel.

My thoughts

This felt like a Korean cousin to Terrace House or Who Is the Wolf? So many gorgeous people, a setup that’s genuinely fun, and it’s short enough to fly through. Ji-a, the one in the photo from my original post, is exactly my type.

Cast

Song Ji-a, Shin Ji-yeon, Oh Jin-taek (Season 1)

Trivia

Ages and occupations stay secret on Inferno. A mutual match unlocks Paradise and the chance to ask those simple first-date questions. Watch what happens when Ji-a reveals her job: a detail that might fill an awkward first minute elsewhere becomes a moment of real suspense.
