Wonder

2017

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4.5/5

One first day of school. A growing circle of kindness.

Synopsis

Ten-year-old Auggie, born with facial differences, leaves homeschooling behind to attend school for the first time. Stares and hurtful remarks make the transition difficult, but friendships gradually form. His journey also brings the private struggles of his sister, parents, and classmates into view.

My thoughts

This is such a famous classic! I finally got to watch it. I really understood how hard life is for the main character, and seeing his family’s struggles and pain made my heart ache. But I was so glad that, by the end, life seemed easier for everyone and they looked happy. Whatever situation you’re in, a good future will surely come, so let’s keep going!!!

Cast

Jacob Tremblay, Julia Roberts, Owen Wilson

Trivia

Makeup played an important part in bringing Auggie to the screen. Artist Arjen Tuiten received an Academy Award nomination for his work. The Academy later featured Wonder at a family event, where Tuiten and director Stephen Chbosky used clips and behind-the-scenes images to discuss how makeup contributes to a movie’s emotional effect. The craft is more than an appearance transformation here; it helps communicate the distance between people, and the process of overcoming it.
