Control Z

2020

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5

Is there an undo key for a secret sent to the whole school?

Synopsis

At a Mexican high school, an anonymous hacker starts outing students’ secrets. Sofía usually watches her classmates from the sidelines, which means she notices things they miss. To stop the next disclosure, she starts checking their stories against one another.

My thoughts

This is more of a high-school mystery, with just a tiny touch of horror. I had so many “wait, THAT’S what happened?!” moments. I loved getting a feel for school life in another country, and so many of the actresses are gorgeous. Perfect if you like piecing clues together.

Cast

Ana Valeria Becerril, Michael Ronda, Yankel Stevan

Trivia

Ctrl+Z is the keyboard shortcut for Undo. Once the secret is on a screen, nobody can undo what their classmates have seen. On a rewatch, try watching the faces around the room during a reveal. That gap between what a computer can erase and what a person remembers gives the title its sting.
