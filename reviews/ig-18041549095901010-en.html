Don’t Move

2024

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★2.5/5Scare2/5

Her body is shutting down. The killer is closing in.

Synopsis

Seeking solitude in the forest, a grieving woman named Iris encounters a stranger who injects her with a paralysing drug. As control of her body slips away, every chance to escape becomes harder to reach. With a killer pursuing her, survival depends on the smallest movements she can still make.

My thoughts

It had just arrived on Netflix and looked interesting, so I watched immediately! Really entertaining and over before I knew it. Simple premise, exactly what the title suggests, but tense throughout. It is a psycho-kidnapping thriller rather than a ghost story, so I would recommend it to a broad range of viewers!

Cast

Kelsey Asbille, Finn Wittrock

Trivia

The directors aimed for a thriller unfolding close to real time, so the audience experiences danger alongside Iris rather than skipping past it. Kelsey Asbille also had to communicate emotion with very little body movement. In Netflix’s interview, she discusses working with the directors on the stages of paralysis and what would register on screen. Watch her eyes and breathing: tiny physical changes take on the significance that a much larger action might have in another thriller.
