Psycho

1960

Scare2/5

There's a vacancy at the Bates Motel.

Synopsis

Marion leaves town with stolen money and stops at a roadside motel in the rain. Its young proprietor seems kind, but the house behind him holds another presence.

My thoughts

I love really old horror, and this black-and-white film keeps topping horror lists, so I watched it. Honestly, what was supposed to scare me?! It barely felt like horror. I did get a little of that special black-and-white eeriness, but otherwise it did nothing for me.

Cast

Anthony Perkins, Janet Leigh, Vera Miles

Trivia

Bernard Herrmann scored the film for strings alone. Listen beyond the famous high notes: the insistent rhythms accompanying Marion's drive come from the same family of instruments.
