47 Meters Down

2017

Scare1/5

Sharks overhead. A dwindling air supply.

Synopsis

Two sisters go cage diving in Mexico. When the cable snaps, the cage drops to the seabed. Even getting a message to the boat means swimming far enough for the radio to reach it.

My thoughts

I wouldn’t call this horror, but the setup felt fresh and I enjoyed it. Worth a watch. I couldn’t see where the ending was going, and the sea feels a little scarier now.

Cast

Mandy Moore, Claire Holt

Trivia

The title measures the vertical distance between the sisters and ordinary life. Even with no shark in frame, a glance at a pressure gauge or a radio cutting out keeps time. Which feels worse: leaving the cage or staying put?
