The Internship

2013

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5

Two tech novices reboot their lives at Google.

Synopsis

Middle-aged salesmen Billy and Nick lose their jobs and gamble on a fresh start in Google’s internship program. Surrounded by technically gifted students, the unlikely recruits tackle team challenges. Their people skills and life experience become their tools in the contest for a handful of permanent jobs.

My thoughts

After watching, you’ll want to work at Google, lol. So good. If you like The Devil Wears Prada or The Intern, you’ll love this too. Movies about people working hard like this are great.

Cast

Vince Vaughn, Owen Wilson, Rose Byrne

Trivia

Vince Vaughn contributed beyond his starring role: he co-wrote the screenplay with Jared Stern and also produced the movie. Director Shawn Levy was a producer as well, so both lead actor and director helped shape the project behind the scenes. Watching Vaughn trade lines with Owen Wilson highlights the comedy’s interest in a skill that does not depend on technical expertise: getting people talking, working together, and becoming a team.
