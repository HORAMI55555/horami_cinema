Spoiled Brats

2021

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5

Dad’s fortune is gone—or so they think.

Synopsis

Three siblings have lived extravagantly on their father’s fortune. Fed up, he pretends to have lost everything and upends their lives. Forced to work for their money, the children encounter unfamiliar realities and begin reconsidering their relationships with their family.

My thoughts

So funny! It’s short and moves along simply, which makes it easy to watch!! A father finally gets fed up with his three ridiculously rich, good-for-nothing kids and makes them live without a penny. The scenes where they face the harshness of society really hit home—so that’s what life will be like for me soon. This is really entertaining, so definitely give it a watch!

Cast

Gérard Jugnot, Camille Lou, Artus

Trivia

The official Netflix synopsis makes the central trick clear: the father only pretends to be ruined to teach his children a lesson. This French family comedy stars Gérard Jugnot and others. Viewing tip: notice the humor created by knowing the father’s secret. What feels like a catastrophe to his children may look like successful parenting to him. The same scene can become funny or poignant depending on whose perspective you take.
