The Menu

2022

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5Scare1/5

Tonight’s tasting menu comes with a sinister surprise.

Synopsis

A young couple travels to an exclusive island restaurant for a celebrated chef’s special tasting menu. As each course arrives, the evening takes on a more disturbing meaning and the diners are drawn into a sinister experience. A sharp thriller in which culinary aspiration turns into dread.

My thoughts

So much fun!!! Another refreshingly different horror movie! Rich people visit a super-famous luxury restaurant on a remote island, and then you start thinking, “Something’s not right here!” The main character is amazing at the end—I would have given up. It’s really entertaining and the story is simple, so I highly recommend it.

Cast

Ralph Fiennes, Anya Taylor-Joy, Nicholas Hoult, Hong Chau

Trivia

Mark Mylod directs, Seth Reiss and Will Tracy wrote the screenplay, and Adam McKay is among the producers. The official site features material about both the horror and the food’s authenticity. Viewing tip: look beyond the beautiful plates to how guests discuss them and how the staff react. The gap between enjoying food and displaying knowledge or status sharpens the comedy and menace within the immaculate service. The language used to describe a dish matters too.
