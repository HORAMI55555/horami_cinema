The Legend of the Blue Sea

2016

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5

On land, a mermaid falls for a man who lies for a living.

Synopsis

A mermaid meets Korean con artist Joon-jae in Spain and follows him to Seoul. Learning to live among humans is bewildering enough, but their meeting also echoes a story that began on a Joseon-era shore.

My thoughts

Whoever came up with this was a genius. A mermaid comes ashore, meets Lee Min-ho and falls for him, but there’s only so long she can keep living like a human. What really got me is that she and his past self met and fell in love too. How lovely is that? Please watch it!

Cast

Jun Ji-hyun, Lee Min-ho

Trivia

In modern Seoul, she can barely read human customs while he makes a living reading people. The Joseon storyline lets you see familiar gestures again in a different light. Try watching who notices the other first each time their paths cross.
