The Blue Drum

2022

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★1/5

A funeral brings buried secrets to the surface.

Synopsis

On the day of her father’s funeral, Leticia is haunted by memories of the mother who left her as a child. A supernatural presence draws her toward secrets buried in the family’s past. As her understanding begins to unravel, grief gives way to something more disturbing in this short horror film.

My thoughts

A really short horror film, under half an hour, but the ending left me thinking, what was that even supposed to mean? It didn’t work for me at all. I was surprised it made it onto Netflix—personally, this felt like a waste of time.

Cast

Crystal Hernandez · Jonathan Medina · Judith Scarpone

Trivia

The film was selected from hundreds of projects for the inaugural NALIP Women of Color Incubator, supported by Netflix. Only four filmmakers participated, receiving guidance from industry professionals throughout preparation, production and post-production.
