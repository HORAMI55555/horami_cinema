2013

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5

Same uniform, different inheritances.

Synopsis

Eun-sang meets Tan in California and finds him again at an elite Korean school, where family fortunes decide who belongs.

My thoughts

I'm posting K-dramas too! First up in my Lee Min-ho obsession is The Heirs. A girl with no money gets into a school full of CEOs' kids and crosses paths with one of its big names in both America and Korea. They fall for each other, he's ridiculously handsome, and the story is so much fun. Highly recommend!

Cast

Lee Min-ho, Park Shin-hye, Kim Woo-bin

Trivia

The series is also known as The Inheritors. Watch the parents alongside the students and the title raises a sharper question about what exactly their children inherit.
