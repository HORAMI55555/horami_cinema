This Child Is Evil

2022

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★2/5

Her mother has returned. Something has not.

Synopsis

After a car accident leaves her family deeply affected, Hana finds a connection with Jun, a boy worried about his own mother. Then Hana’s mother wakes from a long coma and returns home. Her father celebrates a miracle, but Hana cannot shake the feeling that something about this reunion is wrong.

My thoughts

My first Japanese horror in quite a while! I am not even sure how I would classify it—it was not scary for me, although I suppose it is horror. I wanted a little more from it. It also left me with an unresolved, uncomfortable feeling. I cannot quite decide who is really at fault.

Cast

Sara Minami, Ryusei Onishi, Yuki Sakurai, Hiroshi Tamaki

Trivia

The project originated as a runner-up in TSUTAYA CREATORS’ PROGRAM FILM 2017, an original-film project competition. Sho Kataoka wrote and directed it, and the distributor records a special mention in the fantasy section of the Porto International Film Festival. A useful viewing angle is the family reunion itself: an apparently joyful event feels frightening to one person. Consider how the same words or gestures change depending on whose perspective you take, turning domestic reassurance into unease.
