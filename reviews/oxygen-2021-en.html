Oxygen

2021

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5Scare1/5

No memory. Less oxygen with every breath.

Synopsis

A woman wakes inside an unfamiliar cryogenic pod with no memory of her identity or how she arrived there. Unable to find a way out, she watches her oxygen supply dwindle. Piecing together fragments of memory and the few clues available, she must understand her situation before the air runs out.

My thoughts

This combines the breathless, nerve-racking tension of Buried with a world that reminded me of Interstellar, and it was such a rewarding watch! Almost until the end, my mind kept racing: what situation is she actually in, and what is happening? I could not quite work it out. When things clicked, the music and the scenery were breathtaking. There is not that much horror here, so I really recommend it. A definite yes from me.

Cast

Mélanie Laurent · Mathieu Amalric (voice) · Malik Zidi

Trivia

How was that claustrophobic space created? Netflix France’s official making-of feature explores how director Alexandre Aja and production designer Jean Rabasse built a cinematic world from a box of roughly three square metres. Mélanie Laurent had to sustain a largely solo performance inside it. Expressions, breathing and interactions with the equipment keep the confined setting changing. On a rewatch, watching how the filmmakers make that tiny space visually engaging adds another way to appreciate the film.
