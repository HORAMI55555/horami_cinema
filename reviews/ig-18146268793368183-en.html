Holidate

2020

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5

A date for every holiday. No feelings allowed.

Synopsis

Tired of the pressure to have a partner at every celebration, Sloane and Jackson agree to become each other’s holiday plus-ones. The arrangement is supposed to stay casual and free of romantic expectations. But as one festive occasion follows another, keeping feelings out of the deal becomes less straightforward.

My thoughts

Christmas season was already over when I watched it, but this would be such a good film to put on at Christmas!

Cast

Emma Roberts · Luke Bracey · Kristin Chenoweth

Trivia

Producers McG and Mary Viola also produced I Feel Pretty and The Babysitter. The team behind this holiday-dating arrangement has worked across both romantic comedy and horror comedy—an unexpected connection between very different movie nights.
