To All the Boys I've Loved Before

2018

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5

The letters she never meant to send change everything.

Synopsis

High schooler Lara Jean prefers imaginary romance, keeping letters to her crushes safely hidden away. When all five unexpectedly reach their recipients, her private feelings become public. A fake-dating arrangement with popular Peter seems like a solution, until real emotions complicate the plan.

My thoughts

I watched this because it stars the same actress as Boo, Bitch!! It was in three parts, but I finished them so quickly. I’ve been watching an insane number of Netflix romance series lately, but this was surprisingly romantic and I liked it. I want to ask anyone who’s seen it: doesn’t the male lead totally have the vibe of a supporting guy who gets dumped at the end? lol

Cast

Lana Condor, Noah Centineo, Anna Cathcart

Trivia

The late scene on the lacrosse field was filmed just before sunset to achieve a warm, hopeful image. Cinematographer Michael Fimognari recalled the contrast between the hectic setup and Lana and Noah’s quiet connection within the frame. Before anyone knew whether the movie would find an audience, their chemistry felt like a secret the crew had discovered. Fimognari went on to direct both sequels, carrying that visual connection into the rest of the trilogy.
