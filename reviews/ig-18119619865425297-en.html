A countryside escape turns into a fight to survive.

Synopsis

Four women head to rural Chile for a break, only to be attacked by a father and son. Seeking help offers no easy way out as they struggle to survive an escalating ordeal. The attackers’ history casts a shadow over the present in this brutal survival horror.

My thoughts

There are a few seriously extreme scenes, but the story felt similar to other captivity horrors I’ve seen. A lot of the content is really nasty, so I wouldn’t recommend this one. Some of those images could easily come back to you when you’re trying to sleep.

Cast

Catalina Martin · Macarena Carrere · Daniel Antivilo

Trivia

Lucio A. Rojas said the small budget did not force him to drop the effects sequences in his script. Extensive preparation linked cinematography, prosthetics and VFX, combining practical work with digital compositing. Those extreme images were carefully planned long before the cameras rolled.
