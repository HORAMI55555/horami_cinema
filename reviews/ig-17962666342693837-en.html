Escape Room

2019

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5Scare1/5

What if the floor were the first thing you lost in an escape room?

Synopsis

Six strangers accept an invitation to an escape room with a cash prize. A mechanism clicks in the waiting room, the temperature starts to rise, and suddenly the game is already underway. To find a way out, they need details only their fellow players remember.

My thoughts

This was pretty fun! If you liked Squid Game, I think you’ll really like this. The premise is exactly what the title promises, but I had a better time with it than with most escape-game movies. I’d recommend it.

Cast

Taylor Russell, Logan Miller, Deborah Ann Woll

Trivia

The upside-down billiard room was designed as a 1950s Palm Springs bar. The furniture sits overhead while the players stand on what ought to be the ceiling. Imagine turning the shot over: beneath the puzzle there’s a perfectly inviting place to have a drink.
