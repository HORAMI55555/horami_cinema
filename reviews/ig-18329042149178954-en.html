Synopsis

Bookish and outwardly considerate, Joe studies the lives of the people he becomes infatuated with, determined to make himself their ideal partner. In his own narration, surveillance and deception become acts of devotion. But the closer he gets, the more his romantic story reveals a dangerous need for control.

My thoughts

So entertaining! I watched through season four without getting bored. The setting changes regularly and there is such a mix of romance, mystery and psychological suspense. A great choice if you enjoy obsessive characters or mysteries. I am a Love fan, by the way. At the time of this review, I could not wait for the final season!

Cast

Penn Badgley, Victoria Pedretti, Elizabeth Lail

Trivia

Caroline Kepnes’s novels are the source, and her interviews highlight Joe’s obsessive internal monologue as a defining feature. On screen, Penn Badgley’s narration brings us uncomfortably close to Joe’s explanations. A revealing exercise is to separate what the voice claims from what his actions show. The same scene can change completely when its persuasive commentary is set aside. That gap helps explain why romantic-drama imagery and menace can occupy the frame at the same time.
