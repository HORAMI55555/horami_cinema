GRETEL & HANSEL

2020

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5Scare3/5

Deep in the woods, someone has set a perfect table.

Synopsis

Hungry and far from home, Gretel and her little brother follow the smell of food to an old woman's house. The table is full every day. Gretel begins to wonder where the food comes from.

My thoughts

It feels like someone telling you what really happened to Hansel and Gretel. I saw this two years ago and I still remember the shock, which says quite a bit. If you're thinking, ‘Oh, Hansel and Gretel! Let's put that on,’ brace yourself. This is a full-on horror movie, and it gets gory.

Cast

Sophia Lillis, Samuel Leakey, Alice Krige

Trivia

The siblings' names switch places in the title. The change makes sense once you follow the older sister: caring for Hansel is familiar territory, but deciding what to do with her own power is new.
