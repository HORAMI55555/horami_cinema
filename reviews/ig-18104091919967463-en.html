Behind the clues, something darker waits.

Synopsis

An FBI agent investigating a serial killer encounters occult clues woven into the case. What begins as a criminal investigation leads toward something increasingly difficult to explain. She must decipher the signs before the killings continue.

My thoughts

Finally watched Longlegs, which got quite a bit of attention in Japan last year! Nicolas Cage playing a serial killer makes this feel like a big event. For me, the suspense and mystery were stronger than the outright scares. With the devil-worship and serial murders, it felt like a mix of It and Follow Me. Enjoyed it! You do have to think a little.

Cast

Maika Monroe · Nicolas Cage · Blair Underwood · Alicia Witt

Trivia

Written and directed by Osgood Perkins. Viewing tip: pay attention to empty areas of the frame and stretches of apparent inactivity, as well as the investigation itself.
