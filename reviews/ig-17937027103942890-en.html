Ghostbusters

2016

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5Scare1/5

They finally have proof of ghosts. Now they have to catch one.

Synopsis

An investigation at a New York mansion gives a group of scientists a close encounter with a real ghost. As more apparitions surface, subway worker Patty joins them, bringing local knowledge to a team armed with homemade ghost-catching gear.

My thoughts

I saw this one at the cinema. The opening actually felt like horror, but then it turned into more of an action movie. It’s a ghost story you can have fun with without getting scared. Maybe it’ll get you a little more into horror?

Cast

Melissa McCarthy, Kristen Wiig, Kate McKinnon, Leslie Jones

Trivia

Erin works at Columbia, but two MIT physicists suggested objects for her office. Abby and Holtzmann also tinker in a close recreation of an MIT lab. Around every delightfully impossible invention, there are details borrowed from the working lives of actual scientists.
