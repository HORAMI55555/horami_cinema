The Sixth Sense

1999

Scare2/5

Seeing them is frightening. Telling someone is harder.

Synopsis

Child psychologist Malcolm begins working with Cole, a boy who seems frightened even in familiar places. Earning his trust means taking seriously a secret Cole can barely put into words: he sees people who have died.

My thoughts

My friend Kura Sushi reminded me of this one! It's really good. I didn't find it scary, but the story and that ending make it worth watching.

Cast

Bruce Willis, Haley Joel Osment, Toni Collette

Trivia

Both Haley Joel Osment and Toni Collette received supporting acting Oscar nominations. One plays a child guarding a secret; the other plays a mother trying to protect him without knowing what the secret is.
