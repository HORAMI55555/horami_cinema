The Platform 2

2024

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3.5/5Scare2.5/5

When fairness becomes another form of fear.

Synopsis

In a vertical prison, a platform carries food down from the highest levels. New arrival Perempuán learns of strict rules intended to ensure everyone gets a share. But enforcement brings its own violence, and she must decide what survival and solidarity mean inside a system that keeps turning people against one another.

My thoughts

So good—I really love The Platform. Everyone is supposed to eat only the dish they requested, but greedy people take other portions, leaving nothing for the lower floors. By floor 300, how could anyone survive?! The premise is unlike anything else, so I enjoyed both films. It does get deep and confusing, and I had questions afterwards, but you can still enjoy it without decoding everything. Definitely thought-provoking. I would never want to end up there.

Cast

Milena Smit, Hovik Keuchkerian, Natalia Tena

Trivia

Galder Gaztelu-Urrutia returns as director, with Milena Smit and Hovik Keuchkerian playing new residents. His production comments describe expanding the same world through fresh characters and obstacles. A useful way into this instalment is to move beyond who gets food and ask who gets to make the rules. Individual greed and collective righteousness create different kinds of fear. Watching the dishes and the language of those rules makes the prison’s fragile order easier to examine.
