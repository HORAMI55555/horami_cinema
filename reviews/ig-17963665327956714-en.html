Heartbreak High

2022

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3.5/5

A secret map of who hooked up with whom just became public.

Synopsis

At a Sydney high school, someone discovers a map charting the students’ hookups. Amerie takes the heat for helping make it, and even her best friend Harper pulls away. With no idea what happened between them, Amerie starts the term leaning on two unexpected new friends.

My thoughts

Everyone has such a distinct personality that I never got bored. The show gets into gender, feminism and the ugly side of social media. If you like seeing what high school looks like for teenagers in another country, this is right up your alley.

Cast

Ayesha Madon, James Majoos, Chloé Hayden

Trivia

Hartley High had students long before smartphones reached its classrooms. The original Australian series ran for 210 episodes in the 1990s. The Sydney-shot reimagining introduces its new class in just eight episodes in season one. Hearing both generations say the same school name makes Hartley feel like a place with decades of alumni.
