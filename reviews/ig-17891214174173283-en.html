Missing You

2025

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5

Eleven years missing. One familiar face on a dating app.

Synopsis

Detective Kat Donovan spots her former fiancé Josh on a dating app, eleven years after he vanished without explanation. Searching for him reopens the mystery of her father’s murder and exposes secrets among the people closest to her. A long-lost love becomes the starting point for a much larger investigation.

My thoughts

Ohh, this was good!! A mix of bittersweet romance and suspense, and the series isn’t too long either. Some of the mystery feels familiar, but there are enough distinctive details to keep it interesting. The ending had me going, seriously?! Recommend!

Cast

Rosalind Eleazar · Ashley Walters · Richard Armitage

Trivia

Harlan Coben’s novel is set in New York City, but the adaptation moves the story to Britain. Filming took place around Manchester and North West England, giving the same underlying mystery a different urban atmosphere.
