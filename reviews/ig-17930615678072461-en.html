Their plan to revive a Texas town overlooks one longtime resident.

Synopsis

Young entrepreneurs arrive in Harlow to show potential buyers around a fading Texas town. One building they thought was vacant still has residents. A confrontation over their eviction brings Leatherface out of hiding.

My thoughts

A sequel to The Texas Chain Saw Massacre! I hit play the second I saw it. The original seems to top every horror ranking, though I thought it was just okay. This one felt a bit scarier to me. Now THIS is horror: gore and big jump scares, with none of the ghost-story kind of fear. I’d recommend it.

Cast

Sarah Yarkin, Elsie Fisher, Mark Burnham

Trivia

The town on screen is in Texas, but the cameras were in Bulgaria. Director David Blue Garcia is himself a Texan. Long before Leatherface makes an entrance, the movie is already pulling off an illusion: a filmmaker recreating the feel of home on another continent.
