Call Me Chihiro

2023

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5

A little company. A little room to be yourself.

Synopsis

Chihiro works in a bento shop in a seaside town and speaks openly about her past as a sex worker. Children and adults carrying different kinds of loneliness are drawn to her unguarded presence. Through ordinary encounters, the film explores connection without demanding that everyone live or heal in the same way.

My thoughts

A friend was watching this and I joined partway through, but I thought the story was really good. It kept me thinking long after it ended. I feel like I can understand Chihiro's decision, but it's still sad... and maybe that's just how it has to be. Pick this when you want something quiet and deeply moving.

Cast

Kasumi Arimura · Hana Toyoshima · Tetta Shimada · van · Lily Franky · Jun Fubuki

Trivia

Rikiya Imaizumi adapted the manga for the screen. Netflix describes van's performance as Chihiro's friend Basil as her first acting job, adding an interesting debut to the ensemble.
