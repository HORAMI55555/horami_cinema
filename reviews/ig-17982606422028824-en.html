1995

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5

A matchmaking expert with a blind spot for her own heart.

Synopsis

Beverly Hills teenager Cher loves fashion and her place at the top of the social ladder. After successfully matchmaking two teachers, she gives new student Tai a makeover. But arranging other people’s appearances and romances proves much easier than understanding her own feelings and friendships.

My thoughts

It’s quite an old American teen movie, but the whole vibe is so cute 😍 The main character is so adorable… It’s such a cute, comforting movie to watch. Even its age makes it cuter. Cute!!!

Cast

Alicia Silverstone, Stacey Dash, Brittany Murphy, Paul Rudd

Trivia

The dazzling wardrobe was built with considerable ingenuity and a limited budget. Costume designer Mona May translated high-fashion ideas into age-appropriate looks for 16-year-olds, using thrift shops and mall stores along the way. Cher had 63 costume changes. While the story reworks Jane Austen’s Emma, the clothing does more than modernize a classic: it helps define the characters and their development. The outfits are effectively another way of introducing the people wearing them.
