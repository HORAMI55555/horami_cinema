Good Will Hunting

1997

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4.5/5

A brilliant mind can still be afraid to move forward.

Synopsis

Will works as a university janitor while hiding extraordinary mathematical talent and painful memories. After his abilities are discovered, he begins working with a professor and attending sessions with therapist Sean. As friendship, romance and opportunity pull him in different directions, he must confront what he actually wants from his future.

My thoughts

I had seen this before and somehow forgotten to post it! A friend’s wonderful choice gave me the chance to rewatch. Every time, it makes me want to try harder and challenge myself. Maybe stories about human struggles become classics because everyone can recognise something of themselves in them. That is hardly a profound observation, but I love this film.

Cast

Matt Damon, Robin Williams, Ben Affleck, Minnie Driver

Trivia

Matt Damon, who plays Will, and Ben Affleck, who plays his friend Chuckie, also wrote the screenplay together. Their names sit behind the story as well as inside its friendship. Watch for the moments when Will can answer an intellectual challenge but cannot respond to an emotional one. Both Sean’s sessions and ordinary exchanges with friends become about trust as much as talent. On a rewatch, the contrast between quick, confident speech and silence can be especially revealing.
