Get Out

2017

Scare2/5

Why are her parents' friends so interested in him?

Synopsis

Chris, a Black photographer, visits his white girlfriend Rose's family for the first time. Her parents welcome him, yet the guests' questions and the staff's strained smiles leave him struggling to explain exactly what's wrong.

My thoughts

This was fun. It gives me that same “Get out of there!” feeling as Ready or Not. There's real depth under the entertaining setup, too. A horror classic.

Cast

Daniel Kaluuya, Allison Williams, Catherine Keener

Trivia

Writer-director Jordan Peele won the Oscar for original screenplay. Early remarks can pass for awkward small talk on a first viewing; revisit the introductions and the same lines acquire a rather different meaning.
