Grave Encounters

2011

Scare2/5

The hospital doors are supposed to open in the morning.

Synopsis

A paranormal TV crew has itself locked inside an abandoned psychiatric hospital overnight. At first they're hunting for material to spice up the show. Then even finding their way back to the entrance becomes a problem.

My thoughts

They go to a run-of-the-mill abandoned hospital, things go south, everyone's toast. I wasn't scared at all and was ready to switch it off about halfway through. Grr!

Cast

Sean Rogerson, Ashleigh Gryzko, Mackenzie Gray

Trivia

Grave Encounters is also the name of the show within the film. Listen to the host put on his TV voice early on: later, the moment he can no longer keep it up is a scare in its own right.
