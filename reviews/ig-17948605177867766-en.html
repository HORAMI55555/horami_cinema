Choose or Die

2022

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5Scare2/5

Type an answer. Someone else pays for it.

Synopsis

Kayla boots up an old computer game in the hope of claiming its prize. A choice appears on a text-only screen. When she presses a key, the consequence plays out in front of her. Walking away from the computer won’t stop the next prompt.

My thoughts

I spotted this in the rankings and ended up having a really good time. A couple of bits were pretty gory, but I could handle them. It’s about a curse inside a game, and it flew by. I’d recommend it, especially if it’s ghosts you can’t stand. There aren’t any here.

Cast

Iola Evans, Asa Butterfield, Eddie Marsan

Trivia

The voice on the prize hotline belongs to Robert Englund, Freddy Krueger himself. The two leads told Radio Times they never actually met him during filming. For the actors, just as for their characters, the horror legend existed only as a voice.
