Sweet Tooth

2008

The real question is who lives in the cottage.

Synopsis

Two children slip into a cottage in the woods. It feels like a small adventure until they discover whose home they have entered. A witch awaits in this short horror film inspired by the Brothers Grimm.

My thoughts

A live-action Hansel and Gretel in just ten minutes! It pulled me in so fast, and it was genuinely eerie. And that little boy is Timothée Chalamet 😍 I could watch it right after signing up. If you’ve got ten minutes, give it a go!

Cast

Timothée Chalamet, Jackie Rhoads, Tate Steinsiek

Trivia

If you’re looking for Chalamet’s early appearance, look for the ten-minute film from 2008. Rory Kindersley and Jason Noto shared directing duties. With only ten minutes to tell a fairy tale, a decision as small as stepping inside a cottage has to do the work of an entire first act.
