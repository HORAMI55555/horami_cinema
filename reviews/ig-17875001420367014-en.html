Synopsis

Catherine moves to the country for her husband's new job and senses a presence in their house. Investigating the property uncovers uncomfortable gaps in her own marriage.

My thoughts

That husband was absolute trash. I couldn't make much sense of it, things just kept getting worse right to the end, and it all felt shallow to me.

Cast

Amanda Seyfried, James Norton, Natalia Dyer

Trivia

Adapted from Elizabeth Brundage's All Things Cease to Appear, the film makes Catherine an art restorer. Reading what lies underneath a finished surface becomes a useful way to approach the house itself.
