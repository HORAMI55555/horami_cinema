I Am Not Okay With This

2020

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★2/5

Before she can put her feelings into words, things start moving.

Synopsis

After her father’s death, teenager Sydney tries to work out her feelings for her best friend and her frustration at home by writing in a diary. But when those feelings boil over, strange things begin happening outside its pages.

My thoughts

Netflix kept recommending this series to me, so I gave it a go. The episodes are short and I finished it pretty quickly. If you’re into American high school movies, you’ll probably enjoy it. The story itself was just okay for me.

Cast

Sophia Lillis, Wyatt Oleff, Sofia Bryant

Trivia

This began as a graphic novel by Charles Forsman. The adaptation brings together Jonathan Entwistle, a director of The End of the F***ing World, and producers of Stranger Things. It’s a fitting combination for a series where an awkward teenage silence can give way to a supernatural outburst.
