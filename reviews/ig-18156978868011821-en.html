ライフ・イズ・ビューティフル

1997

What his father tries to save is the world his son believes in.

Synopsis

Guido and Dora meet in Italy and start a family. After Guido and his little boy are sent to a concentration camp, Guido improvises an elaborate game to shield his son from the meaning of what he sees.

My thoughts

A non-horror pick. An absolute classic. I cried. There's a lot to sit with here.

Cast

Roberto Benigni, Nicoletta Braschi, Giorgio Cantarini

Trivia

The man inventing jokes and gestures for his son was also directing the film. Roberto Benigni won the Academy Award for Best Actor for his Italian-language performance as Guido.
