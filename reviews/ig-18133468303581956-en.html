Badly in Love

2025

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5

No playing it cool when feelings run this high.

Synopsis

Eleven people with rebellious pasts move into a secluded school for fourteen days. Romance, confrontation and vulnerability emerge as they live together. Beneath the tough exteriors are people deciding whether to trust again—and whom to confess their feelings to at graduation.

My thoughts

This was so much fun!!! That tough, all-in energy—yes, that’s exactly the vibe. It has a completely different appeal from the usual dating shows, and I got through it so quickly. If you’re bored with regular reality dating shows, give this one a try for a change!

Cast

Hosts: MEGUMI · AK-69 · Nagano

Trivia

MEGUMI also developed and produced the show. This is a dating reality series following participants living together, rather than a scripted drama with actors playing characters.
