THE HORAMI COLLECTION
✧✦

Onnanoko Kirai

2014

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★2/5

What happens when being cute stops being enough?

Synopsis

Kiriko is 23, works at a design company and treats being cute as her greatest strength. Attention from men comes easily; getting along with other women does not. When a romantic rival appears and she meets a man who refuses to respond as expected, her confidence begins to unravel. Beneath its sweet appearance lies a story about insecurity and the need to be wanted.

My thoughts

I rewatched this after a long time, and honestly... I was bored!! I enjoyed it as a student, but it didn't work for me as an adult. Maybe one for a day when you have absolutely nothing else to do. It follows a girl with a pretty unpleasant personality who puts on a sweet act, until that facade starts to slip.

Cast

Aoi Morikawa · Kenta Kiguchi · Keigo Tani · Saki Inoue

Trivia

The film won four awards at MOOSIC LAB 2014, including the runner-up Grand Prix and audience award. It is a 2014 production that received a standalone theatrical release in 2015, which explains the different years found in listings.
