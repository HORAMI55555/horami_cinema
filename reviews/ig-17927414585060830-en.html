Playful Kiss

2010

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5

First comes rejection. Then they become housemates.

Synopsis

Ha-ni confesses to top student Seung-jo and gets turned down. When her family loses its home, her father’s friend offers them a place to stay. His son turns out to be the boy who rejected her.

My thoughts

Kim Hyun-joong is in this, and he looks so good! It’s one of those Korean dramas lots of people already know, but I’d choose it over plenty of romance films. A cool, clever boy with zero interest in love falls for a wonderfully clumsy girl.

Cast

Jung So-min, Kim Hyun-joong

Trivia

This is the Korean adaptation of Kaoru Tada’s manga Itazura na Kiss. If you know Kotoko and Naoki from the original, watch Ha-ni and Seung-jo at the dinner table: a school crush once out of reach is suddenly sitting across from her.
