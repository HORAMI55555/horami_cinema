The Mouse Trap

2024

Her birthday party becomes someone else's game.

Synopsis

Alex is spending her 21st birthday working the late shift at an amusement arcade. Her friends arrive with a surprise, but a killer wearing a mouse mask has plans of his own. The celebration becomes a struggle to survive as a place built for fun turns into a hunting ground.

My thoughts

Honestly, this went beyond B-movie territory into D-movie territory for me. I was so bored I stopped watching! The opening message was funny enough to be worth seeing on its own. The concept had potential, but the wooden acting and weak story really didn't work for me. Back to the drawing board!

Cast

Sophie McIntosh · Simon Phillips · Nick Biskupek

Trivia

The filmmakers identified the early Mickey Mouse short Steamboat Willie as their creative starting point. The central idea is the collision between a familiar cartoon image and the conventions of a slasher film.
