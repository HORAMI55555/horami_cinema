What if the person protecting you is the danger?

Synopsis

Chloe, a teenager who uses a wheelchair, is homeschooled and cared for by her mother, Diane. As she looks forward to college and greater independence, small inconsistencies begin to trouble her. Investigating the life her mother has carefully built around her leads Chloe toward a frightening possibility.

My thoughts

This was so entertaining!!! The truth behind the main character’s condition had me going, wait, WHAT? And that mother,, terrifying. If you like thrillers with a seriously disturbing parent, this is such a good pick. The ending felt satisfying too.

Cast

Sarah Paulson, Kiera Allen

Trivia

Run marked Kiera Allen’s feature-film debut. The stage performer stepped into a tense leading role opposite Sarah Paulson.
