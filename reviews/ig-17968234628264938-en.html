You Get Me

2017

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★2/5

One night follows him back to school.

Synopsis

After a major fight with his girlfriend Ali, Tyler spends the night with Holly, a visitor from out of town. He reconciles with Ali the next morning, only to find Holly enrolled at his school. Her refusal to let their connection end threatens his relationship and everyday life.

My thoughts

A high-school movie from abroad about getting involved with a psycho girl and ending up in a mess! Easy to watch and good for passing the time. Recommended when you don’t want a movie to leave you exhausted.

Cast

Bella Thorne, Halston Sage, Taylor John Smith

Trivia

Netflix frames the film as an obsession thriller in which a one-night encounter follows a boy to school. Bella Thorne plays Holly, Halston Sage plays girlfriend Ali, and Taylor John Smith plays Tyler between them. Viewing tip: Tyler treats the encounter as over, while Holly treats it as a beginning. Even ordinary school-romance scenes become more tense when you track who knows which parts of the story.
