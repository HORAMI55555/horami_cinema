XO, Kitty

2023

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3.5/5

An expert matchmaker meets her own messy heart.

Synopsis

Confident matchmaker Kitty moves to a prestigious school in Seoul to reunite with long-distance boyfriend Dae and learn more about her late mother. The reunion goes off script, and new friendships and complicated feelings follow. Life abroad makes her reconsider what she knows about herself.

My thoughts

This is fun, and I recommend it!!! The younger sister of the main character in To All the Boys I’ve Loved Before is the lead this time. So watching those first makes this even more fun because the stories connect. It mixes an American high-school series with Korea, which feels so fresh. You can finish it quickly, so please watch! It’s fun thinking, “Oh, so this is what high school in Korea is like.”

Cast

Anna Cathcart, Minyeong Choi, Gia Kim

Trivia

Kitty, the younger sister who helped set her sister’s romance in motion in To All the Boys, becomes the lead. Netflix’s production announcement confirms Anna Cathcart’s return and the start of filming in Seoul, with original novelist Jenny Han creating the series. Viewing tip: watch how Kitty’s confidence in other people’s love lives holds up when she faces her own feelings. Alongside the film connections, her search for her mother’s past adds another dimension.
