The same face. A different person.

Synopsis

Three girls are abducted by a stranger. As Casey searches for a way out, she realizes that their captor’s speech and behavior shift dramatically between different identities. Some seem approachable; others are threatening. Finding an escape means navigating an unpredictable situation in which the person facing them can change at any moment.

My thoughts

This was SO interesting!!!!! It starts as a desperate escape from an abductor, but his different identities made the setup feel really fresh to me. One moment I thought they might get away, and the next I was convinced they were doomed. I was so tense, honestly haha. If you like Don’t Breathe, give this a go. I want to watch it again!

Cast

James McAvoy, Anya Taylor-Joy, Betty Buckley

Trivia

McAvoy described developing a separate posture, speaking style, history and motivation for each identity. Watch how those physical choices make one face feel like different people.
