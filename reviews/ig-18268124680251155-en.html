Love Village

2023

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5

Love comes with a whole life behind it.

Synopsis

Adults with very different life stories move into a traditional countryside house to look for love. Past marriages, children and difficult experiences all shape their choices. As they restore the home and share everyday life, romance becomes a question of whether they can make room for someone new in the lives they already have.

My thoughts

The setup was so interesting!!! These people have lived: some are divorced, some have children, and everyone brings their own history. That made it feel much more real and serious than the dating shows I’m used to. Hearing what they’d been through honestly made me cry several times. I finished feeling so moved,, Also, I absolutely love Anchovy from season 1. Recommended!

Cast

Atsushi Tamura, Becky (studio hosts)

Trivia

Season 1 spent six consecutive weeks in Netflix’s weekly Top 10 in Japan, finding an audience for a dating show centered on adults with substantial life experience.
