One of Us Is Lying

2021

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5

Five students in detention. Far more secrets.

Synopsis

Five high school students from different circles meet in detention. A death places the remaining students under suspicion. Each has secrets to protect, and their search for the truth becomes a struggle with mutual distrust, school rumors and an unseen adversary.

My thoughts

Netflix mystery series really never miss‼️ This is so good: teens overseas are being messed with by a hacker pulling the strings behind the scenes. The story is easy to follow and doesn’t take too much brainpower, so I recommend it when you want to jump into an entertaining mystery series! It’s really similar to Control Z.

Cast

Annalisa Cochrane, Chibuikem Uche, Marianly Tejada, Cooper van Grootel

Trivia

Netflix describes this as a book-based ensemble mystery about high school students. Its compact starting point brings five different personalities into one detention room. Viewing tip: follow the secrets each person protects as well as the murder investigation. Remembering that someone may lie for reasons unrelated to murder can change how you read them. The gap between public standing and private life lets suspicion and sympathy coexist.
