Yesterday, a doorway. Today, a wall.

Synopsis

Olivia is ready to leave Tim, but opening the apartment door reveals a solid black wall. The windows and other exits are blocked too. Forced to work together, the couple joins their neighbors in searching the building for a way out. Their familiar home has become an unexplained trap, and nobody knows who—or what—put it there.

My thoughts

I really enjoyed this!! Imagine suddenly finding your home surrounded by mysterious walls with no way out. Everyone struggles to escape, and then that ending...!! The setup felt refreshingly different from the horror films I'd been watching. It's not a ghost or psychopath story, so I found it much more approachable.

Cast

Matthias Schweighöfer · Ruby O. Fee · Frederick Lau

Trivia

Writer-director Philip Koch also created Tribes of Europa, and the film is set in Hamburg. Viewing tip: look at the emotional distance between the couple as well as the physical barriers keeping them inside.
