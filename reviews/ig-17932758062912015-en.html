Five Feet Apart

2019

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5

Falling in love across a distance they cannot cross.

Synopsis

Stella meets Will while receiving treatment for cystic fibrosis. Their condition means keeping a safe distance to reduce the risk of cross-infection. As their connection deepens, a few feet become an immense barrier. Their romance asks what closeness can mean when ordinary physical contact is out of reach.

My thoughts

This was so good 😭 Having Cole Sprouse in it was already a win, but the story was distinctive, engaging and heartbreaking too. Being able to touch someone so casually might actually be a miracle. Definitely joining my recommended romance list!

Cast

Haley Lu Richardson, Cole Sprouse, Moises Arias

Trivia

Director Justin Baldoni’s friendship with Claire Wineland, who lived with cystic fibrosis, helped inspire the project. Claire’s Place Foundation also identifies her influence on Stella’s decorated hospital room. Those details make the room a personal space rather than only a place of treatment. A rewarding viewing angle is how the characters find forms of closeness that do not rely on touch: words, looks and the things around them become ways of reaching across the physical distance.
