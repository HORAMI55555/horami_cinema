Haunted Mansion

2023

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5Scare1/5

A new home. Far too many ghostly housemates.

Synopsis

A mother and son move into a New Orleans mansion for a fresh start, only to find their home full of supernatural disturbances. They call on an eccentric group of supposed spiritual experts, each carrying troubles of their own. Together they explore a house of ghostly residents, elaborate tricks and hidden history in this spooky fantasy adventure.

My thoughts

Disney horror might be a first for me, haha! I wondered how it would turn out, and it’s definitely Disney: lighter on the scares, with lots of fun and humor, but still a spooky feel. It’s so faithful to the park attraction—I kept recognizing places! Such a fun Halloween watch for the atmosphere and Disney feeling.

Cast

LaKeith Stanfield · Rosario Dawson · Owen Wilson · Tiffany Haddish · Danny DeVito

Trivia

Justin Simien directs this 2023 film inspired by the Disney theme park attraction. If you know the ride, look for familiar details in the mansion’s design and the ghosts’ appearances.
