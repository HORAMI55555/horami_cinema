Her new heart remembers a girl she never met.

Synopsis

A heart transplant saves teenage Sasha’s life. When the donor’s parents invite her into their world, she starts seeing things she can’t remember experiencing. At the school Becky once attended, Sasha begins piecing together what happened the night her donor died.

My thoughts

I got completely hooked on this Netflix series. After the transplant, the girl seems to be picking up memories and feelings from whoever had her heart before her. I hadn’t seen anything quite like that. There’s a cult angle, plenty of horror, and a story I wanted to follow even between the scares. I kept hitting next episode. Such a recommendation from me!

Cast

Sivan Alyra Rose, Uma Thurman, Tony Goldwyn

Trivia

Sivan Alyra Rose is San Carlos Apache. Native America Calling introduced her as the first Native woman to lead a Netflix series. It’s worth watching what Sasha chooses to keep for herself when Becky’s family offers her a place in their world.
