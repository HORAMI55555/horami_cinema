Miseducation

2023

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★2/5

A fallen it girl heads to university to reinvent herself.

Synopsis

After her mother’s corruption scandal destroys her glamorous lifestyle and reputation, Mbali enrolls at a small-town South African university to reinvent herself. New friendships, romance and student politics offer opportunities, but her plans to regain popularity keep producing unexpected chaos.

My thoughts

International teen dramas are one of my favorite things to watch, and this one is set in South Africa, so it felt a little different from the others—refreshing in a good way!! The storyline is pretty familiar, and it’s an enjoyable enough watch! I was surprised that high-school life in Africa doesn’t seem all that different from America 😳 But what does feel different is how strongly religion and awareness of being white or Black come through.

Cast

Buntu Petse, Lunga Shabalala, Preven Reddy

Trivia

The setting is a university, not a high school. Netflix’s announcement places Mbali at Grahamstown University in Makhanda, South Africa, as she tries to escape her mother’s corrupt legacy. Burnt Onion Productions, the company behind How to Ruin Christmas, produced the series. Viewing tip: follow the student elections and campus gossip alongside the romance. It is a campus comedy where the performance of popularity collides with the honesty friendship requires.
