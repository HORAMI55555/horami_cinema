僕の初恋をキミに捧ぐ

2009

For these two children, “when we grow up” is a huge promise.

Synopsis

Takuma, a boy with a heart condition, promises to marry Mayu, his doctor's daughter. As they grow older, he tries to spare her the pain his illness may bring. Mayu has her own ideas about who gets to make that decision.

My thoughts

A non-horror pick. One of my favorite romances, and one I'd absolutely recommend!

Cast

Mao Inoue, Masaki Okada

Trivia

Adapted from Kotomi Aoki's manga, the film starts with a promise made long before either child can grasp its cost. Watch how the same childhood words come to mean different things to Mayu and Takuma as teenagers.
