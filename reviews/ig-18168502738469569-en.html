Synopsis

In a small Newfoundland fishing town, Calvin Penney is still haunted by his father's mysterious death. When an unknown sea creature threatens the community, protecting his family also means fighting for a way of life that is already disappearing. This six-part thriller turns familiar coastal waters into a source of uncertainty and fear.

My thoughts

This is a fairly short series, so I finished it in a few days! My reaction was basically, 'Okay... that was good??' The very end left me confused, but apart from that I found it a happy ending. It mixes a mysterious deep-sea creature, psychological horror and small-town tension. Not overwhelmingly scary, but entertaining enough that I'd recommend it!

Cast

Josh Hartnett · Mackenzie Davis · Charlie Heaton

Trivia

Creator Jesse McKeown described years of visiting Newfoundland and wanting to set an expansive story in its rugged, isolated landscape. The setting itself helped inspire the series, rather than simply providing a backdrop for its creature.
