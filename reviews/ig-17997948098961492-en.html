One invitation brings an old love back.

Synopsis

University student Izumi is invited back to her old school's drama club by Hayama, the teacher who once helped her through a lonely period. Helping with a performance means seeing him again—and confronting feelings she had tried to put away. Old attachment and new relationships become tangled in a romance shaped by memory.

My thoughts

This brings back memories! I think I watched it around middle-school age, and I remember loving it and feeling its afterglow for ages. I barely remember the details now, but I'm pretty sure it meant a lot to me at the time.

Cast

Jun Matsumoto · Kasumi Arimura · Kentaro Sakaguchi

Trivia

Isao Yukisada directed this adaptation of Rio Shimamoto's novel. Viewing tip: notice not only what happens in the relationship, but also how Izumi remembers it; the experience of looking back is part of the film's emotional texture.
