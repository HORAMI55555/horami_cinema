The Cabin in the Woods

2011

Scare1/5

Someone's workday starts before the kids reach the cabin.

Synopsis

Five friends spending the weekend at a woodland cabin find a collection of objects in the cellar. Elsewhere, employees start their day with ordinary workplace banter. What one location could possibly have to do with the other is the puzzle.

My thoughts

I wouldn't call it horror, but it's fun! Even people who don't usually do horror might enjoy it, though it wouldn't be my first recommendation.

Cast

Kristen Connolly, Chris Hemsworth, Richard Jenkins

Trivia

Director Drew Goddard co-wrote the screenplay with Joss Whedon. Cutting between cabin clichés and office chatter turns a familiar horror setup into a question of perspective: what might this scene look like from the other end?
