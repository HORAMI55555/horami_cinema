Synopsis

Newly split from the Joker, Harley finds Gotham lining up for payback. A stolen diamond brings a young pickpocket and four women into the same fight.

My thoughts

It feels like a Suicide Squad follow-up that gives Harley Quinn the whole spotlight. I could watch her being this cool all day, though Suicide Squad still wins for me.

Cast

Margot Robbie, Jurnee Smollett, Mary Elizabeth Winstead

Trivia

Harley narrates her own adventure. When she realizes she skipped a crucial detail, the edit doubles back with her, turning her train of thought into the film's structure.
