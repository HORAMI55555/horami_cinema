Her uniform is immaculate. Her reasons for taking the job are complicated.

Synopsis

In 1947, nurse Mildred Ratched arrives at a psychiatric hospital in Northern California. With polished manners and a sharp eye for other people’s weaknesses, she manoeuvres her way into a job. Her interest in one man held there is anything but professional.

My thoughts

This didn’t really feel like horror to me, but I enjoyed it. Watching her think several steps ahead of everyone to save her brother was so satisfying. I recognized quite a few faces from other Netflix shows, which made it feel familiar, and Dolly the nurse was especially cute. If you want a Netflix series that won’t take forever to finish, I’d recommend it.

Cast

Sarah Paulson, Cynthia Nixon, Judy Davis

Trivia

One of the costume team’s first decisions was the fabric and precise shade of green for the nurses’ uniforms. The leads would wear them so often that this one choice helped define the whole ward. In a room full of identically dressed nurses, watch how Paulson sets Mildred apart with nothing more than her posture and a glance.
