Who will you tell before Christmas?

Synopsis

As Christmas approaches in London, crushes, marriages and family bonds reach turning points. Seemingly separate lives keep crossing paths.

My thoughts

I'd forgotten I watched this at Christmas! I went in because it's such a holiday classic and it was wonderful. So warm and properly Christmassy. I loved following so many people's stories at the same time.

Cast

Hugh Grant, Emma Thompson, Alan Rickman

Trivia

This was screenwriter Richard Curtis's feature directing debut. Watch for the way a supporting character in one romance becomes a sibling or colleague in another.
