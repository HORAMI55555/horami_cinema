Unknown Number: The High School Catfish

2025

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5

One unknown number turns everyday life upside down.

Synopsis

A teenage girl and her boyfriend become targets of relentless harassment from an unknown phone number. As the messages continue, suspicion strains their school lives and relationships. Through interviews with those involved, this documentary follows the search for the person behind the campaign.

My thoughts

Netflix recommended this and I watched it straight away!! It’s a real-life documentary featuring the people involved, starting with a high school girl receiving horrible messages out of nowhere. And it goes on for over a year—who is doing this? Really compelling and easy to follow. The most surprising part is that even the person responsible appears in it! At that point, are they just owning it?! It flies by. Recommend!

Cast

Interview participants: Lauryn Licari and other people involved in the case

Trivia

Director Skye Borgman made a point of including young people who came under suspicion. Alongside the central mystery, their interviews reveal what it was like to keep attending school while rumors circulated.
