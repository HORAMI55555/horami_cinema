Boo, Bitch

2022

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5

An invisible teen finally stands out—as a ghost?

Synopsis

After spending high school under the radar, Erika decides to make memories with her best friend Gia before graduation. The morning after a party, she finds herself believing she has become a ghost. As she scrambles to find romance and popularity in the time she thinks remains, even her most precious friendship starts to wobble.

My thoughts

This is so quick to finish, so moving, and I recommend it SO much!! An American high-school series. A really plain high-school girl suddenly becomes incredibly popular and starts thriving, except she has already died shortly before that and is getting popular as a ghost. The beautiful friendship at the end made me cry.

Cast

Lana Condor, Zoe Colletti, Mason Versaw

Trivia

Lana Condor, familiar from To All the Boys, serves as both star and executive producer. The co-showrunners are Lauren Iungerich of On My Block and Erin Ehrlich, whose credits include Crazy Ex-Girlfriend. The project brings together creators experienced in awkward adolescence and offbeat comedy. It is an interesting shift for a familiar romantic lead, who also helps support this story of ghostly confusion and school popularity from behind the scenes.
