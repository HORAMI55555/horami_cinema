Uglies

2024

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5

A perfect face. But at what cost?

Synopsis

In a society where everyone undergoes beautifying surgery at sixteen, Tally cannot wait for her transformation. When a friend rejects the procedure and disappears, she ventures beyond the city to find her. What she discovers calls into question the supposedly perfect future she has always wanted.

My thoughts

Really good—I liked this a lot. Everyone gets an operation to become their ideal self, inside and out, once they reach twenty, but this supposedly perfect society does not look so ideal after all. Honestly, such an entertaining watch. Recommend! [Story clarification: the operation age in the film is sixteen.]

Cast

Joey King, Keith Powers, Chase Stokes, Laverne Cox

Trivia

Scott Westerfeld’s novel was published in 2005. Director McG connects its ideas to today’s pressure to edit photographs before posting them online: the futuristic setting reflects a familiar demand to look a certain way. A useful detail to watch is the contrast between the dazzling city and life outside it. Asking which place actually offers more freedom shifts attention from changing faces to the choices people are allowed to make.
