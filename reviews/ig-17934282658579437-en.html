A Ghost Story

2017

Scare1/5

The one left behind is the one who haunts the house.

Synopsis

A dead man returns home beneath a white sheet. Unable to speak to the woman he loves, he watches time pass at a different pace for a house and its occupants.

My thoughts

At first I thought, what on earth am I watching? Then its world pulled me in. How is something so short this vast? Halfway through I felt this ache of sadness. Unlike anything else and not scary, so anyone can try it. I need a few explanations to unravel all the connections.

Cast

Casey Affleck, Rooney Mara

Trivia

The nearly square frame has rounded corners. A story spanning immense stretches of time is confined to an image that resembles an old family photograph.
