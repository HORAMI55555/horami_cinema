Child's Play

1988

Scare3/5

Her son's new friend has a voice no battery can explain.

Synopsis

Andy finally gets the doll he wants for his birthday. When people around him start dying, he insists Chucky can move by himself. His mother Karen has no way of proving what happens whenever the adults look away.

My thoughts

Like Annabelle, it's definitely horror, though I didn't find it all that terrifying. If horror isn't your thing it might finish you off! I did think, “You really think making me jump is enough? Nice try.”

Cast

Catherine Hicks, Alex Vincent, Brad Dourif

Trivia

In the 1988 original, Brad Dourif plays the human killer and voices Chucky. Even after the full-sized man is replaced by a toy, the sound of his voice tells you exactly who's in the room.
