Would you save the people who torment you?

Synopsis

Bullied about her body in a small Spanish town, Sara witnesses the girls who torment her being abducted by a stranger. Should she tell the police what she saw, or stay silent? Her pain and conscience pull her in different directions as the film explores the uneasy boundary between revenge and complicity.

My thoughts

This felt fresh! After being bullied, she sees her tormentors being kidnapped and pretends she hasn’t seen anything. But the kidnapper is dangerous too, and she nearly gets drawn into it before her conscience wins out. For me, it’s a good watch when I don’t want to overthink things—not a deeply layered story.

Cast

Laura Galán · Richard Holmes · Carmen Machi · Irene Ferreiro

Trivia

Carlota Pereda expanded her own 2018 short film into this feature. Viewing tip: focus not only on what Sara sees, but also on what she chooses not to say.
