Dollhouse

2025

☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★ 3/5 · RecommendedScare 3/5

The doll they loved begins to change their home.

Synopsis

Grieving the loss of her young daughter, Yoshie finds comfort in a doll that resembles her. After another daughter is born, the family gradually leaves the doll behind. Years later, the child starts playing with it, and unsettling events begin disrupting their home.

My thoughts

I’d heard this was pretty scary, so I gave it a go! For a Japanese horror film, I felt it had a fairly developed story. It wasn’t just one random scare after another—there was some slowly building dread too!! It felt believable enough to pull me into the film. Nice!

Cast

Masami Nagasawa · Koji Seto · Tetsushi Tanaka · Aoi Ikemura · Jun Fubuki

Trivia

Shinobu Yaguchi, known for Waterboys and Swing Girls, created the story, wrote the screenplay and directed. According to the official introduction, Masami Nagasawa was eager to participate after reading the script.
