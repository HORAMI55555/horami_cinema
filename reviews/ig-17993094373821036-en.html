Fakes

2022

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★2.5/5

Fake IDs. Real money. Real consequences.

Synopsis

Teenage best friends Zoe and Becca unexpectedly turn fake IDs into a thriving business. Orders and money transform their lives, but their casual venture leads into genuine danger. As each tells a different version of events, the costs of friendship and success begin to emerge.

My thoughts

Once again, the cute main character is named Zoe! The series is about fake IDs in America, and it seems like it might be based on a true story. It follows two high-school girls making fake IDs and earning loads of money. It was entertaining, but after seeing the scary reality at the end, I definitely didn’t want to try it myself.

Cast

Emilija Baranac, Jennifer Tong, Richard Harmon

Trivia

The distinctive device is the question of whose success story to trust. Netflix’s official materials describe two unreliable narrators, alternating viewpoints, and direct addresses to the audience. Even the first two episodes disagree about whose idea launched the business. Viewing tip: compare their memories of the same events. Enjoying their self-serving accounts and noticing when each shifts the blame onto the other makes the storytelling device easier to appreciate.
