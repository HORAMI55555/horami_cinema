To the Bone

2017

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5

Recovery begins with a difficult look inward.

Synopsis

Twenty-year-old Ellen has been through several eating-disorder treatment programmes. Her family sends her to a group home led by an unconventional doctor, where she meets young people facing their own struggles. Shared routines, difficult conversations and unexpected connections challenge her to reconsider how she relates to herself and to recovery.

My thoughts

Oof, this hurt, but I was glad the main character seemed ready to make a better start by the end. It is set in a home for people with eating disorders, and their pain felt so raw and immediate. It really made me feel how difficult it can be to get out of that struggle. Highly recommended if this kind of story interests you.

Cast

Lily Collins, Keanu Reeves, Alex Sharp

Trivia

This was television writer Marti Noxon’s feature directing debut, with Lily Collins at its centre and Keanu Reeves playing the doctor. Netflix’s production notes highlight the film’s mix of difficult subject matter and humour. One rewarding detail is the conversation that is not directly about illness: small jokes, preferences and reactions reveal personalities beyond a diagnosis. Paying attention to those moments changes the way the shared meals and routines register, keeping the characters’ individual lives in view.
