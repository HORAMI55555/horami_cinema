The Last House on the Left

2009

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5Scare3/5

The danger outside has come to their door.

Synopsis

While her parents stay at their remote lake house, Mari heads into town with a friend and crosses paths with dangerous criminals. Later that night, strangers arrive at the house seeking shelter from a storm. Her parents have no idea how these visitors are connected to their missing daughter. A family holiday becomes a brutal story of survival and revenge.

My thoughts

Wow. The story is simple, but it felt like I'd been through a much longer film! I was really rooting for the parents fighting for their daughter, and the ending was satisfying. What unsettled me most was the atmosphere around that group of four and the fear of encountering people like them. It felt horribly plausible. A pretty gripping watch, so I'd recommend it!

Cast

Sara Paxton · Garret Dillahunt · Monica Potter · Aaron Paul

Trivia

This is the 2009 remake of the 1972 film. The original's director and producer, Wes Craven and Sean S. Cunningham, returned as producers. Include the year when searching: the two films share the same English title.
