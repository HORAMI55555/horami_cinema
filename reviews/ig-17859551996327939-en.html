Synopsis

After losing her job, Lou takes a position caring for Will, who uses a wheelchair after an accident. Conversation is awkward at first. As Lou plans trips together, each discovers that the other has very different ideas about the future.

My thoughts

How can a movie be this lovely and this sad? My heart actually hurt. Yep, I cried.

Cast

Emilia Clarke, Sam Claflin, Janet McTeer

Trivia

Novelist Jojo Moyes wrote the screenplay herself. On screen, Lou's clothes and her habit of talking through awkward silences do some of the work her inner voice could do on the page.
