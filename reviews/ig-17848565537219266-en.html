The Neon Demon

2016

Scare0/5

Every compliment leaves the room a little colder.

Synopsis

New to Los Angeles, aspiring model Jesse quickly attracts the attention of fashion photographers and designers. Each opportunity changes how the other models look at her, until admiration and hostility become difficult to tell apart.

My thoughts

First off, I wouldn't call it horror. I can see people becoming absolutely obsessed with it. It's about women resenting each other in this beautiful world. Art lovers will love it. It went so deep I honestly had no idea what to make of it.

Cast

Elle Fanning, Jena Malone, Bella Heathcote

Trivia

Cliff Martinez, who scored Nicolas Winding Refn's Drive, supplied the music. As the camera cuts between silent faces, the electronic pulse carries on, inviting you to work out who's appraising whom.
