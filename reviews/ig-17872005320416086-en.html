Crazy, Stupid, Love.

2011

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5

Even the dating coach falls in love.

Synopsis

Facing divorce, Cal accepts dating lessons from smooth stranger Jacob. When the mentor falls for someone himself, his own playbook suddenly proves less useful.

My thoughts

So much fun! Properly crazy and refreshing. If you're bored with the usual romance, give this one a go.

Cast

Steve Carell, Ryan Gosling, Emma Stone

Trivia

Jacob's showpiece lift comes from Dirty Dancing. The joke is that his carefully practiced move works into the conversation even after he admits which movie he stole it from.
