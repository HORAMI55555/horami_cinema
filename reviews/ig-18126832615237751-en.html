Anaconda

1997

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★1/5

The camera crew becomes the quarry.

Synopsis

A film crew on the Amazon takes a stranded man aboard. His obsession with capturing a giant snake soon hijacks their entire expedition.

My thoughts

Honestly, unless you absolutely adore snakes, don't bother. The snake effects looked bad, the plot was awful, the ending made no sense and it got a little gross too. For me it was a total waste of time.

Cast

Jennifer Lopez, Ice Cube, Jon Voight

Trivia

The filmmakers set out to observe life in the jungle and become the ones being watched. Shots at water level invite you to ask whose eyes are now following the boat.
