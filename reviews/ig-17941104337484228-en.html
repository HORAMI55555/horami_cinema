One Missed Call

2003

Scare2/5

Your voicemail has a message from your future self.

Synopsis

A missed call arrives dated in the future, carrying the recipient's own voice. As the predictions come true, Yumi seeks a way to stop the next death.

My thoughts

I usually skip Japanese horror because it doesn't scare me. People said I should see this one, and it didn't scare me at all either. At least there's a story to follow, which beats a confusing one.

Cast

Ko Shibasaki, Shinichi Tsutsumi, Kazue Fukiishi

Trivia

Takashi Miike directed the Japanese original. The ordinary interface of a call log or voicemail becomes frightening through one tiny alteration: a date that hasn't happened yet.
