Howling Village

2019

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★2.5/5Scare3.5/5

They say there’s a village beyond the tunnel that no map will show you.

Synopsis

Strange events begin closing in on Kanae, a clinical psychologist. A tunnel visited by her brother, a children’s song and a supposedly vanished settlement keep surfacing. Following the trail brings a local legend uncomfortably close to her own family.

My thoughts

Finally got around to this! I kept putting it off because people around me didn’t rate it, but I didn’t think it was that bad. The real place behind the legend made it more interesting to me. It definitely delivers the horror atmosphere; I can’t imagine someone who hates scary movies sitting through it. The story was a little hard to follow and didn’t grab me as much. I’d still recommend it if you like Japanese horror.

Cast

Ayaka Miyoshi, Ryota Bando, Tsuyoshi Furukawa

Trivia

Director Takashi Shimizu starts from a real place, the old Inunaki Tunnel in Fukuoka, and a local legend about a village beyond the reach of Japanese law. It’s an unnerving combination: a road you could actually visit, and a story that says the rules might change as soon as you step off it.
