Searching

2018

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5

To find his daughter, a father opens her browser.

Synopsis

When his teenage daughter Margot vanishes, David traces her contacts through her laptop. Photos and transactions remain where she left them, but the life they describe bears little resemblance to the one he knew.

My thoughts

Wait, everyone seriously needs to see this! I couldn't predict where it was going, and it felt so satisfying afterwards. A whole movie on computer screens? Such a fresh idea and genuinely so much fun.

Cast

John Cho, Debra Messing, Michelle La

Trivia

The search unfolds through digital screens. Watch for messages typed and erased before sending: the desktop can show you something a conventional dialogue scene wouldn't, the words someone decided to keep to himself.
