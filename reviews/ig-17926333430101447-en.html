Hellbound

2021

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5Scare1/5

What if damnation came with an appointment?

Synopsis

People in Seoul receive predictions of the exact moment they will die. When monstrous figures carry them out in public, a group preaching divine judgment gains power. The condemned and their families suddenly have an audience.

My thoughts

I really liked this Korean series. Everyone sees the executions, so you understand why people start believing that the victims must have sinned. Then a baby gets a decree, and I was like, wait, what could a baby possibly have done? Watching that certainty crack was the best part for me. The first season is just six episodes, so it flies by.

Cast

Yoo Ah-in, Kim Hyun-joo, Park Jeong-min

Trivia

The webtoon was created by Train to Busan director Yeon Sang-ho and artist Choi Gyu-seok. Both wrote for the screen adaptation, with Yeon directing. During a demonstration, try watching the onlookers’ faces. Their fascination can be as unsettling as the creatures.
