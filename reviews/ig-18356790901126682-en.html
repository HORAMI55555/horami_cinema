Incident in a Ghostland

2018

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3.5/5Scare3/5

The nightmare in that house should have been over.

Synopsis

A mother and her two daughters move into a house filled with old objects and dolls, where a violent attack leaves lasting scars. Years later, Beth has become a writer when her sister Vera calls for help. Returning to the house brings her back into the shadow of the night that changed their lives.

My thoughts

This was intense… That drained feeling after finishing might be one of the strongest I’ve had. I kept thinking, this is so painful to watch, and nearly stopped partway through. If you like bleak psychological horror, definitely consider it. Honestly, it’s really compelling.

Cast

Crystal Reed · Anastasia Phillips · Emilia Jones · Taylor Hickson · Mylène Farmer

Trivia

Pascal Laugier described his starting point as filming a character’s dreamlike world as though it were reality. Rather than only showing frightening events, he built the film around subjective perception—an idea he explained in his statement for the Torino Film Festival.
