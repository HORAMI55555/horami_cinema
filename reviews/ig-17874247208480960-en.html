SILENT Hill

2006

Scare1/5

In a town blanketed by ash, a mother calls one name.

Synopsis

Rose takes her daughter to Silent Hill, a town the girl keeps naming in her sleep. After a crash, the child vanishes. As Rose searches the fog, a siren signals a much darker version of the streets.

My thoughts

I thought this was absolute garbage. I got so bored I switched it off halfway through. It just felt like a video game.

Cast

Radha Mitchell, Laurie Holden, Sean Bean

Trivia

Players might recognize Silent Hill with their eyes closed. The soundtrack draws on music by the games' composer Akira Yamaoka, so the town still sounds familiar even after the move to live action.
