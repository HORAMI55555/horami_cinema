Black Mirror

2011

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5Scare1/5

The next episode comes with a different set of rules.

Synopsis

Imagine replaying a memory whenever you like, or having strangers’ ratings determine where you can live. Each episode introduces a different world and follows an ordinary desire into the extraordinary trouble technology can make of it.

My thoughts

I forgot to post this one! The famous Black Mirror. Some episodes lean into horror, if I remember right, but mostly it’s just a really interesting watch. It reminds me of Japan’s Tales of the Unusual. Each story stands alone, so it’s easy to dip in and then keep going. Definitely recommend!

Cast

Bryce Dallas Howard, Gugu Mbatha-Raw and others (cast varies by episode)

Trivia

Creator Charlie Brooker explained that a switched-off screen looks like a black mirror. Try locking your phone after an episode. Where a moment ago you were watching someone else live with their choices, the face looking back is suddenly yours.
