Passengers

2016

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5

Awake together. Ninety years too soon.

Synopsis

On a spaceship bound for a distant colony, Jim and Aurora wake from hibernation ninety years too early. With everyone else asleep, the ship’s luxurious facilities become their entire world. Companionship offers relief from isolation, but worsening malfunctions and a hidden truth threaten the life they are building together.

My thoughts

I loved this!!!! I rarely watch this kind of film, but gave it a try and felt completely immersed—like I was actually in space. Such a fresh experience, and a world you can really disappear into. Highly recommend! Aurora is adorable too ♡

Cast

Jennifer Lawrence, Chris Pratt, Michael Sheen

Trivia

For a story set aboard an enormous spacecraft, the central cast is deliberately intimate. Alongside Jennifer Lawrence and Chris Pratt, Michael Sheen plays Arthur, an android bartender. A detail worth watching is how hotel-like comforts change meaning when there is nobody awake to share them with. A beautiful bar or spacious corridor can suggest a dream holiday—or an existence with no exit. Looking at the contrast between luxury and isolation offers another way to appreciate the ship beyond its spectacular space views.
