イット･フォローズ

2014

Scare1/5

You can outrun it. It never stops walking.

Synopsis

After a date, Jay is warned that something will follow her on foot. It can look like a stranger or someone she knows. Even a busy street offers little comfort when any approaching pedestrian could be the one.

My thoughts

A different kind of horror. Didn't scare me, though.

Cast

Maika Monroe, Keir Gilchrist, Daniel Zovatto

Trivia

Disasterpeace composed the electronic score. Try listening while you scan a wide shot for an approaching figure: the music can rush at you long before the person in the distance has crossed the frame.
