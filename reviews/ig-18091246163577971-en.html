Wicked: For Good

2025

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★5/5

Different paths. A bond that remains.

Synopsis

Elphaba is hunted as the Wicked Witch, while Glinda is celebrated as a symbol of goodness. The friends now stand on opposite sides of life in Oz, living with the consequences of their choices. Belief, loyalty and friendship are tested as their story reaches its final chapter in a world of music and magic.

My thoughts

Ari 😭 So beautiful, and the songs were all so, so good... the whole world was incredible. I’m so happy I got to see both films at the cinema. This one left me emotional long after it ended. If you love fantasy or musicals, please watch it. Such a good film.

Cast

Cynthia Erivo · Ariana Grande · Jonathan Bailey · Michelle Yeoh · Jeff Goldblum

Trivia

This is the concluding film directed by Jon M. Chu. The stage musical features music and lyrics by Stephen Schwartz and a book by Winnie Holzman. Viewing tip: revisit the first film’s friendships and choices to appreciate the emotional weight of the songs.
