FALL

2022

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5Scare2/5

600 meters up. No way down.

Synopsis

After losing someone she loves in a climbing accident, Becky has struggled to move forward. Her best friend Hunter persuades her to climb an abandoned tower rising roughly 600 meters above the desert. Reaching the top should feel like a victory—until the ladder collapses and their way down disappears. With little equipment and only each other to rely on, they must find a way back to the ground. The premise is simple. The feeling of being trapped in open air is anything but.

My thoughts

Such a simple setup, yet I was on edge the whole time. Five days later, I was still thinking about it. A low scare rating does NOT mean an easy watch if you’re afraid of heights.

Cast

Grace Caroline Currey · Virginia Gardner · Jeffrey Dean Morgan · Mason Gooding

Trivia

How did they capture that height? The crew built tower sections on a mountain cliff edge, capturing real wind and light. Visual effects helped with shots looking straight down. That mix of a physical location and effects gives the danger its tangible feel.
