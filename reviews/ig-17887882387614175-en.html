Orphan

2009

Scare2.5/5

The newest member of the family knows how to keep a secret.

Synopsis

After losing a child, Kate and John adopt Esther, a poised young artist they meet at an orphanage. A series of disturbing accidents leaves Kate voicing doubts the rest of the family finds increasingly hard to share.

My thoughts

Esther! I've seen this at least four times. I can't get enough of the psychological menace. It isn't about big horror scares so much as how frightening a person can be. An easy, wholehearted recommendation.

Cast

Isabelle Fuhrman, Vera Farmiga, Peter Sarsgaard

Trivia

Isabelle Fuhrman was herself a child actor when she played Esther. Listen to the change from careful courtesy with adults to clipped commands among children; different family members effectively encounter different Esthers.
