Red Riding Hood

2011

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★2/5

Someone in the village knows that voice.

Synopsis

In a village terrorized by a werewolf, Valerie faces an unwanted marriage. When suspicion turns inward, even the man she loves becomes hard to trust.

My thoughts

I watched this ages ago! A grown-up Little Red Riding Hood is such an intriguing idea. The plot takes some concentration, but I enjoyed being in that world. Skip it if you want to switch your brain off.

Cast

Amanda Seyfried, Gary Oldman, Shiloh Fernandez

Trivia

Twilight director Catherine Hardwicke blends a woodland romance with a village whodunit. The same red cloak threads its way through both stories.
