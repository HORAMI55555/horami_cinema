It's her house. Why can't she ask them to leave?

Synopsis

A woman restores the country house she shares with her husband. As he welcomes one stranger after another, she begins losing her place inside the home she built.

My thoughts

I expected it to be upsetting, but THIS upsetting?! I was furious the whole time and felt terrible for her. Reading an explanation afterward made me appreciate how carefully it was put together. If religious symbolism interests you, try it. It annoyed me, but I've never seen anything quite like it.

Cast

Jennifer Lawrence, Javier Bardem, Michelle Pfeiffer

Trivia

Characters are credited by roles such as mother and man rather than ordinary names. A visitor's behavior reads quite differently as a family dispute and as part of an allegory.
