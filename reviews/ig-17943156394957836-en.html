The Roommate

2011

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★2/5Scare2/5

The person you want to avoid already has a key.

Synopsis

New to Los Angeles, college freshman Sara strikes up a friendship with Rebecca, her dorm roommate. Having a familiar face on campus feels like a gift. Then Sara starts dating and making other friends, and Rebecca’s attention begins to feel stifling.

My thoughts

I had a pretty wild roommate when I studied abroad, so parts of this hit close to home, although this takes it WAY further. It felt more like a psychological thriller than horror, so I could watch it just fine. I liked getting that study-abroad feeling all over again.

Cast

Minka Kelly, Leighton Meester, Cam Gigandet

Trivia

Rebecca is played by Leighton Meester, best known as Blair on Gossip Girl. She told Parade how strange it felt to be the source of a film’s scares. Casting such a familiar smile is a neat way of making us share Sara’s dilemma: when would you stop trusting it?
