The Prom

2020

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5

She wants one dance with her girlfriend. The whole town weighs in.

Synopsis

When Emma wants to take her girlfriend to prom, the school cancels the dance. Broadway performers descend on her Indiana town to help, though rescuing their public image is also rather high on their agenda.

My thoughts

This was everywhere when I watched it! It's so good and leaves you with such a lift. So that's what prom can feel like!

Cast

Jo Ellen Pellman, Ariana DeBose, Meryl Streep

Trivia

The film adapts the Broadway musical of the same name. The visiting performers get elaborate numbers about their generosity, while the teenagers at the centre of the commotion just want a dance together.
