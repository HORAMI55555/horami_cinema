The Suicide Squad

2021

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5

These are the people saving the world?

Synopsis

Imprisoned supervillains are recruited into the secret Task Force X for a mission that may get them killed. Harley Quinn, Bloodsport and their unpredictable teammates must contend with both dangerous enemies and each other. Trust is scarce; getting home alive is far from guaranteed.

My thoughts

I found out there was another film connected to The Suicide Squad, which I love, so of course I watched it straight away ‼️ Harley Quinn and some other familiar faces are back, and the fun and structure reminded me of the earlier film. Simply entertaining, Harley is adorable, and there’s quite a bit more gore this time. Apart from thinking “isn’t that a lot of gore?”, I’d recommend it for a group movie night!

Cast

Margot Robbie · Idris Elba · John Cena · Joel Kinnaman · Viola Davis

Trivia

This 2021 film was written and directed by James Gunn. Alongside Margot Robbie, the cast includes Joel Kinnaman, Jai Courtney and Viola Davis.
