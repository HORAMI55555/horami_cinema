Happy Death Day

2017

Scare1/5

Her birthday morning keeps coming back. So does her killer.

Synopsis

College student Tree is murdered on her birthday and wakes up that same morning. Repeated classes, conversations and parties give her a succession of chances to identify the person behind the mask.

My thoughts

I really love this one. So much fun! I didn't find it scary, so I'd recommend it to everyone. You don't come across this particular mix very often.

Cast

Jessica Rothe, Israel Broussard, Ruby Modine

Trivia

The killer's baby-face mask doubles as the university mascot. It's exactly the sort of thing anyone might wear on campus, so getting a good look at the killer still doesn't tell Tree who it is.
