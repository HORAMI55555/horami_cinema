Synopsis

Five years after high school, Rue and her friends are navigating very different paths into adulthood. A new setting does not erase old choices or old wounds. Love, desire, belief and belonging take on new meanings as they confront the lives they are building. What will they hold on to, and what will they leave behind? The series turns its gaze beyond adolescence, following the complicated lives that come after it.

My thoughts

I looked forward to this every week. This season felt more grown-up and emotionally layered. Zendaya and Sydney are incredible. Personally, my favorite series in years.
