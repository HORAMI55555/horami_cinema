Eli

2019

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5Scare2.5/5

When even the air outside makes you sick, where can you run?

Synopsis

Eli, a boy who becomes violently ill when exposed to the outside air, arrives with his parents at a sealed medical facility inside an old house. He can finally take off his protective suit. When he sees things in windows and corridors, his doctor blames the treatment, but Eli begins to suspect a different explanation.

My thoughts

I had such a good time with this. The scares are moderate, so even if horror isn’t usually your thing, I think you could brave it. This one really hit for me. The payoff at the end felt SO satisfying. Netflix people, give it a watch!

Cast

Charlie Shotwell, Kelly Reilly, Lili Taylor

Trivia

Director Ciarán Foy deliberately avoided the smoke and shafts of light associated with haunted houses. Outside air is what frightens Eli. The cleaner the interior looks, the easier it is to share his relief at finding somewhere he can finally breathe.
