Insidious

2010

Scare4/5

They can move house. Their son still won't wake up.

Synopsis

Dalton falls into an unexplained coma soon after his family moves. Disturbances follow them even to a different home, forcing his parents to reconsider whether the place they live is really what is haunted.

My thoughts

I really recommend this. Proper horror with a story I love, without being unbearably scary for me. Top three horror films, easily! I remember there being a few in the series and want you to watch them all. If horror isn't your thing, though, this one might finish you off!

Cast

Patrick Wilson, Rose Byrne, Lin Shaye

Trivia

James Wan and screenwriter Leigh Whannell had teamed up on Saw. Whannell also plays Specs here: the writer who devised the haunting gets to walk into it carrying investigative equipment.
