Cinderella

2021

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5

A prince is nice. What she wants is her own shop.

Synopsis

Ella wants to sell the clothes she designs. The royal ball offers a chance to meet a prince, but also the customers who might change her working life.

My thoughts

Cinderella is my absolute favorite Disney princess, and I love Camila, so I watched the second this came to Amazon. Brilliant music, that fun live-action Aladdin energy, and a sense of humor too. Loved it!

Cast

Camila Cabello, Nicholas Galitzine, Billy Porter

Trivia

This 2021 musical gives familiar pop songs to its fairy-tale characters. Its heroine sees the ball from both sides of the dress: as the person wearing it and as the designer seeking a client.
