Something is waiting to be born beneath the prayers.

Synopsis

Margaret, a young American preparing for a religious life, arrives in Rome to work at an orphanage. Disturbing events surrounding a girl begin to unsettle her. As she uncovers signs of a sinister plan, the institution that was supposed to provide certainty becomes the source of her deepest doubts.

My thoughts

I happened to find the newest Omen film on a flight. The atmosphere was incredible—religious horror set in Rome really has something! The scares were moderate and the story a little familiar, but I still enjoyed it. A good pick if religious horror is your thing.

Cast

Nell Tiger Free, Ralph Ineson, Bill Nighy

Trivia

Arkasha Stevenson directs this prequel to the 1976 film, with the official credits acknowledging David Seltzer’s original characters. The perspective is an interesting part of the approach: a young woman’s experience leads into a horror story many viewers already know. Consider whether the imposing buildings and rituals feel reassuring or overpowering. That question can change how Rome itself appears, while viewers familiar with the earlier film can look for connections without needing the new story’s outcome explained in advance.
