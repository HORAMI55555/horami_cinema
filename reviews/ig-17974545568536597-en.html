The Green Inferno

2013

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3.5/5Scare5/5

Their protest is over. Their flight home never makes it.

Synopsis

College student Justine joins a group of activists protesting deforestation in the Peruvian Amazon. On the way out, their plane crashes into the rainforest. The survivors have no bearings and no way to call for help when they’re taken captive.

My thoughts

I finally worked up the nerve to watch this after hearing how notoriously gory it was. The setup really hooked me: students go to Peru to protect a community and wind up in the hands of cannibals. The gore was brutal enough that I spent half the film covering my eyes, but I was still invested in the story. If gore gets to you, take a friend and peek when it’s safe.

Cast

Lorenza Izzo, Ariel Levy, Daryl Sabara

Trivia

The village scenes were filmed in the Peruvian Amazon with local people taking part as performers. Actor Daryl Sabara recalled that Eli Roth talked residents through what a movie was when he scouted the location. That encounter sits behind the fictional village we see on screen.
