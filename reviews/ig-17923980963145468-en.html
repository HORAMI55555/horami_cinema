The Silence of the Lambs

1991

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5Scare1/5

To catch a killer, enter another killer’s mind.

Synopsis

FBI trainee Clarice Starling seeks help with a serial murder investigation from imprisoned psychiatrist Hannibal Lecter. Brilliant and dangerous, Lecter is a killer himself. As she searches for clues, he probes her private fears and memories. The urgency of the investigation runs alongside an unsettling battle of minds.

My thoughts

An older film, but such a timeless classic. The psychological game between Lecter and Clarice feels so convincing and gives it something special. A fantastic psychological thriller, full of suspense. There’s real substance and depth to the story too—definitely worth watching!

Cast

Jodie Foster · Anthony Hopkins

Trivia

At the 1992 Academy Awards, the film won Best Picture, Directing, Actor, Actress and Adapted Screenplay. Its acclaim extends beyond the crime story to the performances and the exchanges between Clarice and Lecter.
