Mean Girls

2004

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5

The pinkest clique has the sharpest rules.

Synopsis

Raised and homeschooled in Africa, Cady enters an American high school for the first time. Queen bee Regina welcomes her into the popular clique, where everything from clothes to dating comes with unwritten rules. As Cady investigates the group, she becomes caught up in its struggle for social power.

My thoughts

I watched this because it was also one of the themes in Ari’s “thank u, next” video. It’s a slightly older American teen high-school movie, and it was cute. Classic mean girls and a nonstop gossip-fest 😂 It was fun!

Cast

Lindsay Lohan, Rachel McAdams, Amanda Seyfried, Tina Fey

Trivia

Tina Fey wrote the sharp dialogue and also plays Ms. Norbury, while Mark Waters directed. Rosalind Wiseman’s book about girls’ social relationships provided the inspiration. School cliques and gossip become a comedy driven by larger-than-life personalities. The 2004 movie later inspired a Broadway musical, giving its distinctive characters and memorable language another stage on which to live.
