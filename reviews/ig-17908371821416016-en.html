Surviving Death

2021

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★2/5Scare1/5

What do you remember after you wake up?

Synopsis

People describe near-death experiences, families search for signs from loved ones, and children recall what they believe are past lives. Across these accounts, the documentary asks what, if anything, survives the end of a life.

My thoughts

If you’re genuinely interested in the afterlife or near-death experiences, this documentary really digs into that stuff. If you’re not, I can see it being a pretty boring watch, haha. It takes those questions seriously the whole way through.

Cast

Leslie Kean, Mary Neal and other interviewees

Trivia

The starting point was journalist Leslie Kean’s book of the same name. Director Ricki Stern devotes two of the six episodes to mediums. Listen for the difference between someone trying to explain their own experience and someone hoping for an answer from a person they miss.
