HOSTEL

2005

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5Scare3/5

The backpackers think they're the customers.

Synopsis

Backpackers change their itinerary after hearing about a hostel in Slovakia. Then one of them disappears after a night out. Someone else has paid for an experience there, too.

My thoughts

Another one I saw a while back. I've seen a few with a similar setup, so it didn't feel especially new, but the idea that people could do this to each other still scares me. It's an older film, and it gave me more fear and suspense than quite a few recent horror movies that just left me confused.

Cast

Jay Hernandez, Derek Richardson, Barbara Nedeljáková

Trivia

The hostel is in Slovakia in the story, but production crossed the border into the Czech Republic. The Czech Film Commission also documented Eli Roth's return to shoot the sequel there.
