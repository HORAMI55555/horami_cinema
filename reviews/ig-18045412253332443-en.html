Terror Tuesday: Extreme

2024

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5Scare3/5

Someone’s story becomes your nightmare.

Synopsis

This eight-part Thai horror anthology draws on stories shared with a radio programme. Each episode introduces new characters and unsettling situations involving families, relationships and familiar spaces. Small disturbances in ordinary life gradually turn into nightmares, making every story a fresh entry point into the series.

My thoughts

A pretty good Asian horror recommendation! Thai and Vietnamese horror can have such an eerie atmosphere, and this gives you that kind of experience in a series. Every episode is different, which is satisfying. Not overwhelmingly terrifying for me, but the fear creeps up on you. Recommended if you like Asian horror—and probably scary for less seasoned viewers!

Cast

Cherprang Areekul, Praewa Suthamphong, Duangporn Sonthikhun and an ensemble cast varying by episode

Trivia

The starting point is the Thai horror radio programme Angkhan Khlumpong. Netflix’s announcement describes listener accounts being reworked for an intensified screen experience. That translation from a story heard to a story seen is an interesting part of the appeal. Each episode resets the characters and setting, making the anthology approachable without a long commitment. Listen for sounds and pauses before anything visibly supernatural happens: that is often where an ordinary room starts to feel threatening.
