ゴーストバスターズ

1984

Scare0/5

In New York, there's a business that bills by the ghost.

Synopsis

Dismissed from their university, three scientists set up a ghost-removal service in an old firehouse. The calls seem like separate jobs until trouble in one apartment points to something much bigger happening across New York.

My thoughts

I wouldn't call it horror. It's fun! Even if you don't do scary movies, you'll be fine with this one.

Cast

Bill Murray, Dan Aykroyd, Harold Ramis, Ernie Hudson

Trivia

Dan Aykroyd and Harold Ramis, who play Ray and Egon, also wrote the screenplay together. The men explaining the equipment with straight faces are the very people who dreamed up this peculiar line of work.
