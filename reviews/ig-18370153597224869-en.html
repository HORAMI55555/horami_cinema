House of Sayuri

2024

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5Scare3/5

This haunted house has other plans.

Synopsis

The Kamiki family moves into the house they have always wanted, only for disturbing events to turn their new life upside down. A classmate warns Norio to leave, but the danger has already reached his family. Their encounter with the girl haunting the house takes the story in an unexpected direction.

My thoughts

At first glance, you’d think this was a Japanese ghost film in the Ju-On lane... and at the beginning, it is. Then halfway through I was like, wait... what?! My brain couldn’t keep up. Horror, comedy and sadness all mixed into something completely different. What even is this film, it’s so unhinged lol. If you struggle with horror, I think getting through the scary opening might make the rest manageable—maybe it could even help you get a little more comfortable with the genre. Such a surprise.

Cast

Ryoka Minamide · Toshie Negishi · Hana Kondo · Zen Kajiwara

Trivia

Koji Shiraishi adapts Rensuke Oshikiri’s manga. This was Ryoka Minamide’s first horror film. Viewing tip: avoid detailed plot summaries and let the changes in tone surprise you.
