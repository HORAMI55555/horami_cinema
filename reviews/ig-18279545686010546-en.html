Mamma Mia!

2008

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5

Three wedding invitations. One of them is probably going to Dad.

Synopsis

About to marry on a Greek island, Sophie finds three possible fathers in her mother Donna’s old diary. Certain she’ll recognize her dad in person, she secretly invites all three to the wedding. Donna’s hotel is about to host quite a reunion.

My thoughts

I forgot to post this one! I saw it ages ago and still remember it so clearly. I loved the story, it made me cry, and I came out of it feeling ridiculously refreshed. That afterglow stayed with me.

Cast

Meryl Streep, Amanda Seyfried, Pierce Brosnan

Trivia

Before she played Donna, Meryl Streep was in the audience. She saw the Broadway show in 2001 and wrote the cast a note about how much she loved it. Picture someone applauding from those seats, then picture her a few years later getting to sing Dancing Queen herself.
