Hush

2016

Scare3/5

He can see her. She hasn't seen him yet.

Synopsis

Maddie, a deaf writer, lives alone in the woods. An intruder at her window realizes she can't hear him. With help out of reach, she has to turn her knowledge of the house against him.

My thoughts

That does it, I don't want to live alone! So scary. It didn't feel like horror to me, but I was on edge the whole time. Pretty good, I'd say.

Cast

Kate Siegel, John Gallagher Jr.

Trivia

Kate Siegel, who plays Maddie, wrote the script with director Mike Flanagan. The actor playing the target also helped decide when the audience should notice something her character hasn't.
