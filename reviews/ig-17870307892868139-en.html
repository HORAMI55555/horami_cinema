レオン

1994

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5

For once, someone is waiting for the hitman to come home.

Synopsis

Léon, a solitary New York hitman, shelters his young neighbour Mathilda after her family is murdered. She wants him to teach her his trade so she can take revenge. Sharing his apartment is only the beginning of the disruption.

My thoughts

A non-horror pick and a personal favorite. Please watch it! It's so good, and the kind of love it shows felt different to me.

Cast

Jean Reno, Natalie Portman, Gary Oldman

Trivia

Mathilda was Natalie Portman's feature-film debut. In scenes where Jean Reno barely moves his face, watch how often the newcomer beside him shifts her gaze or changes her tone to get through to him.
