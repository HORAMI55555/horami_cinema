Hereditary

2018

Scare3/5

Grandma is gone. Her presence fills the house.

Synopsis

After her mother dies, Annie Graham begins to reckon with what she left behind. Her husband and children live under the same roof, but each seems to be mourning in a different house.

My thoughts

I’d wanted to see this forever because it’s such a horror staple. I finally caught it on Netflix and the gore absolutely finished me. Easier to follow than Midsommar, a bit gorier and scarier too. So strange and unsettling. If gore is a deal-breaker, seriously, skip it.

Cast

Toni Collette, Alex Wolff

Trivia

Ari Aster made his feature directing debut here. Look at where Annie places people in her miniatures. When the camera returns to the real house, you may find yourself looking into its rooms in exactly the same way.
