The Animal Kingdom

2023

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★1.5/5Scare1/5

When bodies change, what makes us human?

Synopsis

An unexplained phenomenon is transforming people into animal-like beings. François and his son Émile try to stay close to his affected wife, but an accident during her transfer leaves her missing. As their family life becomes increasingly uncertain, father and son must reconsider what it means to understand and accept someone who is changing.

My thoughts

Oh, so this is about people gradually turning into animals and struggling with it! It reminded me of a sadder version of Wednesday, where the abilities can no longer be controlled. Easy enough to watch without overthinking, but it didn’t leave much of an impression on me.

Cast

Romain Duris · Paul Kircher · Adèle Exarchopoulos

Trivia

Thomas Cailley’s second feature opened Un Certain Regard at Cannes in 2023. Viewing tip: notice the difference between seeing someone as a threatening creature and seeing them as family.
