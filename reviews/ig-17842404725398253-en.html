Scare2/5

The masked collector doesn't collect things. He collects stories.

Synopsis

A masked figure introduces stories in which a wish or a useful piece of technology behaves in unexpected ways. Each episode gives a familiar corner of childhood its own unsettling rule.

My thoughts

I seriously couldn't stop watching. It's that good. Absolute gold. There aren't many really scary bits, so pretty much anyone can give it a go.

Cast

Victoria Diamond, William Romain, Sydney Wade

Trivia

The children change from story to story, but the masked collector called the Curious connects the anthology. His appetite for another strange tale mirrors the viewer reaching for just one more episode.
