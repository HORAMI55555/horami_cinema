Greenland

2020

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★5/5

Imagine losing sight of your family at the end of the world.

Synopsis

As fragments of a comet begin hitting Earth, structural engineer John receives an evacuation alert. He heads for the designated airfield with his wife and young son. His neighbors are watching the same sky, but their phones never rang.

My thoughts

I was still thinking about this long after it ended. The family is trying to reach a shelter before the impacts wipe everything out, and it felt so real I could imagine waking up to it tomorrow. I was on edge the whole way. All I could think about was what happened to the dinosaurs. This was my drop-everything-and-watch-it Netflix pick.

Cast

Gerard Butler, Morena Baccarin, Roger Dale Floyd

Trivia

Director Ric Roman Waugh consulted scientists at NASA’s Jet Propulsion Laboratory about what happens when a comet enters the atmosphere. Watch the family watching the sky: part of the dread is the gap between seeing something extraordinary and realizing you’re in danger.
