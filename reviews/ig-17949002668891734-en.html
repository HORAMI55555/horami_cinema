Tomorrow, I’ll Be Someone’s Girlfriend

2022

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5

Five women share a city. What each wants from it is different.

Synopsis

University student Yuki works as a rental girlfriend. The story moves from the dates she stages for her clients into the lives of women navigating loneliness, money, appearance and desire. When Moe steps inside a host club, a world she once thought had nothing to do with her starts pulling her in.

My thoughts

A little detour from my usual picks, but I love this one. I read the manga first, so the drama felt pretty faithful. I just really like these stories, especially the one about getting hooked on host clubs. Each storyline goes deeper than you expect and gives you plenty to sit with.

Cast

Ai Yoshikawa, Yumena Yanai, Nagisa Saito

Trivia

Hinao Wono’s manga was serialized on the Japanese manga app Cycomi. The title says “I”, but the identity of that “I” keeps shifting. When Moe or Yua comes into focus, the same nightlife district takes on a different meaning. For manga readers, part of the pleasure is recognizing the moment their favorite character’s chapter begins.
