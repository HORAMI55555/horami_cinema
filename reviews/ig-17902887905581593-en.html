Don’t Breathe 2

2021

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5Scare2/5

Could you root for the man you once wanted to escape?

Synopsis

Blind veteran Norman lives in isolation with a girl named Phoenix, teaching her to sense danger and protect herself. One night, armed intruders enter their home, and the skills he has taught her suddenly become all too necessary.

My thoughts

I was SO excited about this. Don’t Breathe is in my horror top three, and I never expected a sequel. At first I thought, wait, this is where they’re taking it? Then it clicked. The old man’s role is so different this time that I actually found myself rooting for him. I even cried at the end. Highly recommend.

Cast

Stephen Lang, Madelyn Grace, Brendan Sexton III

Trivia

As Phoenix hides from the intruders, the camera follows their movements through the house in a long take. Cinematographer Pedro Luque wanted viewers to absorb the layout almost unconsciously. Once you know which rooms connect, even an empty passage can make you hold your breath.
