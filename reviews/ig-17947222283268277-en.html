Wednesday

2022

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5

A colorful roommate. A grisly mystery. Guess which one has her attention.

Synopsis

Wednesday Addams arrives at Nevermore Academy, her parents’ old school. Dorm life holds little appeal, but a string of killings in the nearby town is another matter. As strange visions interrupt her days, her investigation starts to brush up against her own family history.

My thoughts

I loved seeing the Addams daughter get her own Netflix series. She starts off so cold that watching her slowly figure out other people’s feelings was my favorite part. The whole school gave me Harry Potter vibes. It flew by, too, so it’s an easy recommendation. Now I want to revisit The Addams Family! Magic, mystery and teen drama all in one.

Cast

Jenna Ortega, Emma Myers, Victor Dorobantu

Trivia

Thing had a scene partner’s timing because there really was a performer there. Victor Dorobantu wore a blue suit so the effects team could remove everything but his hand. The crew even put him on a dolly when Thing had to move fast. Watch the tiny pause before his fingers answer Wednesday.
