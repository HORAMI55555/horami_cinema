Her next patient is centuries away.

Synopsis

Goryeo warrior Choi Young travels to present-day Seoul to find a doctor for the wounded queen. Eun-soo expects to go home after treating her patient, but court politics get in the way.

My thoughts

I forgot this one in my Lee Min-ho roundup! His character lives centuries in the past and brings a modern Korean doctor back with him. Just as he tries to send her home, other people get in the way. A lot happens while she waits for another chance.

Cast

Lee Min-ho, Kim Hee-sun

Trivia

The series is also known as The Great Doctor. Keep that second title in mind when Eun-soo works: a procedure that looks routine in a modern hospital can look miraculous to the people gathered around her in Goryeo.
