Zombieland

2009

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★2/5

Survival starts with a very particular rulebook.

Synopsis

A young survivor crosses zombie-filled America with a private rulebook. A trigger-happy traveler and two resourceful sisters disrupt his plan to go it alone.

My thoughts

Zombies aren't my thing, so I was bored, though zombie fans might love it. The comedy did get laughs out of me. I just thought the plot was rubbish and wished they'd tried harder to come up with something else. Oh well.

Cast

Jesse Eisenberg, Woody Harrelson, Emma Stone

Trivia

Rules appear as text inside the frame. The words interact with the action, turning what could have been straightforward exposition into another running visual joke.
