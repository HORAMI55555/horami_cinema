They left the doll behind. It got there anyway.

Synopsis

Expectant mother Mia receives an antique doll from her husband. After a violent intrusion at home, strange events begin. Moving should give the couple a fresh start, but it doesn't put the doll behind them.

My thoughts

One of those horror movies everyone knows! It's tied to The Conjuring, and the doll comes up again, so I'd recommend seeing it. It's fun, very much horror, and the ending even moved me a little.

Cast

Annabelle Wallis, Ward Horton, Alfre Woodard

Trivia

Director John R. Leonetti had been the cinematographer on The Conjuring, where this doll appeared earlier. The person who helped decide how we first saw her went on to direct an entire feature around her presence.
