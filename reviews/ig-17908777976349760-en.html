Can you put the camera down and leave?

Synopsis

Videographer Aaron accepts a one-day job at a secluded house. Each new request from his client makes the line between a job and an intrusion harder to see.

My thoughts

I watched because it ranked high on a Netflix horror list last year. It didn't scare me, it felt familiar, and I didn't like the way it ended. An easy skip for me.

Cast

Patrick Brice, Mark Duplass

Trivia

Director Patrick Brice also plays Aaron, the man behind the camera. His awkward silence on our side of the lens is as much a performance as the client's behavior on screen.
