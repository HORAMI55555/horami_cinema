The boss from hell wants your blood.

Synopsis

After centuries of finding victims and obeying Dracula’s every command, Renfield wants a life of his own. In the modern world, he begins looking for a way out of their toxic relationship. But leaving an immortal, controlling boss is easier said than done. Vampire mythology meets a very messy breakup in this action comedy.

My thoughts

This was fun!! It somehow had a bit of a Disney feel to me, and the pace kept things entertaining. I wasn’t interested at first and went in with low expectations, but it turned out better than I thought! A really solid pick for watching with friends.

Cast

Nicholas Hoult · Nicolas Cage · Awkwafina · Ben Schwartz

Trivia

Nicholas Hoult plays Renfield opposite Nicolas Cage as Dracula. Viewing tip: watch it as a story about leaving a controlling relationship as well as a vampire movie. That perspective brings their power struggle into focus.
