What if your reading becomes your fate?

Synopsis

A group of friends uses someone else's tarot deck for an innocent-looking reading, unknowingly releasing an evil trapped within the cards. When events begin to mirror the predictions, they must decipher what the readings mean before their own turns arrive. Can they change a future that seems to have already been written?

My thoughts

A pretty familiar horror setup, but I still had fun! If you struggle with horror, this is probably too much for you. If you're already a horror fan and just want something entertaining to put on, it's a perfectly decent choice.

Cast

Harriet Slater · Adain Bradley · Avantika · Jacob Batalon

Trivia

Spenser Cohen and Anna Halberg share the writing and directing credits. Viewing tip: pay attention to the wording of the initial readings, then watch how later scenes connect back to those predictions.
