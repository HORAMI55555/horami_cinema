Before the spotlight came the work.

Synopsis

Young performers from around the world train for a place in a global girl group created by HYBE and Geffen Records. Singing and dancing are only part of the challenge: constant evaluation tests their confidence, friendships and determination. This documentary series follows the work and pressure behind KATSEYE's formation.

My thoughts

I watched this ages ago!! It follows the days leading up to KATSEYE's debut, and as someone who loves audition shows, I had a great time. If you enjoy K-pop competitions, you'll probably like it too. Seeing that training system in an American setting felt fresh. Everyone was so cute and so determined!

Cast

Sophia · Manon · Daniela · Lara · Megan · Yoonchae and fellow trainees, appearing as themselves

Trivia

The series goes beyond what audiences saw in The Debut: Dream Academy, following the process behind the public competition. Director Nadia Hallgren also made Becoming, the documentary following Michelle Obama.
