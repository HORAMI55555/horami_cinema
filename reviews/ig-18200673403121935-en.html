Synopsis

Deep in debt, Gi-hun enters a game promising an enormous cash prize. Childhood playground rules are familiar, but the cost of losing isn't.

My thoughts

Piyo wanted to see it, so we watched, and I ended up absolutely sobbing. Especially the old man's scenes and Sae-byeok's. Some parts felt familiar, but I really enjoyed it!

Cast

Lee Jung-jae, Jung Ho-yeon, O Yeong-su

Trivia

The title comes from a Korean playground game named for its squid-shaped playing field. The opening explanation of its rules takes on a different weight later.
