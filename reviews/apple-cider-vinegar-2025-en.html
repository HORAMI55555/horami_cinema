A lie with real consequences.

Synopsis

Belle Gibson builds an online following around an extraordinary claim: that food and natural remedies helped her overcome terminal brain cancer. As people search for health and hope, her compelling posts grow into a wellness business. But a devastating lie sits at the heart of that success. This six-part drama explores the gap between the need to believe and the desire to be believed—and how words on a screen can affect real lives. Inspired by real events, it also fictionalizes characters and events.

My thoughts

I finished this with so many feelings I couldn’t put into words. Knowing it was inspired by real events made it hit even harder. Only six episodes. Please give it a watch.

Cast

Kaitlyn Dever · Alycia Debnam-Carey · Aisha Dee · Tilda Cobham-Hervey · Ashley Zukerman

Trivia

Filmed on location around Melbourne, the series stars American actor Kaitlyn Dever as an Australian influencer. Dever worked with a dialect coach over Zoom three times a week to develop the accent—a small behind-the-scenes detail that makes her vocal performance worth noticing.
