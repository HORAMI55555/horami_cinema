American Horror Story: Murder House

2011

Scare2/5

Here, former residents aren't altogether former.

Synopsis

The Harmons move into a Los Angeles mansion hoping to repair their family. It becomes both a home and a therapy practice, but strange visitors start blurring the line between the current occupants and people from the house's past.

My thoughts

This is so good. One of my favourite American Horror Story seasons! Not all that scary, but I got completely hooked. Twelve episodes, and I can't recommend it enough.

Cast

Connie Britton, Dylan McDermott, Jessica Lange

Trivia

The anthology began with this season. Returning to the same rooms in different decades, with different people in them, gives the house a longer memory than any single family could have.
