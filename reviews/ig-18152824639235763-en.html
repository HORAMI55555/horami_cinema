Wounds

2019

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★1.5/5Scare1.5/5

The real mistake was getting into the phone.

Synopsis

After a fight at a New Orleans bar, a group of students leaves a phone behind. Will, the bartender, takes it home. Then a stranger texts. Looking through the photos for a clue to the owner, he finds images he wishes he could unsee.

My thoughts

The start really had me. A lost phone full of gruesome photos, messages from a stranger… I was completely on board. Then the ending ruined it for me. I couldn’t make sense of the whole cult angle. Also, if bugs are a dealbreaker for you, consider yourself warned.

Cast

Armie Hammer, Dakota Johnson, Zazie Beetz

Trivia

Nathan Ballingrud’s novella is called The Visible Filth. The film changes that title to Wounds. Keep both in mind as Will goes from seeing the aftermath of a bar fight up close to peering at pictures on a screen. What counts as getting your own hands dirty?
