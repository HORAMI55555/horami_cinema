I Am Legend

2007

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5

Same time, same broadcast. Will anyone answer today?

Synopsis

In a New York emptied by an outbreak, scientist Robert Neville roams the streets with his dog Sam, broadcasting a message to anyone still alive. Daylight gives him a chance to search for a cure. After dark, the streets belong to the infected.

My thoughts

This felt more like a zombie movie to me. It reminded me of The Mist and A Quiet Place, where the world suddenly changes and everyone has to figure out how to survive. There was so much sadness in this one. The ending came and went a bit fast for me, but overall I liked it!

Cast

Will Smith, Alice Braga, Charlie Tahan

Trivia

If the ending left you thinking “Wait, that’s it?”, there’s another one to watch. Warner Bros. released an alternate ending. Watch Neville looking through the lab glass. It’s the same room with the same people, yet the question of whom that glass protects takes on a different meaning. Save the source clip for after the film.
