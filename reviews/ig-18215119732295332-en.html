He survived the crash. Can he escape the hospital?

Synopsis

After a near-fatal car crash, Sam wakes in an eerily quiet hospital. The people he encounters offer little reassurance, and something sinister seems determined to keep him inside. Searching for an exit only draws him deeper into the mystery of the building and those trapped within it.

My thoughts

This felt like a slightly fresher kind of horror. That idea of drifting between life and death plays out inside a hospital. It kept me wondering where things were going, and there were proper horror moments too. The ending made me sad, though—I would have preferred a different one, but oh well.

Cast

Jonathan Rhys Meyers · Rachelle Goulding · Elyse Levesque

Trivia

Director Michael Winnick cited images from Jacob’s Ladder that had stayed with him for years, alongside an undercurrent of Dante’s Inferno. The hospital nightmare brings together psychological horror and the idea of a journey through an otherworldly realm.
