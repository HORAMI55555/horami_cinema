The Human Centipede (First Sequence)

2009

The doctor who offers shelter has already planned the experiment.

Synopsis

Stranded by a flat tyre in Germany, two travellers ask for help at an isolated house. Their host, surgeon Josef Heiter, has another captive waiting and a very specific operation in mind.

My thoughts

Oh right, I watched this with a friend after a recommendation. The gore absolutely finished me and all I felt was regret. If gore doesn't bother you, you might find it interesting. Gore level: four out of five.

Cast

Dieter Laser, Akihiro Kitamura, Ashley C. Williams

Trivia

Akihiro Kitamura plays Katsuro, the Japanese captive. Japanese and English are heard among the three prisoners; even before the operation, they have limited means of communicating about a danger they all share.
