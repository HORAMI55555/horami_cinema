In the Tall Grass

2019

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5Scare1.5/5

The voice sounds close. Why can’t they reach it?

Synopsis

Driving through Kansas, Becky and her brother Cal hear a boy calling for help. Within minutes of stepping into grass taller than they are, they lose sight of each other and the road. All they have to follow are voices, and even the distance between those voices seems to shift.

My thoughts

My hairdresser recommended this yesterday when we got talking about horror. It’s a Stephen King story, so of course it gets pretty wild. Some bits were hard to wrap my head around, but the setup is so simple: a kid calls for help, you walk into the grass, and now you can’t get out. I had fun with it and didn’t find it especially scary. It reminded me a bit of Midsommar, though I found this one easier to follow.

Cast

Laysla De Oliveira, Avery Whitted, Patrick Wilson

Trivia

Stephen King wrote the story with his son Joe Hill for Esquire. The filmmaker who adapted it was Vincenzo Natali, director of Cube. This time his maze has no numbered rooms or doors to keep track of. Just try finding a landmark in all that green.
