The Prom

2020

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5

She already knows who she wants to dance with.

Synopsis

Emma just wants to take her girlfriend to prom in Indiana. When that sets off a local uproar, Broadway actors in need of good publicity descend on the town to help.

My thoughts

I caught this on Netflix and I’d especially recommend it if stories about gender and identity are your thing. Seeing these characters live exactly as they want to is so cool. Loved the songs, too!

Cast

Jo Ellen Pellman, Ariana DeBose, Meryl Streep

Trivia

The source is a Tony-nominated Broadway musical: stage actors trying to take centre stage in a small town were first played on an actual stage. Listen to the difference between their big entrances and Emma’s quieter solo moments.
