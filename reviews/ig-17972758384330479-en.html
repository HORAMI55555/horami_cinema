Ready or Not

2019

Scare2/5

On her wedding night, the in-laws insist on hide-and-seek.

Synopsis

Newly married Grace draws a card for her wealthy in-laws' traditional wedding game. Hide-and-seek sounds easy enough, until the people searching the mansion start carrying weapons.

My thoughts

I heard this was pretty popular, so I gave it a go! It kept me on edge, though I did think it was a little familiar.

Cast

Samara Weaving, Adam Brody, Andie MacDowell

Trivia

This family made its fortune in games, so even a wedding has to involve a draw. The title borrows the seeker's call from childhood hide-and-seek, turning a familiar playground phrase into a threat.
