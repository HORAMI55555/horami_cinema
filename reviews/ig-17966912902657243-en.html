Synopsis

A family on holiday is directed to a secluded beach. Between a swim and a meal, the children grow visibly older. Everyone on the sand is aging years in a matter of hours, leaving the family just one afternoon to work out how to leave.

My thoughts

I’d wanted to see this since before it came out and caught it on Prime. A private resort beach where everyone ages incredibly fast: that alone sounded fun, and they actually built a story around it. It felt fresh. If you like weird horror that isn’t all that scary, this is right up your street. You don’t have to puzzle over every detail to enjoy it either.

Cast

Gael García Bernal, Vicky Krieps, Thomasin McKenzie

Trivia

Shyamalan’s daughters gave him the graphic novel Sandcastle for Father’s Day. He recalled how immediately cinematic it felt on the page. There’s a lovely irony in that gift: a story about a parent watching childhood disappear in an afternoon first reached its director through his own children.
