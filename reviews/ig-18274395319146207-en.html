Run Rabbit Run

2023

Scare3/5

Her daughter’s words awaken a buried past.

Synopsis

Sarah, a fertility doctor and single mother, lives with her young daughter Mia. When Mia starts behaving strangely, Sarah’s firm beliefs about life and death begin to crumble. Anxiety about motherhood collides with memories she would rather forget, forcing her to face her past.

My thoughts

Wow, this was actually scary. The atmosphere and darkness were captured so well that watching it felt suffocating. After reading an explanation, there were so many moments where I thought, “Wait, that’s what that meant?!” So interesting. A recommendation for horror fans. If you have siblings and have ever been mean to them during a fight, you’ll probably get even more immersed.

Cast

Sarah Snook, Lily LaTorre, Damon Herriman

Trivia

The oppressive dry landscape draws on writer Hannah Kent’s memories of growing up in Waikerie, South Australia. Netflix’s production feature explains how those memories informed the setting; landscapes in regional Victoria and the Riverland also reflect Sarah’s state of mind. Viewing tip: notice when open scenery starts to feel like a trap. Watching the space around mother and daughter, as well as their conversations, can deepen the unease.
