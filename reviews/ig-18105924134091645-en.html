The Invisible Man

2020

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5Scare2/5

No one in sight. Nowhere feels safe.

Synopsis

Cecilia escapes her controlling partner and goes into hiding. Even after learning of his death, she cannot shake the feeling that she is being watched. Strange events begin to unravel the life she is rebuilding. An unseen threat and the difficulty of convincing others turn ordinary spaces into sources of dread.

My thoughts

This really shocked me. At first I thought it would be a straightforward horror film, but then I started thinking, surely this can’t just be a ghost?! The direction it took surprised me. I won’t forget the tension near the end. Such a good, nerve-racking horror film!

Cast

Elisabeth Moss · Oliver Jackson-Cohen · Aldis Hodge · Storm Reid

Trivia

Leigh Whannell reimagines a classic Universal monster as a contemporary psychological thriller. Viewing tip: pay attention to the empty areas of the frame. The possibility of something occupying that space becomes part of the fear.
