Buried

2010

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5Scare1/5

One flick of a lighter reveals the size of his world.

Synopsis

Paul, a truck driver working in Iraq, wakes inside a buried coffin. With a lighter and a mobile phone, he has to reach someone who can find him before the battery and the air give out.

My thoughts

I saw this ages ago and it just popped back into my head! I loved it. He wakes up in a box underground with a lighter and barely any phone battery. How do you get out of that? It’s the same situation for an hour and a half, yet I never got bored.

Cast

Ryan Reynolds

Trivia

One coffin on screen took seven differently built coffins on set. Some had movable walls, and a longer version gave the crew room to work. The design even allowed a camera move all the way around Paul. Notice how far the lens can travel in a space where he can barely turn.
