The Innocents

2021

Innocent games. Terrifying powers.

Synopsis

During a bright Norwegian summer, children living around an apartment complex discover unusual abilities. Away from adults, they experiment in playgrounds and nearby woods. What starts as a shared secret begins to take a darker turn. This Nordic thriller asks what happens when extraordinary power meets a child’s still-developing sense of right and wrong.

My thoughts

Nordic horror really does have its own unsettling atmosphere. Personally, I didn’t find this scary, and I was still confused when it ended. But the Norwegian apartment setting felt so true to everyday Nordic life that it made me nostalgic. Yes, that’s exactly the feeling! It could be worth watching for that atmosphere alone.

Cast

Rakel Lenora Fløttum · Alva Brynsmo Ramstad · Sam Ashraf

Trivia

Written and directed by Eskil Vogt, the film was selected for Un Certain Regard at Cannes in 2021. Viewing tip: notice how the bright Nordic summer replaces the darkness usually associated with horror.
