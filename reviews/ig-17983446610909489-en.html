The Social Network

2010

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5

The invention that connected the world divided its founders.

Synopsis

Harvard student Mark Zuckerberg’s social website rapidly becomes a major business. Behind its rise, friendship with his co-founder fractures and disputes over ownership intensify. Through competing accounts, the film traces the excitement and isolation surrounding Facebook’s beginnings.

My thoughts

A movie about Mark Zuckerberg, the guy who created Facebook!!! It follows the story of how Facebook came about, and I kept thinking, “Whaaat, that’s how Facebook started?” and “Isn’t Mark kind of mean?” The story surprised me in that sense.

Cast

Jesse Eisenberg, Andrew Garfield, Justin Timberlake

Trivia

David Fincher directed Aaron Sorkin’s screenplay, based on Ben Mezrich’s The Accidental Billionaires. The production emphasized the conflicting perspectives of the young men involved rather than one hero’s success story: the friend who supplied seed money, the entrepreneur who connected the company with investors, and classmates claiming ownership of the idea. Layering their accounts creates tension by showing how the same events look different depending on who is telling the story.
