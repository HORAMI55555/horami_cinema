The Black Phone

2021

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3.5/5Scare2.5/5

A disconnected phone. One chance to escape.

Synopsis

As children disappear from a suburban town, young Finney is kidnapped and locked in a basement. A disconnected black phone begins to ring, carrying the voices of earlier victims. Their clues may offer a way to survive. Outside, his sister searches for him in a supernatural thriller built around fear, resilience and an unlikely connection.

My thoughts

This really held my attention. He’s trapped in such a hopeless situation, but the kids who died are trying to help him through that black phone... So tense, with an emotional finale. Really good—I recommend it!

Cast

Mason Thames · Ethan Hawke · Madeleine McGraw · Jeremy Davies

Trivia

The film adapts a short story by Joe Hill and is set in Colorado in 1978. Viewing tip: notice how the absence of modern communication makes the old telephone feel even more significant.
