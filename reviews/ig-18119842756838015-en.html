The Life List

2025

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3.5/5

An old list opens the door to a new life.

Synopsis

At a crossroads in her life, Alex receives an unusual challenge from her late mother: complete the list of dreams she wrote as a teenager. Reluctantly taking on each task brings forgotten ambitions, family secrets and new connections into view. The journey asks whether she can stop living by other people’s expectations and choose a life of her own.

My thoughts

This was lovely!! She’s lost her confidence and doesn’t feel like doing anything, but starts tackling the list connected to her late mother, one item at a time. Everything seems difficult, yet as she gives it a go, her life gradually starts changing for the better...! Just a really lovely film. Recommend!

Cast

Sofia Carson · Kyle Allen · Sebastian de Souza · Connie Britton

Trivia

Adapted from Lori Nelson Spielman’s novel, the film is written and directed by Adam Brooks, whose credits include Definitely, Maybe. Viewing tip: follow Alex’s changing relationship with her old ambitions as closely as the romance.
