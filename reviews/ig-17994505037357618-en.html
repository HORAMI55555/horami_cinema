Dumbo

2019

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3.5/5

His extraordinary ears become wings—and a way back to love.

Synopsis

Born into a struggling circus, Dumbo is mocked for his enormous ears until the family caring for him discovers he can fly. His sudden fame brings him to a spectacular entertainment park hiding dark secrets. An adventure about a calf’s love for his mother and the people determined to protect him.

My thoughts

I saw this at the cinema too... I was sobbing one minute in 😭😭😭 I thought a live-action Dumbo would be incredibly difficult to pull off, but they handled it so well—it really felt like Dumbo!!! It’s about human cruelty and the love between Dumbo and his mother overcoming it. I love it.

Cast

Colin Farrell, Michael Keaton, Danny DeVito, Eva Green

Trivia

Tim Burton expands the Disney classic into a live-action adventure. The official description highlights celebrating differences and cherishing family. Danny DeVito plays the circus owner, while Michael Keaton plays the entrepreneur behind Dreamland. Viewing tip: compare how Dumbo is treated in the small circus and the spectacular park. Even the same act of flying can feel different depending on whose needs it serves.
