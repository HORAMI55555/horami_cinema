Parasite

2019

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5

Two worlds move under one roof.

Synopsis

The unemployed Kim family sees an opportunity in the wealthy Parks. When their son enters the Parks’ elegant home as a tutor, the family’s fortunes begin to shift. An unforeseen incident destabilizes the arrangement in a story of inequality told through biting humor and mounting tension.

My thoughts

I saw this at the cinema a few years ago and forgot to post it!! The inequality and irony really came through, and I found it so interesting. You get to experience both the hardship of being poor and the luxury of being rich! I felt sorry for everyone by the end.

Cast

Song Kang-ho, Lee Sun-kyun, Cho Yeo-jeong, Choi Woo-shik, Park So-dam

Trivia

Bong Joon Ho’s film won Best Picture and Best Director at the 2020 Academy Awards, as recorded by the Academy. Viewing tip: pay attention not only to dialogue but also to where people stand and whether their movements take them upward or downward. As wealthy and struggling families grow closer, shared space can expose what they cannot share. Small differences in distance and height reveal tension beneath apparently light conversation.
