The Intern

2015

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★5/5

Experience never goes out of style.

Synopsis

Seventy-year-old widower Ben seeks a fresh purpose after retirement and joins an online fashion company as a senior intern. He is assigned to its founder, Jules, who is struggling to balance a rapidly growing business with family life. Different generations and working styles gradually give way to an unexpected friendship, built on Ben’s patience, experience and quiet attentiveness.

My thoughts

With Anne Hathaway starring, my expectations were high—and it absolutely delivered. It has a similar appeal to The Devil Wears Prada, except this time Anne is the boss, working hard and looking so cool. Ben is incredibly capable and charming too. If you love The Devil Wears Prada, I think you’ll love this.

Cast

Robert De Niro · Anne Hathaway · Rene Russo · Anders Holm

Trivia

The soundtrack has a playful side. The official production notes describe Nancy Meyers combining Theodore Shapiro’s score with songs by Benny Goodman, KC and the Sunshine Band, and Meghan Trainor. One sequence even pays musical homage to Ocean’s Eleven. That mix of eras provides a fitting backdrop for Ben and Jules’s cross-generational friendship. On a rewatch, listen to the music behind the dialogue as well as the conversations themselves.
