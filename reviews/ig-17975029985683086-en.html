Synopsis

Arriving in town for a job interview, Tess discovers a stranger already staying in her rental. A double booking leaves her reluctantly sharing the house for the night. But the unease she encounters goes far beyond an unfamiliar roommate, and the property holds something she never expected.

My thoughts

Ohhh, this was a really entertaining, substantial horror film! Loved how unpredictable it was ‼️ Even after watching so much horror, I kept thinking, what is happening, where is this going?! Good pacing, and it flew by. My one lingering complaint is whether Keith really needed to die that quickly. Still, I enjoyed it, and even felt a little sympathy for the monster at the end. If you can handle horror, watch this. The first half especially is properly scary, so it’s not one I’d suggest to someone who can’t stand the genre.

Cast

Trivia

Zach Cregger spent about two years picturing the film and its camera positions before production. He brought large storyboards to the set, but also stressed being ready to change the plan when an actor had a good idea.
