Last Night in Soho

2021

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5

Step behind the glitter of the swinging sixties.

Synopsis

Aspiring designer Eloise mysteriously enters 1960s London, where she becomes captivated by an aspiring singer named Sandie. But dark secrets lurk beneath the nightlife’s glamour. As the past she idolizes becomes a nightmare, her present-day life begins to unravel.

My thoughts

Absolutely amaaazing—the afterglow is unreal!!! It’s been ages since I saw a horror movie this stylish and fun, yet so sad and emotionally heavy. What a wonderful film. The whole world is pure genius: a lovely girl living in a dream of the 1960s, only to hit the wall of reality… So sad 😭 Wow, I haven’t felt this satisfied with a movie in ages. Thank you for recommending it.

Cast

Thomasin McKenzie, Anya Taylor-Joy, Matt Smith

Trivia

In an official interview, Matt Smith said Wright recommended Poor Cow and Peeping Tom as references. Suits, tie pins, a quiff and constant smoking gradually helped him build Jack. Viewing tip: look beyond the costumes’ retro appeal to the combination of attraction and unease they create. Soho itself can also feel like a character.
