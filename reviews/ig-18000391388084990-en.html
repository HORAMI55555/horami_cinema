Synopsis

In British-ruled India, Bheem seeks to rescue an abducted girl while Rama serves as a police officer for the British. Unaware of one another’s missions, they form a powerful friendship. When their goals collide, their loyalties and hidden convictions drive them toward an epic struggle.

My thoughts

I love this so much and somehow forgot to post it!!!!! I didn’t expect much at first, but by the end I was so moved, thinking: this is the absolute winner. Anyone who loves the live-action Aladdin will definitely love it. The music is unbelievably good. Such an energetic, refreshing movie, overflowing with Indian spirit. I want to watch it again and again! Highly recommend!

Cast

N. T. Rama Rao Jr., Ram Charan, Ajay Devgn

Trivia

The three Rs in the title come from director Rajamouli and leads Ram Charan and N. T. Rama Rao Jr. Netflix’s guide also explains the phrase Rise Roar Revolt. A title formed from the filmmakers’ names doubles as a declaration of the story’s energy. Viewing tip: alongside the spectacular action, watch how the distance between two men on different paths changes. Following the friendship at the heart of those enormous set pieces makes their emotional impact even more satisfying.
