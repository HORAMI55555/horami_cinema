The Exorcist

1973

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★2/5Scare3/5

A child in danger. A battle beyond explanation.

Synopsis

Young Regan begins to show disturbing changes that doctors cannot explain. Desperate to help her daughter, her mother turns to priests. As Regan becomes increasingly unrecognizable, faith, doubt, fear and love collide. A family crisis develops into a harrowing confrontation through the rite of exorcism.

My thoughts

This is one of those timeless exorcism classics you have to know. It’s from 1973, so it definitely has that older-film feel. For me it was less about being absolutely terrified and more about how raw and disturbing it felt. If you like exorcism films, this is essential viewing!

Cast

Ellen Burstyn · Linda Blair · Max von Sydow · Jason Miller

Trivia

William Peter Blatty adapted his own novel for director William Friedkin. With the novelist also writing the screenplay, comparing the book and film offers another way to explore how the story creates fear.
