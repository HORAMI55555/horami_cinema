A Tale of Mari and Three Puppies

2007

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5

Separated by disaster. Still a family.

Synopsis

Mari becomes part of a family in Yamakoshi Village and eventually gives birth to three puppies. Then an earthquake changes everything. As the residents evacuate, the dogs are left behind in the damaged village. Inspired by real events, the film follows the bond between a family and its dog, and Mari's struggle to protect her puppies.

My thoughts

I've watched this several times since elementary school, and I sob every single time. Such a beautiful film... Mari... 😭 If you want a proper cry, watch this. Knowing it was inspired by real events makes it hit even harder.

Cast

Eiichiro Funakoshi · Akiko Matsumoto · Ryohei Hirota · Mao Sasaki · Ken Utsui

Trivia

Ichi, the Shiba Inu playing Mari, trained for four to five months. The trainer said that the adorable head tilt came from a technique he had stumbled upon—and kept a trade secret. Even that seemingly spontaneous expression had some craft behind it!
