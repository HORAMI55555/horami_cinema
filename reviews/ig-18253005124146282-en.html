Do Revenge

2022

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3.5/5

Trading enemies turns friendship into a dangerous game.

Synopsis

After an intimate video leaks, prep-school queen Drea loses her status and friends. New student Eleanor also has a grudge against someone who hurt her. The unlikely pair secretly agree to take revenge on each other’s enemies, but behind their glamorous school lives, the scheme starts heading somewhere neither expected.

My thoughts

This one was so fun! Another movie set in an American high school, but the girls swap the people they want revenge on and go after each other’s targets. There’s a twist along the way, and the modern setting makes it really entertaining. Recommended!

Cast

Camila Mendes, Maya Hawke, Austin Abrams

Trivia

The pastel uniforms grew out of uniform images the director had collected, including Japanese-inspired looks, and a nod to Clueless. She and costume designer Alana Morshead wanted vivid colors that suited Miami. The school’s Horowitz Hall also references Clueless heroine Cher Horowitz. The production designer tucked teen-film references into building names, hiding the filmmakers’ affection for the genre inside the sweet-looking world surrounding this venomous story.
