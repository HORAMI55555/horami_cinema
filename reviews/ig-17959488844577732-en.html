If Anything Happens I Love You

2020

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5

Their shadows say what they can't.

Synopsis

Two grieving parents sit at the same table. Their shadows drift apart and lead us back through the memories still held by their quiet home.

My thoughts

I remembered seeing this little short. It only takes about thirteen minutes and it's worth it. It left me sad and aching, but so moved by the end. It made me want to treasure the people who are here with me now.

Cast

Dialogue-free animation; no speaking roles

Trivia

There is no spoken dialogue. When a shadow moves independently of its owner, it can express an argument or a longing the person can't put into words.
