1987

Some boxes should stay closed.

Synopsis

Searching for experiences beyond ordinary pleasure, Frank opens a puzzle box that brings him into contact with terrifying beings. Later, when his brother's family moves into the house, disturbing events begin to unfold. Young Kirsty finds herself caught between hidden desires within her family and a world that should never have been opened.

My thoughts

Whoa, those creatures!! The one on the poster alone is unforgettable, haha. The setup felt familiar: someone gets hold of something they should never touch, and everything goes wrong. But the beings coming from hell—angels, apparently, even though they look like demons—are so extreme that I almost laughed a few times. Pretty chaotic, but maybe worth a watch if you want some bizarre horror to pass the time. Definitely chaotic!!

Cast

Andrew Robinson · Clare Higgins · Ashley Laurence

Trivia

Clive Barker adapted his own novella, The Hellbound Heart, and directed the film himself. Its relationship between desire and horror comes directly from the author behind the story, rather than from a separate filmmaker interpreting his work.
