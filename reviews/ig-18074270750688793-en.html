A witness. A missing passenger. Nobody believes her.

Synopsis

On assignment aboard a luxury yacht, a journalist sees a passenger thrown into the sea. Yet everyone insists that nobody is missing. With no one willing to believe her, she investigates what happened that night—and puts herself at risk in the process.

My thoughts

Netflix recommended this, so I gave it a go! More entertaining than I expected, and the story took some unexpected turns. It’s a mystery unfolding over a few days on a cruise, with a good pace and just enough intrigue to keep things moving. Not especially scary—more of a murder mystery. Pretty enjoyable!

Cast

Keira Knightley · Guy Pearce · Hannah Waddingham · Kaya Scodelario

Trivia

Based on Ruth Ware’s novel and directed by Simon Stone. Viewing tip: alongside the clues, watch how people respond to the journalist’s testimony; being dismissed is part of the tension.
