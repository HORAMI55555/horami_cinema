Synopsis

After a Black Friday riot ends in tragedy, a masked killer brings Thanksgiving-themed terror to Plymouth. As residents become targets, Jessica and her friends find themselves singled out in disturbing social media posts. They must work out who is behind the mask before the holiday claims more victims.

My thoughts

I’d heard the title because it was fairly well known, and finally watched it!! Genre-wise, it reminded me of Terrifier and The Texas Chain Saw Massacre. Similar vibe, but with a bit more background to make the situation easy to follow. If you want a horror film that keeps startling you without requiring too much thinking, I recommend it ❣️ Simply fun ❣️

Cast

Trivia

The idea began as Eli Roth’s fake trailer for the 2007 Grindhouse project. Sixteen years later, Roth turned it into an actual feature film.
