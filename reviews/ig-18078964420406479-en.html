This team-building retreat has a body count.

Synopsis

Municipal employees gather at a retreat to strengthen their team. Allegations surrounding a development project and simmering resentments quickly poison the atmosphere. When an unidentified killer attacks, the coworkers must try to survive while still distrusting one another.

My thoughts

For some reason this was in the rankings, but since it was Swedish I wasn’t expecting much—and I was right 🤣 It’s just employees gathering on a remote island for some project, then a serial killer attacks them one after another. So cliché! Fine for killing time, I guess!

Cast

Katia Winter, Adam Lundgren, Eva Melander

Trivia

The film adapts Mats Strandberg’s 2021 novel, and the author also served as executive producer. In the official announcement, he praised its preservation of the book’s blood, tension, humor and uniquely awkward Swedish conference atmosphere. Patrik Eklund directs. Viewing tip: listen to the coworkers before the killer arrives. The gap between a bonding exercise and colleagues who cannot get along produces comedy alongside the horror.
