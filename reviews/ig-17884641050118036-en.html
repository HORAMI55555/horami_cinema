The Call

2020

Scare1/5

Same house. Same phone. Twenty years apart.

Synopsis

Seo-yeon takes a call from Young-sook, who lived in the same house twenty years earlier. As their friendship grows, the women try to alter the past. Only Seo-yeon can immediately see what that does to the present.

My thoughts

I heard this Korean thriller was popular, and seeing Park Shin-hye in the lead had me excited right away. It's genuinely so, so good, and I even cried at the end. Doesn't feel like horror, so I'd recommend it to anyone.

Cast

Park Shin-hye, Jun Jong-seo

Trivia

The leads share a house without sharing a present. Keep an eye on the room itself: a change in the setting can reveal the effect of a decision on the other end of the line before anyone explains it.
