The Good Place

2016

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5

She made it to paradise. By mistake.

Synopsis

Eleanor wakes in an afterlife reserved for good people. There is just one problem: she was selfish in life and seems to have been mistaken for someone else. Keeping the error secret to avoid expulsion, she tries to become a better person in a comedy that begins after death.

My thoughts

This was so satisfying to watch and absolutely fantastic!! The whole theme is what the afterlife is like, which is interesting in itself, and it’s a pretty unique series. The story keeps turning things upside down, so it never gets boring ❣️ If you wonder what happens after we die, definitely watch it.

Cast

Kristen Bell, Ted Danson, William Jackson Harper

Trivia

Creator Michael Schur also worked on Brooklyn Nine-Nine and Parks and Recreation. NBC introduces this series as a comedy about what makes someone good. Viewing tip: after each funny mistake, consider whether Eleanor is merely avoiding punishment or genuinely acting for someone else. The mysteries of the afterlife can then be enjoyed alongside questions about kindness that apply to everyday life.
