Synopsis

A criminal crew is hired to kidnap a powerful man’s twelve-year-old ballerina daughter and guard her overnight. The promised ransom seems worth the risk—until members of the group begin falling victim to something inside the isolated mansion. Abigail is far from the helpless child they expected.

My thoughts

This was SO much fun!!! Why isn’t everyone talking about it?! They kidnap a child for money, only to discover she’s a vampire, and the moment they realize it is incredible. It keeps going from there—if you enjoy zombie movies, I think you’ll like this too! The ending somehow felt both warm and satisfying. It’s vampire horror rather than ghosts, with a fair number of jump scares. Such a good time, and the young lead’s acting is amazing!

Cast

Melissa Barrera · Alisha Weir · Dan Stevens · Kathryn Newton · Angus Cloud

Trivia

Directed by Radio Silence’s Matt Bettinelli-Olpin and Tyler Gillett. Official extras include a look at Alisha Weir and choreographer Belinda Murphy developing Abigail’s ballerina performance.
