Ju-On: The Grudge

2002

Scare4/5

A routine home visit. Something comes home with her.

Synopsis

Volunteer caregiver Rika visits a house to look after an elderly woman. A boy and an unsettling presence draw her into a history that reaches far beyond the building and into the lives of people who passed through it.

My thoughts

An enduring Japanese horror classic that everyone knows. Japan really delivered! It's all about the atmosphere for me; that distinctly Japanese setting makes it so much scarier.

Cast

Megumi Okina, Misaki Ito, Misa Uehara

Trivia

This Japanese theatrical film followed Takashi Shimizu's earlier video productions. Its shifting character chapters make the house the point of continuity; your attention keeps moving on to the next person who enters its orbit.
