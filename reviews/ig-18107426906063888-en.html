An unexpected guest unsettles a perfect holiday.

Synopsis

A wealthy German family spends the summer at their holiday home in southern France. After injuring a young woman in a road incident, they bring her into their home. As she grows closer to individual family members, their comfortable routine begins to shift. Beneath the attractive surroundings lie tensions and desires that make this holiday increasingly unsettling.

My thoughts

This gave me Orphan vibes: the person they welcome into their home turns out to be dangerous, and the family starts falling apart! I really enjoy this kind of setup. If you like Orphan, give this a try!

Cast

Valerie Pachner · Fahri Yardim · Carla Díaz · Naila Schuberth

Trivia

Nele Mueller-Stöfen’s directorial debut had its world premiere in the Panorama section of the 75th Berlin International Film Festival in 2025. Viewing tip: watch the contrast between the holiday setting and the growing unease within the family.
