Pretending to see ghosts only works until one appears.

Synopsis

A team of fake ghost hunters led by a brother and sister takes a job at an old house where children can be heard crying. Their usual act starts to unravel when Angela sees something nobody staged.

My thoughts

I hadn’t heard much about this one, but something made me press play. What a find! I had a great time, and the scares are manageable enough that I’d happily recommend it. I love horror nights like this.

Cast

Florence Pugh, Ben Lloyd-Hughes

Trivia

The source novel is called Hush, and its author, Eva Konstantopoulos, also co-wrote the screenplay. That little word gives the first assignment an extra chill: the team has been hired to make the children quiet.
