ドクムシ

2016

Scare1/5

Seven strangers, seven days, one pot. No food.

Synopsis

Seven strangers wake inside a sealed school building. They find a timer and cooking equipment, but nothing to eat. As hunger sets in, their theories about why they were brought together begin to turn them against one another.

My thoughts

More gory than scary, but it felt fresh and I had fun with it.

Cast

Ryota Murai, Rina Takeda

Trivia

The story went from online fiction to manga before becoming a film. Its central image comes from kodoku, in which venomous creatures are confined in one vessel. Here, seven people share that vessel, and it happens to be a school.
