2022

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5Scare1/5

A daughter returns. A trap begins.

Synopsis

After escaping an Estonian institution, Leena travels to America posing as a missing girl named Esther. Welcomed into a wealthy family, she appears to have secured a new life. But a confrontation with a mother determined to protect her family sends her scheme in an unexpected direction.

My thoughts

I was so excited for Orphan 2, so I’m happy Prime Video added it this quickly 🥹😍 The first one already left me pretty satisfied, so I wondered where they would take it. It starts properly connected to the first one, and she destroys another family in a similar way, but the developments are quite different. That made it interesting, and I kept thinking Esther is seriously incredible and way too clever. By the end I wanted Esther dead, but she’s so hard to kill! Every Orphan fan should watch. If you want to try horror without ghosts, this is fun, so give it a go. It’s a psycho thriller, so it isn’t scary!

Cast

Isabelle Fuhrman, Julia Stiles, Rossif Sutherland

Trivia

Although released second, this is a prequel set before the original Orphan. Paramount’s announcement presents it as Esther’s beginning, directed by William Brent Bell. Viewing tip: knowing the original film’s secret makes the family’s reactions especially worth watching. Pay attention to what each person believes and conceals; that creates a different tension from expecting the first story simply to repeat.
