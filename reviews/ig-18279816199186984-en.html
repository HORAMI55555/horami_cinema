Emily in Paris

2020

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★5/5

A Paris reset for work, love, and your wardrobe.

Synopsis

Chicago marketing executive Emily unexpectedly lands an assignment in Paris. Faced with a different language and workplace culture, she relies on determination and her social-media instincts. New friendships and romantic possibilities turn her career-focused routine into a much more colorful adventure.

My thoughts

It’s soooo good!! Like The Devil Wears Prada, it follows a heroine who works really hard, but the setting is France, so all those French elements make it wonderful. If you love France or have studied French, you’ll enjoy it even more. It made me think French people seem complicated, France is incredibly stylish, and I’d love to live there. Just watching puts me in a good mood and makes me feel positive and want to go abroad—especially to Paris. Everyone should definitely watch this.

Cast

Lily Collins, Ashley Park, Philippine Leroy-Beaulieu

Trivia

Emily’s wardrobe also tells her story. Costume designer Marylin Fitoussi explained that Season 4’s reinterpretation of Parisian style reflects a more self-assured woman. Autumn and winter settings introduced richer layers, coats, and boots. Lily Collins also described changing heel shapes to suit a life spent walking on cobblestones. Those practical and emotional details give the clothes another dimension: the city and Emily’s growing confidence are written into what she wears.
