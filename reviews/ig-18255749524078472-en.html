Hannibal

2001

Scare2/5

In Florence, a monster can pass for a gentleman.

Synopsis

Years after Lecter's escape, a detective in Florence suspects he has found him. A surviving victim wants revenge, while FBI agent Clarice Starling is drawn back into the hunt for reasons of her own.

My thoughts

Now this felt like proper horror. The fear is all about what a person is capable of, and the atmosphere really gets inside your head. Every so often there's a gory scene where some of you will absolutely want to shut your eyes. It kept me interested right up to the end.

Cast

Anthony Hopkins, Julianne Moore, Gary Oldman

Trivia

Hopkins returned as Lecter after The Silence of the Lambs. On the other side of their encounter, Julianne Moore took over Clarice from Jodie Foster, and Ridley Scott took over directing from Jonathan Demme.
