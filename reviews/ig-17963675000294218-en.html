28 Days Haunted

2022

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5Scare3/5

One night is one thing. Could you stay for four weeks?

Synopsis

Three teams move into different American buildings with a history of reported hauntings. Each team stays for 28 days. Cutting between the three locations, the series follows what happens when a noise you could shrug off on night one keeps you company for weeks.

My thoughts

I usually skip ghost-hunting shows because they feel staged, but I got really into this one. Three teams spend 28 days investigating different properties. The connection to the real-life Warrens from Annabelle and The Conjuring made the stories feel especially chilling to me. Six short episodes fly by. My pick was the Madison case, about a father who killed his family and then himself. I nearly cried at the end. It didn’t scare me the way a horror film does. Seeing people actually spend the night there got to me in a different way.

Cast

Tony Spera, Aaron Sagers

Trivia

The number in the title sets the length of each team’s stay. The format grew out of a theory associated with Ed and Lorraine Warren, the investigators dramatized in The Conjuring. Following three groups on the same clock makes for an intriguing comparison: would the other investigators even have noticed the noise that kept this team awake?
