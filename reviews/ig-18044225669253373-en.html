A casual gathering becomes a night beyond repair.

Synopsis

Teacher Emily hosts a gathering of women who share her beliefs. Beneath the friendly conversation, racism and hostility come into focus. A confrontation at a shop after the meeting pushes the group toward escalating violence. The film follows one afternoon at an uncomfortably close distance.

My thoughts

I somehow went in expecting something like A Quiet Place, but this is completely different. It’s a harsh look at white racism. If you’re interested in that subject, you may find it compelling, but it wasn’t what I wanted from this watch. I ended up wondering what kind of film I’d just watched, honestly.

Cast

Stefanie Estes · Olivia Luccardi · Dana Millican

Trivia

The shoot lasted just four days. Designed to unfold as a continuous take, the finished film mainly uses day four’s performance, seamlessly joined with selected moments from day three. The director secured nearby locations before writing, so even the travel between scenes could become part of the action.
