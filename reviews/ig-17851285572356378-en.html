The Watcher

2022

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★2.5/5Scare1.5/5

Their dream house comes with an unseen watcher.

Synopsis

After moving into their dream suburban home, the Brannock family begins receiving disturbing letters from someone calling themselves the Watcher. Neighbours, local history and everyday encounters all start to look suspicious. The house they bought for a fresh start becomes the centre of an increasingly consuming mystery.

My thoughts

It had been a while since I watched a horror series! A house being such a constant focus felt interesting, and this is inspired by a real case. The way everyone becomes obsessed with one home leads to such an ironic ending. For me, it’s a fairly solid suspense-horror drama—the final stretch was especially interesting. Recommend.

Cast

Naomi Watts · Bobby Cannavale · Jennifer Coolidge · Mia Farrow

Trivia

The story takes place in New Jersey, but the family’s screen home is a house in Rye, New York. A location assistant identified it in local reporting. The building that dominates the series has a real-world identity in a different state.
