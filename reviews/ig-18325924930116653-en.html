Let It Snow

2019

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5

A snowbound town. A night full of possibilities.

Synopsis

A snowstorm hits a small town on Christmas Eve, disrupting plans and bringing a group of young people into unexpected contact. Family responsibilities, work, friendships and crushes collide across the day. As their paths cross, the snowbound setting creates opportunities for feelings that ordinary routines might have kept hidden.

My thoughts

Such a lovely film 🥺 Definitely one to watch before Christmas! It made me feel cheerful and energised, with the kind of mood I get from Home Alone or Love Actually. About an hour and a half, so if you cannot decide what to watch, give this a go. Loved it!

Cast

Isabela Merced, Shameik Moore, Kiernan Shipka, Liv Hewson

Trivia

The book was written by John Green, Maureen Johnson and Lauren Myracle, and Green’s own website documents the adaptation. Rather than following only one couple, the film lets several stories overlap in the same town. Someone in the background of one encounter can become central elsewhere. Watch the small choices people make after the snow disrupts their plans: moments of inconvenience turn into opportunities to connect, giving the holiday coincidences their warmth.
