Wedding-night hide-and-seek, with deadly stakes.

Synopsis

Grace marries into a wealthy family and is invited to take part in their wedding-night tradition: a game chosen by drawing a card. Hide-and-seek sounds harmless, until she realizes the relatives searching the mansion are armed. Her first night as part of the family becomes a desperate struggle to stay alive.

My thoughts

I’d known about this for ages, and finally gave it a proper watch!!! The tension just keeps going, but the story is simple enough to enjoy effortlessly. My heart was seriously racing haha. If you love Don’t Breathe-style suspense, you’ll have such a good time. Recommended!!

Cast

Samara Weaving, Adam Brody, Andie MacDowell

Trivia

Seventeen versions of Grace’s wedding dress were prepared, showing different stages of damage. The costume tracks the increasingly brutal progress of her night.
