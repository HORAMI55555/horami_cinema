The Platform

2019

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5

What the floor above eats, the floor below loses.

Synopsis

A banquet descends through a tower of cells once a day. What newcomer Goreng gets to eat depends on the appetites of everyone above him.

My thoughts

I was looking forward to this long before its Netflix release and finally saw it in Finland. No Japanese subs, annoyingly! The setup felt fresh, and I loved digging into the metaphors and biblical imagery afterward. Fair warning, the gore nearly finished me off several times.

Cast

Iván Massagué, Zorion Eguileor, Antonia San Juan

Trivia

The Spanish title El hoyo means the hole. The English title draws your attention to the moving platform; the original points to the void that swallows it.
