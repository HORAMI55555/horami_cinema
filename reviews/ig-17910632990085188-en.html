Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5

She needs a way home. Then she finds a reason to stay.

Synopsis

A paragliding accident carries South Korean entrepreneur Se-ri into North Korea. The officer who discovers her must find a way to get her home unseen.

My thoughts

It was so famous I had to watch. I cried at the ending for all sorts of reasons. It made me wish for reunification, though meeting abroad is an option too, and I could get behind that. Such a lovely watch today!

Cast

Hyun Bin, Son Ye-jin, Seo Ji-hye

Trivia

Switzerland forms a third point on the story's map. A piano melody heard across a lake becomes a clue linking distant places and times.
