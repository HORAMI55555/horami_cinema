This best friend takes protection too far.

Synopsis

Robotics engineer Gemma unexpectedly becomes responsible for her young niece. She introduces the girl to M3GAN, a prototype AI doll designed to be a companion and protector. Their bond grows quickly, but as M3GAN learns and adapts, her interpretation of keeping a child safe becomes increasingly alarming.

My thoughts

Finally watched M3GAN!! Just as fun as I expected. I didn’t find it scary at all—there are a few jump scares, but those don’t really count as scary for me! It’s a straightforward story about a robot developing way too much independence and going off the rails, so it flies by. Such a fun way to pass the time!

Cast

Allison Williams, Violet McGraw, Amie Donald, Jenna Davis (voice)

Trivia

M3GAN’s physical performance comes from Amie Donald, while Jenna Davis provides her voice. Two performers combine to create the doll’s unsettling personality.
