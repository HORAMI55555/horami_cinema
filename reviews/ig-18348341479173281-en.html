Love Again

2023

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5

A message to the past. A chance at something new.

Synopsis

After losing her fiancé, Mira keeps sending messages to his old phone number. She does not know it now belongs to journalist Rob's work phone. Moved by the words of a woman he has never met, Rob seeks advice from his interview subject, Celine Dion. An unexpected connection offers a new possibility to someone still holding on to the past.

My thoughts

Such a lovely film! Celine Dion actually appears as herself—I was so surprised when she showed up!! The setup is so sweet: messages sent to a late fiancé's phone number reach the man who now has it, and that's how everything begins. If you're in the mood to put on a romance, give this one a try.

Cast

Priyanka Chopra Jonas · Sam Heughan · Celine Dion

Trivia

Celine Dion plays herself, in what Sony Pictures describes as her first film role. Her presence is woven into the romance: she becomes someone Rob turns to for advice, rather than simply a famous name mentioned in passing.
