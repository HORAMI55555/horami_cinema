Synopsis

As environmental collapse threatens humanity’s future, former pilot Cooper joins a mission to search for a new home beyond Earth. Leaving his children behind, he travels into unfamiliar reaches of space. The journey tests more than endurance and distance: it challenges the very possibility of keeping his promise to return.

My thoughts

I was NOT prepared for that scale or how real it felt!!!!! It was so famous that I somehow never got around to watching it, and now I regret waiting. Everything is so enormous it almost made me laugh in disbelief. And Cooper,, what a journey. Absolutely loved it. This might turn me into a sci-fi fan.

Cast

Matthew McConaughey, Anne Hathaway, Jessica Chastain, Michael Caine

Trivia

The production combined anamorphic 35mm film with IMAX film photography, using large-format images to give its cosmic journey a tangible sense of scale.
