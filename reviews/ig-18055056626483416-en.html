Shutter Island

2010

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5

On this island, even certainty feels unsafe.

Synopsis

U.S. Marshal Teddy Daniels and his partner arrive at an island psychiatric institution to investigate a patient’s disappearance. How could anyone vanish from such a closely guarded place? Evasive staff, elusive clues and disturbing memories make the assignment increasingly personal, until Teddy struggles to know what to trust.

My thoughts

Finally watched this famous one!! My first Leonardo DiCaprio film apart from Titanic. An investigator arrives at a psychiatric institution, but something clearly isn’t right. If you enjoy that kind of mystery, there’s a lot to like—and the ending really had me going, ohhh, so THAT’S where this was heading. A good watch!

Cast

Leonardo DiCaprio · Mark Ruffalo · Ben Kingsley · Michelle Williams

Trivia

Scorsese screened Laura and Out of the Past for DiCaprio, Mark Ruffalo and Ben Kingsley. He used them to convey a world-weary hero’s body language and the feeling of being caught in a mystery. Classic cinema became a shared reference point for the cast.
