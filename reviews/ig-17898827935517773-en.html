She took the noise next door as a sign of life.

Synopsis

After moving into an aging apartment complex, Asuka hears scratching through the wall at night. What she discovers about her elderly neighbour draws her to Sasahara, a cleaner who deals with the rooms people leave behind.

My thoughts

I saw this ages ago and still remember what happened, which says something about how much I enjoyed it. Moderately scary and a good watch.

Cast

Atsuko Maeda, Hiroki Narimiya

Trivia

Ring director Hideo Nakata locates the haunting just one thin wall away. Sasahara's work cleaning homes after unattended deaths gives him a reason to cross that threshold, connecting an everyday urban tragedy with a ghost story.
