Verónica

2017

Scare4/5

The eclipse ends. The séance doesn't.

Synopsis

In 1990s Madrid, Verónica joins her schoolfriends in a séance during a solar eclipse. The disturbances follow her home, where looking after her younger siblings leaves her little chance to escape or even explain them.

My thoughts

This is so good, and reasonably scary. I loved how it connects an eclipse with horror and draws on a reported Spanish case. If you have even a little tolerance for scary movies, please see it!

Cast

Sandra Escacena, Bruna González, Claudia Placer

Trivia

Paco Plaza of REC drew inspiration from a reported Madrid case. His film locates the haunting in a teenager's daily responsibilities, so feeding and protecting her younger siblings matters as much as the séance.
