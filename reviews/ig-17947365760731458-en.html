2017

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5Scare3/5

He has just enough time to find someone for his daughter.

Synopsis

In rural Australia after an outbreak, Andy learns that he too is infected. With his baby daughter on his back, he searches for someone who can keep her safe once he can no longer trust himself around her.

My thoughts

That image really jumped out at me the first time I saw it. I enjoyed the film and cried my eyes out at the end. I’d recommend this if you like zombie stories that move you more than they scare you.

Cast

Martin Freeman, Simone Landers, Anthony Hayes

Trivia

Directors Ben Howling and Yolanda Ramke first told this story in a short film of the same name, then returned to direct the feature. Look again at the poster with that single word in mind. What this father is carrying gives an otherwise ordinary freight term a very different weight.
