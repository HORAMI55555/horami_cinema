He’s All That

2021

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5

Can she turn the school outsider into prom king?

Synopsis

Teen influencer Padgett faces a collapsing reputation after a romantic humiliation goes public. To restore her status, she bets that she can turn school outsider Cameron into prom king. Getting to know him, however, makes her question the carefully constructed image she has built for herself.

My thoughts

Another American high-school movie! This time, unusually, the main character is the leader of the popular group—and she’s actually nice! Lovely.

Cast

Addison Rae, Tanner Buchanan, Rachael Leigh Cook

Trivia

This movie reverses the central gender roles of the 1999 teen romance She’s All That. Original screenwriter R. Lee Fleming Jr. returned to write the reimagining, with Mean Girls director Mark Waters behind the camera. Rachael Leigh Cook and Matthew Lillard from the earlier film also appear in the cast. That gives the influencer-era story a direct creative connection to the people who helped shape its 1990s predecessor.
