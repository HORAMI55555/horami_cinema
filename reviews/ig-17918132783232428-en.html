The King: Eternal Monarch

2020

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5

He crosses into another world. His crown means nothing there.

Synopsis

Emperor Lee Gon rides through a bamboo forest into a parallel Korea where he meets detective Jeong Tae-eul. Together they begin to trace the movements of someone else who knows how to cross between their worlds.

My thoughts

Another Lee Min-ho drama 😍 There’s this passage in a Korean forest that takes you to a parallel world, the Kingdom of Corea. People are crossing back and forth to do terrible things, time and space start acting up, and people from one world take someone else’s place in the other. I had so much fun keeping up with it all.

Cast

Lee Min-ho, Kim Go-eun, Woo Do-hwan

Trivia

Kim Eun-sook, who wrote Guardian: The Lonely and Great God, wrote this series too. Kim Go-eun plays a detective here. Watch their encounter from her side: a man calling himself emperor is someone whose identity needs checking. His moment of destiny is her first lead.
