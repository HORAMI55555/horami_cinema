The box contains a voice he can no longer question.

Synopsis

After his classmate Hannah dies, Clay receives cassette tapes she recorded. As he follows her account through familiar hallways and friendships, he discovers how differently the people involved remember the same events.

My thoughts

This gave me so much to think about. People online said they'd never felt so heartsick watching something, so I tried it. They were right. I felt that heaviness through all four seasons. I really think people should see it.

Cast

Dylan Minnette, Katherine Langford

Trivia

Adapted from Jay Asher's novel, the first season moves between Clay listening in the present and Hannah's account of the past. The same school corridors hold both of them, yet the conversation can only travel one way.
