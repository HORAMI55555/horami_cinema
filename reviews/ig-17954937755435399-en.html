A beauty queen in the making. A revenge streak out of control.

Synopsis

Bullied over her appearance, high schooler Patty finds that an unexpected change transforms how people treat her. She teams up with disgraced pageant coach Bob to chase a crown, but their glamorous ambition unleashes her anger and appetite for revenge.

My thoughts

It’s about a girl who suddenly starts entering American beauty pageants and winning her way up toward becoming Miss America, but it’s seriously psycho and wild. I think people who like The End of the F***ing World would like this, but the ending hurts. Apparently it got canceled even though there was supposed to be more 😭😭 Basically, it’s a slightly psycho high school girl who loves murder going all in on beauty pageants!

Cast

Debby Ryan, Dallas Roberts, Alyssa Milano

Trivia

Created by Lauren Gussis, the series is presented by Netflix as a revenge comedy pairing a bullied teenager with a disgraced coach. Debby Ryan plays Patty opposite Dallas Roberts, with Alyssa Milano also in the principal cast. Part of the fun of this setup is that Patty’s reinvention does not remove the adults’ own ambitions. The familiar mentor-and-protégé race for a crown becomes a partnership in which neither person seems entirely in control.
