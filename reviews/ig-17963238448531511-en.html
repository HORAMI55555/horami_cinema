Stairway to Heaven

2003

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5

What happens when the person who knows you best becomes a stranger?

Synopsis

As children, Jeong-seo and Song-ju find comfort in each other after each loses a parent. Her father remarries and he leaves to study abroad. Suddenly the two who grew up side by side have to fight to find their way back to each other.

My thoughts

This one goes way back, but it’s still one of my favourites. It moved me so much I cried and cried. The sheer strength of that love…

Cast

Choi Ji-woo, Kwon Sang-woo

Trivia

The childhood scenes hold the key to the romance. One child has lost a mother, the other a father. Before either can put a name to love, they know what it feels like to have someone who understands an empty place at the table. Remember that when you see them as adults.
