Four people in the driveway. Four familiar faces.

Synopsis

Adelaide returns to Santa Cruz with her family, uneasy about something she experienced there as a child. That night, four strangers stand hand in hand outside the house. In the light, each visitor looks exactly like someone inside.

My thoughts

I’d wanted to see this for ages and finally caught it during a limited Netflix run. I’d expected something like Get Out, with racism at the center, and it took me somewhere else entirely. That opening is so unsettling. It felt more like action as it went on, but those early scares really worked for me.

Cast

Lupita Nyong’o, Winston Duke, Shahadi Wright Joseph

Trivia

Jordan Peele liked the way scissors join two matching halves into one object. They can look utterly ordinary on a desk and terrifying in someone’s hand. That makes them a tiny working model of the film’s idea: the same familiar shape can mean two very different things.
