The Shining

1980

Scare3/5

Too many footsteps for a hotel with just one family in it.

Synopsis

Jack takes a winter caretaker job at an isolated hotel and brings his wife and son. Danny sees troubling things in its empty corridors while his father, supposedly there to write, grows increasingly unpredictable.

My thoughts

Everyone says this is terrifying, so I watched it and thought, “Wait, none of this scares me!” Apparently it's psychological fear. It didn't click for me. Maybe I'll try it again when I'm older.

Cast

Jack Nicholson, Shelley Duvall, Danny Lloyd

Trivia

Steadicam work keeps the camera low behind Danny's tricycle. Listen as the wheels cross from hard floor to carpet and back; even a corner with nothing around it starts to feel like an event.
