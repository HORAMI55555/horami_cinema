Synopsis

Working airport security on Christmas Eve, Ethan is contacted by a stranger who demands that he let a dangerous bag through screening. With someone he loves under threat, he must find a way to protect both her and the passengers. A busy holiday shift becomes a high-pressure contest in which every decision matters.

My thoughts

If you love travelling abroad and spend a lot of time in airports, this is such a good pick. The security checkpoint itself becomes the setting for a terror plot! I was tense the whole time, wondering what would happen next. Highly recommend!!!

Cast

Taron Egerton · Jason Bateman · Sofia Carson · Danielle Deadwyler

Trivia

Although the story takes place at LAX, filming took place in New Orleans. Netflix explains that the old airport terminal was redesigned to stand in for Los Angeles International Airport.
