Miracle in Cell No. 7

2013

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5

A prison cell has a secret small enough to hide.

Synopsis

Yong-gu, a father with an intellectual disability, is wrongfully imprisoned and separated from his little girl, Ye-seung. His cellmates begin looking for a way to bring father and daughter together inside the prison.

My thoughts

A non-horror pick. I honestly cried more at this than at any other movie I've seen. It moved me so much. If you've got an evening free, watch this.

Cast

Ryu Seung-ryong, Kal So-won, Park Shin-hye

Trivia

In the Korean film, Kal So-won plays young Ye-seung and Park Shin-hye plays her as an adult. The same prison memories belong both to a child and to a woman finally able to speak on her father's behalf.
