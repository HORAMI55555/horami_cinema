How long would you ignore that uneasy feeling?

Synopsis

After meeting a British family on holiday, Ben and Louise accept an invitation to visit their country home with their daughter. The weekend starts warmly, but odd remarks and unsettling behavior gradually undermine the welcome. Trying to remain polite becomes increasingly difficult as the guests realize their hosts may have very different intentions.

My thoughts

This was so good!! I couldn't tell where it was heading or even what kind of horror it would become for quite a while. That deeply unsettling, unhinged atmosphere was exactly my thing—I loved it! It felt like a roller coaster of nerves and suspense. If you like Don't Breathe, give this a try.

Cast

James McAvoy · Mackenzie Davis · Scoot McNairy · Aisling Franciosi

Trivia

This 2024 version was written and directed by James Watkins, drawing on the screenplay of the 2022 Danish film by Christian and Mads Tafdrup. Search by year or by James McAvoy's name to distinguish the two films.
