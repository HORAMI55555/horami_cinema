Incantation

2022

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4.5/5Scare4.5/5

When the woman on screen asks for your help, will you answer?

Synopsis

Six years after breaking a religious taboo, Ronan begins living with her young daughter. When disturbing things start happening around the child, Ronan records an appeal for help. The people she’s counting on include whoever is watching the footage.

My thoughts

I’d been waiting for this Taiwanese horror film since the Netflix announcement. Finally watched it, and wow. The atmosphere is so convincing that halfway through I caught myself thinking, “Hang on, am I getting cursed too?” Hearing it was inspired by real events made it even creepier for me. If you can’t handle horror, this would finish you off. Even horror fans will probably get scared. It’s a fun dare if you feel brave. Me? I actually looked away for the last scene. I didn’t want to risk it!

Cast

Tsai Hsuan-yen, Huang Sin-ting, Kao Ying-hsuan

Trivia

The viewer has a part to play. By the time you’ve memorized a sign or caught the rhythm of a chant, watching can start to feel like helping Ronan. Try asking a friend afterwards which of her requests they went along with. Two people can watch the same scene and draw the line in very different places.
