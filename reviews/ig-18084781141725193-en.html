Euphoria

2019

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5

Growing up, with nowhere to hide the hurt.

Synopsis

Rue struggles with addiction while the teenagers around her navigate love, friendship, sexuality and identity. Their choices reshape one another’s lives, exposing loneliness and pain beneath the glittering surface. A visually intense drama about growing up and trying to connect.

My thoughts

I had such a good time watching this, and everyone looks so gorgeous! It follows American high school students in a dark, messy world, a little like 13 Reasons Why. Drugs and same-sex relationships are among the themes, and they’re explored with a depth that felt real to me. Zendaya AND my favorite Sydney—what a cast. If you love international teen dramas, you’ll love this. It is pretty dark and heavy, though not as heavy as 13 Reasons Why for me.

Cast

Zendaya · Hunter Schafer · Sydney Sweeney · Jacob Elordi

Trivia

Although it follows American teenagers, its starting point was an Israeli series of the same name. Sam Levinson developed the HBO version from the series created by Ron Leshem and Daphna Levin.
