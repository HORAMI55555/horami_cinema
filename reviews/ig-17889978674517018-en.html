Ghostbusters II

1989

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5Scare0/5

Under Manhattan, the city’s bad mood has become a river.

Synopsis

Out of the ghost-catching business, the team reunites when Dana’s baby carriage rolls into traffic on its own. Beneath the street where it happened, they discover a river of pink slime that responds to human emotion.

My thoughts

This felt like a whole new adventure to me. It was even less scary than the first one, much more of an action movie. A few bits actually got me a little emotional. I had a good time with it.

Cast

Bill Murray, Dan Aykroyd, Sigourney Weaver

Trivia

That glint on the underground slime was deliberate. The effects crew used “diamond dust,” a material found in metallic car paint, to give the river its shimmer. Look past the bright pink and watch the way the highlights move across the dark tunnel.
