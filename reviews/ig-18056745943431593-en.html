Synopsis

Cosmetics seller Seo A-ri enters the social-media world after reconnecting with an old friend. Her sudden rise brings her into a glamorous circle of influencers, where envy and manipulation simmer beneath the surface. Fame comes with secrets that may put her life at risk.

My thoughts

This was so, so good!! It’s about Korean Instagrammers, and learning so much about how people become celebrities and how they live was really fun, haha. It felt so real. The story kept leaving me thinking, “Wait, what happened next?” And the anonymous troublemaker getting exposed at the end reminded me of Unlocked. Loved it! I think girls especially will enjoy this. It’s stylish too—highly recommended! The dark side of women!

Cast

Park Gyu-young, Kang Min-hyuk, Lee Chung-ah

Trivia

Director Kim Cheol-kyu did not use social media before the production and opened an account to research it. Netflix explains that he also cast nearly 30 real Instagram and YouTube personalities in cameos and consulted cast members. People familiar with that glamorous world helped shape the series both on screen and behind it. Viewing tip: compare the glamorous posts with the private behavior of the people creating them.
