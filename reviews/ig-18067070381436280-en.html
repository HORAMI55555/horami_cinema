Cocaine Bear

2023

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5Scare1/5

A bear. A bag of cocaine. Absolute chaos.

Synopsis

In 1985, a bear discovers cocaine dropped into a Georgia forest. Criminals searching for the drugs, police, visitors and children all find themselves in the animal's path. Their separate plans collapse into one chaotic struggle to survive. The film takes inspiration from an actual incident and turns it into a darkly comic rampage.

My thoughts

Why was this so entertaining?! The bear is out of control on cocaine and attacking people, but the comedy woven through it made the whole thing so much fun. I couldn't look away! An easy recommendation from me.

Cast

Keri Russell · O'Shea Jackson Jr. · Alden Ehrenreich · Margo Martindale · Ray Liotta

Trivia

An alternate ending exists and is included in the home-release extras. Behind-the-scenes features also explore rigs, effects makeup and some actors performing their own stunts—the practical work behind all that mayhem.
