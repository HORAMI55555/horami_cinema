Synopsis

After their father’s sudden death, OJ and Emerald notice unexplained phenomena above their Southern California ranch. The siblings, who supply horses for screen productions, plan to record the mystery. With a neighboring theme-park owner also involved, the pursuit of spectacle becomes terrifying.

My thoughts

This felt so fresh and was really entertaining! Horror covers all kinds of subjects, but making the UFO itself feel genuinely horrifying is impressive. The monkey storyline that appears along the way was interesting too. This is a fun horror movie that isn’t too scary. If you’re interested in aliens or UFOs, you’ll definitely enjoy it!

Cast

Daniel Kaluuya, Keke Palmer, Steven Yeun

Trivia

The siblings train animals for films, while their neighboring theme-park owner is a former child star. These occupations offer a key to the film: Universal’s official description connects its violence and risk to the American West and show business. Viewing tip: alongside guessing what is in the sky, track who wants to turn what into a spectacle. The relationships between those filming, those being watched, and those seeking profit can connect seemingly separate scenes.
