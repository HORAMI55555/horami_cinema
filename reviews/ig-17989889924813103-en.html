A possession opens the door to a buried secret.

Synopsis

Father Gabriele Amorth, the Vatican’s exorcist, investigates a boy’s terrifying possession. The case leads him toward secrets the Church has kept hidden for centuries. As he tries to save the child, the battle becomes a confrontation with both demonic evil and a concealed past.

My thoughts

The Vatican connection gave this so much atmosphere—it’s one of my personal favorites among the exorcism films I’ve seen. People might expect only scares, but these stories often become psychological battles too, which I find interesting. The horror is fairly strong, but if you like exorcism films, this is a great fit.

Cast

Russell Crowe

Trivia

Russell Crowe, who plays Father Amorth, won the Best Actor Oscar for Gladiator. The actor behind that arena warrior takes on a very different kind of battle here, as an exorcist.
