The Conjuring

2013

Scare4/5

A family moves in. The house already has a history.

Synopsis

In 1970s Rhode Island, strange disturbances unsettle the Perrons after they move into a farmhouse. Ed and Lorraine Warren listen to the family's accounts and investigate the history of their new home.

My thoughts

My number-one horror movie. If someone wants to try horror, this is the one I'd recommend first. If you're easily scared, consider it a serious test of courage! For someone who enjoys horror, it's more exhilarating than terrifying, like a roller coaster.

Cast

Vera Farmiga, Patrick Wilson, Lili Taylor

Trivia

The story draws on the real Warrens' account of the Perron case. A separate incident involving the doll Annabelle opens the film, introducing the sort of calls the couple handled before they met this family.
