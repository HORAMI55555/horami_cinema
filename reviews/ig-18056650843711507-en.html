Talk to Me

2022

Scare3.5/5

A party game with a ninety-second limit.

Synopsis

Grieving her mother, Mia joins friends in a ritual that summons spirits through an embalmed hand. They film the thrill of possession on their phones and treat it like a party game. When a session breaks the rules, the barrier between entertainment and something much more dangerous begins to disappear.

My thoughts

Finally watched it!!!! So much fun. The scare level was fairly typical horror for me, but the premise felt different and kept me completely engaged. The ending left me going “wait?” and then “oh, that is where it goes?!”—I wanted to read interpretations afterwards. Filming a séance on your phone is so modern. Those ninety minutes flew by. Definitely recommend; I had not enjoyed a horror this much in a while!

Cast

Sophie Wilde, Alexandra Jensen, Joe Bird

Trivia

Twin directors Danny and Michael Philippou came to feature horror after making videos on YouTube, a background highlighted by the Japanese distributor. Filming the ritual, showing it to friends and chasing their reactions turns an old supernatural idea into a recognisably modern social game. Watch the faces around the person being possessed as well as the possession itself. The moment laughter gives way to fear reveals how a group can lose its chance to stop something before it becomes dangerous.
