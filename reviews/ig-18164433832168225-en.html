A Classic Horror Story

2021

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5Scare3/5

How do you lose the entire road you were driving on?

Synopsis

Five strangers share a camper through southern Italy. After a crash, they wake surrounded by forest, with no sign of the road that brought them there. Their search for help leads to a strange wooden house standing alone in a clearing.

My thoughts

I thought this might be something like American Horror Story, which I love. Turns out it’s a completely separate movie, and I had fun with it. A “classic horror story” is pretty much the premise, just as the title says. I don’t often see a film approach horror from quite this angle. More cult stuff than ghosts.

Cast

Matilda Lutz, Francesco Russo, Will Merrick

Trivia

The whole film was shot in Puglia and Rome in five weeks. Its two directors conceived it as a tribute to Italian horror. When that lone house in the woods makes you think you know what comes next, make a mental note of exactly when you made the prediction.
