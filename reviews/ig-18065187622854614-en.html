Bodies Bodies Bodies

2022

☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★ 3/5 · Recommended

Once suspicion starts, friends become the threat.

Synopsis

As a hurricane approaches, a group of young adults gathers at a remote mansion and starts a murder game. When an actual body turns up, the party collapses into suspicion. Cut off from easy escape, the friends turn on one another as old resentments surface. Finding the killer becomes tangled up with deciding whom to trust.

My thoughts

This is horror that didn’t feel very scary to me. Young people play a game at a mansion, then paranoia and misunderstandings spiral into people dying. The ending is so ironic. A fun suspense-horror watch you can take fairly lightly.

Cast

Amandla Stenberg · Maria Bakalova · Rachel Sennott · Pete Davidson · Myha’la

Trivia

Phones, a headlamp and glow-stick accessories became part of the lighting design. Cinematographer Jasper Wolf even compared the light colours produced by different phones. What the characters carry helps determine how the entire scene looks.
