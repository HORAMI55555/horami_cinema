Can a checklist help her catch up with life?

Synopsis

Returning to school after treatment for an eating disorder, Mia feels that her friends have moved on without her. She creates a list of experiences to catch up on, from romance to parties. But trying to accelerate adolescence brings recovery, friendship and her own expectations into uncomfortable conflict.

My thoughts

Oh, this was darker and heavier than expected. I thought it would be a fairly typical overseas high-school series, but the eating-disorder theme made it feel different. It was one of my first experiences with a story like this and the struggle looked incredibly painful. Worth trying if the subject interests you; it felt quite raw.

Cast

Sophie Wilde, Lauryn Ajufo, Stephen Fry

Trivia

Ripley Parker created the series, with Sophie Wilde—also the lead in Talk to Me—playing Mia. Netflix’s introduction highlights its young creator, while Wilde brings a different form of emotional uncertainty to this coming-of-age story. Watch how the checklist can look both liberating and restrictive. Does completing an experience actually make Mia happier? Comparing her expression with her friends’ responses gives the familiar teen-adventure structure a more complicated emotional meaning.
