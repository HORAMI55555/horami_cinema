His sister is being held on an island of believers.

Synopsis

In 1905, Thomas travels to an island religious settlement to find his kidnapped sister. Posing as a convert, he discovers failing crops and rituals at odds with the prophet's promise of plenty.

My thoughts

I saw this ages ago and it popped back into my head yesterday. It's eerie occult horror, and what stuck with me was the people in that village. It gave me Midsommar vibes, but I had way more fun with this one. Some of the gore nearly finished me off. If you like a slightly old-fashioned kind of occult horror where the people are what scare you, this is your film.

Cast

Dan Stevens, Michael Sheen, Lucy Boynton

Trivia

Gareth Evans, director of The Raid, puts his protagonist in a very different bind here. Thomas has to pass as a believer, so even a small gesture in front of the congregation can be more dangerous than a fight.
