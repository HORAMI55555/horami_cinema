Alice Through the Looking Glass

2016

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★5/5

Can yesterday save a friend today?

Synopsis

Back through the mirror, Alice finds the Hatter fading. To discover what happened to his family, she seeks out Time himself.

My thoughts

I waited for this from the moment the sequel was announced and rushed to the theater when it finally arrived. Same world, slightly more grown-up feel. It moved me and reminded me what matters. Looking for a Disney recommendation? Start here!

Cast

Mia Wasikowska, Johnny Depp, Sacha Baron Cohen

Trivia

Time appears as a character you can actually talk to. Everyday expressions about running out of time or borrowing time take on a wonderfully literal meaning.
