An unwelcome guest on a quiet Christmas campus.

Synopsis

As a college campus empties for the holidays, Riley and her sorority sisters prepare for Christmas. A masked stalker begins targeting students, turning familiar surroundings into a threat. With their friends in danger and trust wearing thin, the women decide they cannot simply wait to become the next victims.

My thoughts

Honestly,, this felt way too predictable and I was bored. Some of it didn’t make much sense to me either. You can skip this one.

Cast

Trivia

The American-style campus was filmed in New Zealand, including the University of Otago. Shooting during the Southern Hemisphere’s winter helped create the Christmas setting.
