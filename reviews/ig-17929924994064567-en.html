Don't Listen

2020

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5Scare3.5/5

A voice from the walls turns up on a recording.

Synopsis

After his family moves into an old house, Eric hears voices nobody else can hear. When his father Daniel picks up a strange voice on a recording, he turns to a paranormal researcher.

My thoughts

Spanish horror really delivers here. I loved how it drew on witch trials. If horror isn’t your thing, this one might finish you off! The title had me expecting A Quiet Place, but The Autopsy of Jane Doe is a much closer comparison.

Cast

Rodolfo Sancho, Ana Fernández

Trivia

The Spanish title, Voces, means “voices.” Where the original names the sound, the English title gives you an instruction. Keep both in mind during the recording scenes and notice which one makes you lean closer.
