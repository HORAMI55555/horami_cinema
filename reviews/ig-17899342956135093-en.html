I Am a Killer

2018

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5

One account is never the whole story.

Synopsis

People convicted of murder recount their lives and crimes from prison. Their accounts are placed alongside testimony from relatives and others connected to the cases, as well as documentary evidence. Contradictions and omissions challenge the viewer to consider how the same event can be described in profoundly different ways.

My thoughts

I personally really liked this. If you’re seriously into crime or prison documentaries, it’s a great fit, though it won’t be for everyone. The prisoners tell their stories, but other accounts sometimes contradict them, making you rethink everything. I felt like I understood a little of what judging a case might be like. Interesting, and it shows how different people’s circumstances can be.

Cast

Participants: convicted prisoners, victims’ relatives, family members and others connected to the cases

Trivia

The team finishing Season 4 described using colour to guide attention and emotion. Bluer tones outside the prison and touches of green in the shadows helped create unease. Even in an interview-led documentary, colour grading quietly shapes the viewing experience.
