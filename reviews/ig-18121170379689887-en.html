Synopsis

Raised as a strict vegetarian, Justine begins veterinary school. After eating raw meat during a hazing ritual, she develops an unfamiliar appetite. Adjusting to student life, negotiating her relationship with her sister and discovering her changing body become entangled with disturbing desires in this coming-of-age body horror film.

My thoughts

Well, that was different. To me, it focused much more on cannibalism than on the story. If that’s your thing, I’d recommend it, but otherwise you might not get much enjoyment out of it, haha.

Cast

Garance Marillier · Ella Rumpf · Rabah Nait Oufella · Laurent Lucas

Trivia

Julia Ducournau wrote and directed the film. Viewing tip: pay attention to Justine’s desire to fit in as well as her appetite. The initiation rituals and changes in her body echo one another.
