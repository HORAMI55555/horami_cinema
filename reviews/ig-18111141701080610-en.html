An ordinary life. An extraordinary family secret.

Synopsis

Mavi's ordinary life in Germany is upended when she discovers her connection to a wealthy Turkish family. In Istanbul, a glamorous new world comes with expectations about who she should become. Torn between her familiar life and a family she never knew, she must decide what love and belonging mean on her own terms.

My thoughts

This was fun! An easy watch when you want something to pass the time. Imagine living your normal life and suddenly finding out you're the granddaughter of a super-rich Turkish family. That premise alone is fun to think about! I don't come across this kind of Turkish setting that often, so give it a go if you want that atmosphere.

Cast

Beritan Balcı · Sinan Güleç · Serkan Çayoğlu · Meral Perin

Trivia

Netflix lists Hamburg, Istanbul and Cappadocia as filming locations. The production's real-world settings help establish the different places and cultural expectations pulling Mavi in competing directions.
