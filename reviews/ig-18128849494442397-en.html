When Evil Lurks

2023

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★2/5Scare2.5/5

Trying to remove the evil only spreads it.

Synopsis

Two brothers in a rural community discover a man possessed by evil. Their attempt to remove the threat instead sets a disastrous chain of events in motion. As they try to protect their families, the danger keeps spreading, and each wrong decision leaves fewer places to hide.

My thoughts

It wasn’t as scary as people said, and I didn’t find the story particularly elaborate either. Basically, a mysterious demonic infection starts and won’t stop. For that kind of spreading-threat story, Bird Box is still my favorite. The really nasty imagery was impressively done though lol—so gross. It didn’t register as gory to me, just disgusting. Otherwise fairly standard horror for me, with such a miserable ending. Ugh, no!

Cast

Ezequiel Rodríguez · Demián Salomón · Silvina Sabater

Trivia

It won Best Feature Film at Sitges in 2023, becoming the first Latin American film to take that award in the festival’s 56-year history.
