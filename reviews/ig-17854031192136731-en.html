As the Gods Will

2014

Scare1/5

Red light, green light. This time nobody laughs when you lose.

Synopsis

During an ordinary lesson, a daruma doll takes over the classroom and starts a lethal children's game. Shun and the other students have to discover which familiar rules still apply before they can hope to leave school.

My thoughts

Not sure why this was filed under horror, but I'll include it anyway. It's more gory than anything else, and that's why I didn't like it.

Cast

Sota Fukushi, Hirona Yamazaki, Ryunosuke Kamiki

Trivia

Takashi Miike adapted the manga by Muneyuki Kaneshiro and Akeji Fujimura. Familiar childhood games need little explanation: as soon as the daruma turns, the audience understands the danger of moving a muscle.
