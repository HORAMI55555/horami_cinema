テケテケ

2009

Scare2/5

You've crossed the tracks. The sound keeps coming.

Synopsis

After a friend's death near a railway crossing, Kana hears about Teke Teke, a figure that chases people on its hands. A sighting of her own sends Kana and her cousin looking for the story behind the legend.

My thoughts

Japanese horror doesn't usually scare me that much, but I loved seeing the famous Teke Teke legend turned into a movie!

Cast

Yuko Oshima, Mami Yamasaki, Yoko Chosokabe

Trivia

Koji Shiraishi directed, and the creature has its own acting credit: Yoko Chosokabe plays Teke Teke. An urban legend usually conjured up by an eerie noise has a performer embodying it on screen.
