A Silent Voice

2016

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5

Some words take years to find.

Synopsis

As a child, Shoya bullied Shoko, a deaf girl who transferred to his school. Years later, he learns sign language and goes looking for her. Their reunion brings former classmates back into each other’s lives, each carrying a different memory of the same classroom.

My thoughts

It’s so rare for me to watch anime, but I’m glad I watched this. It’s a coming-of-age film about bullying and disability that felt close to things that could happen in a real classroom. It brought back a feeling from my own school days that I hadn’t thought about in ages. The artwork and music left me feeling lighter.

Cast

Miyu Irino, Saori Hayami, Aoi Yuki

Trivia

Listen around the notes in the piano score and you can hear the instrument itself. Composer Kensuke Ushio put a microphone inside the old piano at his family home, improvised for hours, then rearranged fragments of the recordings. The little mechanical noises he had heard since childhood became part of the music.
