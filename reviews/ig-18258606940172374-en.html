Some Like It Rare

2021

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5Scare1/5

Their bestselling ham has a horrifying secret.

Synopsis

A butcher and his wife face a failing business and a faltering marriage. After killing an activist who attacked their shop, they turn the body into ham, which is accidentally sold to customers. Its unexpected popularity offers a grotesque solution to their troubles—and a temptation to go much further.

My thoughts

The idea is hilarious, honestly. It is packed with jokes at vegans’ expense, so I definitely would not choose it for a vegan friend! Funny horror is relatively rare, which makes this worth recommending. It is uncomplicated, easy to watch and over before you know it.

Cast

Fabrice Eboué, Marina Foïs

Trivia

Fabrice Eboué, a comedian, both directs and stars. The Japanese distributor highlights its combination of marital comedy and human hunting. The original French title is Barbaque, while the English-language title is Some Like It Rare. Watch how the couple discuss an appalling problem with the rhythms of business planning or everyday marital negotiation. The mismatch between their conversational tone and the situation creates much of the uncomfortable humour, beyond the shock of the premise alone.
