Britney vs Spears

2021

Offstage, who was calling the shots?

Synopsis

Documents and interviews trace the conservatorship that governed Britney Spears's life and career, asking how much of a voice she had in decisions about herself.

My thoughts

I'm shocked. I've loved Britney since I was little; she was the first English-language artist I listened to and I even had her perfume. I had no idea things were this bad. The conservatorship was hard to follow, but the sadness and injustice came through. I'd give it two stars if I didn't know Britney. Her dad made me angry enough to give it three.

Cast

Erin Lee Carr, Jenny Eliscu, Britney Spears (archive footage)

Trivia

Director Carr and journalist Eliscu take us through their own investigation. The structure follows a trail of records rather than building around a new sit-down interview with Spears.
