Don't Worry Darling

2022

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5Scare1/5

A perfect life leaves no room for questions.

Synopsis

Alice enjoys a comfortable life with her husband Jack in the desert community of Victory. The men work on a classified project while the wives enjoy luxurious homes and leisure. Unsettling events lead Alice to question the system behind their happiness, bringing her into conflict with the town’s order.

My thoughts

Wow, that was good! First of all, what a cast, haha. The lead from Midsommar and Harry Styles—amazing, and their acting is so good. It’s an engaging mystery-style horror that isn’t scary, the kind that pulls you further and further into its world. The men in this movie were unbelievably selfish and awful! Recommended!

Cast

Florence Pugh, Harry Styles, Chris Pine, Olivia Wilde

Trivia

Olivia Wilde both directs and acts, while the screenplay comes from her Booksmart collaborator Katie Silberman. The official synopsis describes Victory as a company town that meets residents’ needs in return for discretion and unquestioning loyalty. Viewing tip: consider its beautiful lifestyle alongside those conditions. Thinking about how comfort can obscure what people surrender adds tension to the orderly everyday scenes.
