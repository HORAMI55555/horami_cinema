Tall Girl 2

2022

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5

She landed the lead. Her toughest critic is already in her head.

Synopsis

Jodi wins the lead in her school musical. She thought she’d learned to stand tall, but the closer opening night gets, the louder her doubts become. Between rehearsals and less time with her boyfriend Jack, getting what she wanted turns out to be the tricky part.

My thoughts

Oh, there’s the Swedish exchange student I mentioned! This sequel has a slightly different feel from the first one, but it’s a fun American teen movie. I’d recommend it.

Cast

Ava Michelle, Griffin Gluck, Sabrina Carpenter

Trivia

The school puts on Bye Bye Birdie. Director Emily Ting credited Ava Michelle’s background in singing and dance as a reason for bringing a musical into the sequel. Jodi is stepping out of her comfort zone just as the actor playing her gets to step into hers.
