Night Teeth

2021

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5

Tonight’s passengers are beautiful—and deadly.

Synopsis

Young driver Benny picks up two women for a night of parties across Los Angeles. What looks like a glamorous job changes completely when he discovers who his passengers really are. Pulled into the dangerous world beneath the city’s nightlife, he must make desperate choices to survive.

My thoughts

This was pretty entertaining!! Zoe is so cute. Characters named Zoe on Netflix always seem to be the cutest, haha. It’s about vampires and humans who have an agreement not to interfere with each other. The vampires were living in mansions out in secluded areas, but they break the agreement and come into the city! Above all, the two vampire girls are so cute and cool. If you like a girl-crush vibe, they’ll be your type. Recommended ❣️ It’s a movie, so you’ll finish it quickly.

Cast

Jorge Lendeborg Jr., Debby Ryan, Lucy Fry

Trivia

Although the story moves through Los Angeles nightlife, Netflix announced that principal photography would begin in New Orleans. Adam Randall, director of iBoy and I See You, directs from Brent Dillon’s script. Viewing tip: take the driver’s perspective and compare how his passengers behave inside the car and at their destinations. The balance of power between those choosing the stops and the person taking them there brings out the danger beneath the fashion and night scenery.
