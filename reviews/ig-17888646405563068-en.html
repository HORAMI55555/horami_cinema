I Feel Pretty

2018

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3.5/5

Same face. A whole new confidence.

Synopsis

After an accident at the gym, insecure Renee becomes convinced she has transformed into a stunning beauty. Her appearance has not changed, but her confidence has—and she starts taking chances in love and at work. How far can that new self-belief take her? This comedy explores the gap between how we look and how we see ourselves.

My thoughts

This was so fun, and such a lovely film! Some parts reminded me of The Devil Wears Prada. It really gives you a confidence boost. Loved it—definitely recommend!

Cast

Amy Schumer · Michelle Williams · Rory Scovel · Emily Ratajkowski

Trivia

Abby Kohn and Marc Silverstein share writing and directing credits. Viewing tip: watch how Amy Schumer changes Renee’s posture and behavior while her appearance stays the same.
