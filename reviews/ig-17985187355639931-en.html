Night Swim

2024

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★2/5Scare2/5

A dream pool. A nightmare after dark.

Synopsis

After illness ends his baseball career, Ray moves with his wife and children into a house with a backyard pool. Swimming seems to offer a way back to health, but unsettling incidents begin to trouble the family. Their new home’s most inviting feature hides something far more dangerous beneath the surface.

My thoughts

A swimming pool as the main horror attraction—that was new to me! Interesting idea, but I felt like the usual haunted-object formula had just been given a pool-shaped makeover. You can already guess someone will be targeted. It wasn’t very scary for me, though, so beginners might enjoy it as a light horror watch.

Cast

Wyatt Russell · Kerry Condon · Amélie Hoeferle

Trivia

The creatures had to be designed to withstand underwater filming, not just look frightening on dry land. The film also turns the familiar pool game Marco Polo into a scare sequence—using the things that normally make a pool fun as its horror material.
