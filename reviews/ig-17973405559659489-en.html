Haunted

2018

Sometimes the scariest thing is your friend’s face as they start telling the story.

Synopsis

Someone sits down with the people closest to them to describe an experience they still can’t explain. Dramatized scenes take us inside the memory, then the camera returns to the family and friends hearing the account. Each episode brings a different storyteller.

My thoughts

Hearing people tell their own scary stories alongside the reenactments made this feel incredibly real to me. There’s something different about the scares in this one compared with other paranormal reality shows. Highly recommend it. Each story wraps up in about 20 to 30 minutes, and there’s so much more to watch that I could keep going forever.

Cast

Participants recounting their experiences, their loved ones, and reenactment actors, varying by episode

Trivia

The reconstruction can end, but the storyteller still has to face the people in the room. Their reactions become part of the ghost story. The format also travelled to Haunted: Latin America, where a different group of storytellers recounts experiences brought to life in reenactments.
