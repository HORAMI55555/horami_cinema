Her hospitality comes with strings attached.

Synopsis

Sue Ann buys alcohol for a group of teenagers and invites them to party in her basement. They nickname her Ma and welcome the freedom she offers. But her enthusiasm becomes increasingly possessive, and the apparently perfect hangout starts to feel like a place with no easy exit.

My thoughts

Pretty interesting! It feels like a long revenge story about a woman deeply affected by being bullied when she was younger. Personally, the last scene made me a little sad—it left me thinking she really had loved him.

Cast

Octavia Spencer · Diana Silvers · Juliette Lewis · Luke Evans

Trivia

Octavia Spencer reunites with Tate Taylor, who directed her in The Help. This time their collaboration moves from a human drama to a thriller about disturbing obsession. Spencer also serves as an executive producer.
