Synopsis

In a world stalked by creatures that hunt by sound, a family signs to one another and walks barefoot on paths of sand. They have built a quiet life, but a baby is on the way and silence won't always be theirs to control.

My thoughts

Maybe I'd heard too much about this one already? It didn't scare me, but I had a good time.

Cast

Emily Blunt, John Krasinski, Millicent Simmonds

Trivia

Millicent Simmonds, who plays Regan, is deaf, and her castmates learned sign language for the film. Their exchanges suggest a shared family language that existed long before staying quiet became a matter of survival.
