Synopsis

A comet’s fragments begin striking Earth, turning an ordinary day into a global emergency. John Garrity, his wife Allison and their young son set out toward a possible refuge. With panic spreading and travel becoming increasingly difficult, staying together becomes as urgent as reaching safety before time runs out.

My thoughts

I watched this two years ago, and I still remember it so vividly. That’s how much of an impact it had. A family tries to reach Greenland as the world is falling apart, but getting there is unbelievably stressful. I honestly thought my heart couldn’t take it. If you love huge-scale disaster movies or sci-fi, this is such a good pick!!!

Cast

Gerard Butler, Morena Baccarin, Roger Dale Floyd

Trivia

Director Ric Roman Waugh reunited with Gerard Butler after Angel Has Fallen. This time, their lead is a father trying to protect his family rather than a presidential bodyguard.
