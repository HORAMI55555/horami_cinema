Behind Her Eyes

2021

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5

The closer you get, the less you know.

Synopsis

Single mother Louise begins an affair with her psychiatrist boss, David, then unexpectedly befriends his wife, Adele. Caught between the couple, she becomes increasingly curious about their behavior and their past. An already complicated relationship grows into a web of secrets where appearances cannot be trusted.

My thoughts

This story is wild... I genuinely did not see that ending coming. Please go into it knowing as little as possible. For me it’s more suspense, mystery and psychological tension than horror. I’m still shocked.

Cast

Simona Brown · Tom Bateman · Eve Hewson · Robert Aramayo

Trivia

Adapted from Sarah Pinborough’s novel, the series is written by Steve Lightfoot and Angela LaManna and produced by Left Bank Pictures, also known for The Crown. Viewing tip: avoid plot explanations before watching.
