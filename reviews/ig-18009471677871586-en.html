Saving her friend means crossing a line.

Synopsis

Eun-su carries childhood trauma from witnessing violence at home. When she learns that her best friend Hui-su is being abused by her husband, she resolves to help. Desperation leads them to plan his murder, a decision that places their friendship and hopes for escape under extraordinary pressure.

My thoughts

I found this Korean drama because it seemed popular, but it was even more compelling than I expected... It made me wonder how much it reflects real social conditions in Korea. The actress playing the abused wife is so good I couldn’t look away. It portrays a very dark situation; it’s intense, but worth watching. Thinking about people around the world actually living like this is heartbreaking. I was happy with the ending—it left me feeling relieved.

Cast

Jeon So-nee · Lee You-mi · Jang Seung-jo · Lee Mu-saeng

Trivia

Adapted from Japanese author Hideo Okuda’s novel Naomi & Kanako and directed by Lee Jeong-lim. Viewing tip: watch the responses of people around the abuse, not only the two friends’ decisions.
