Our House

2018

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★2.5/5Scare2.5/5

His invention was supposed to transmit electricity. Something else gets through.

Synopsis

After his parents die in an accident, Ethan takes care of his younger brother and sister. At night he works in the garage on a device for wireless electricity. When strange things begin happening around the house, the siblings start to hope their parents have found a way back to them.

My thoughts

This one was fun! A homemade machine generates a field that draws in evil spirits, but the family thinks it’s their parents coming back. I liked that setup. It’s manageable even if you’re not great with horror, and you still get that haunted-house feeling.

Cast

Thomas Mann, Percy Hynes White, Kate Moyer

Trivia

An earlier film, Ghost from the Machine, is credited as the basis for Our House. The two titles make an interesting viewing experiment. One has you watching the apparatus for signs of trouble. The other has you looking around the rooms the siblings come home to every night.
