Alice in Borderland

2020

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5

Imagine hearing only your own footsteps at Shibuya Crossing.

Synopsis

Arisu and his friends duck out of sight at Shibuya Station. When they emerge, the crowds have vanished. After nightfall, lights lead them to a game tied to a playing card. Their lives depend on finding the right move before the clock runs out.

My thoughts

I jumped in at season two and had such a good time. If you love Squid Game, I think you’ll love this. The games keep putting people up against each other, but lots of them were more intricate than I expected, and some were unlike anything I’d seen. That gave it the edge over Squid Game for me. Plus, if you live in Tokyo, half the fun is going, “Wait, I know that spot!”

Cast

Kento Yamazaki, Tao Tsuchiya, Nijiro Murakami

Trivia

Think you recognize Shibuya in episode one? The deserted crossing was filmed on an outdoor set in Ashikaga, more than 100 km from the real intersection. The run through the crowd, the stop in the station restroom and the walk back out into silence unfold in a single take lasting over four minutes. On a rewatch, follow where the camera points as the crowd disappears.
