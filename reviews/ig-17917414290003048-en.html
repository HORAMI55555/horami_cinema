Searching for belonging in a pink-colored world.

Synopsis

People with unusual bodies and appearances navigate loneliness, desire and the wish to be accepted. Their separate lives gradually intersect inside a striking pastel-colored world. This Spanish ensemble film uses stylized fantasy to question who gets to define beauty and normality, and what it takes to find a place to belong.

My thoughts

Wow,, I’d never seen this subject approached in such a surprisingly casual way. It follows several people whose bodies look different, with their stories unfolding separately. I kept imagining how difficult their lives must feel and got completely absorbed in their world. I was glad that, to me, most of them seemed to reach the best ending they could. Really recommend it—it flew by.

Cast

Ana Polvorosa, Candela Peña, Macarena Gómez

Trivia

Eduardo Casanova’s feature directorial debut screened in the Panorama section of the 2017 Berlin International Film Festival, introducing his distinctive visual world to an international audience.
