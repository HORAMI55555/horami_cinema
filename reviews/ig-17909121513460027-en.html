La Dolce Villa

2025

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5

A one-euro house. A whole new chapter.

Synopsis

Eric travels to Italy to stop his daughter from spending her savings renovating a crumbling villa she bought for one euro. But the trip becomes more than a disagreement over a house. New people, an unfamiliar pace of life and an unexpected romance start to challenge his plans. Restoring the villa may also give father and daughter room to rebuild their relationship.

My thoughts

I'd definitely recommend this! It's set in Italy, with an old house bought for almost nothing and a plan to renovate it into a business. The local atmosphere really made me feel like I'd travelled there. Family love, personal conflicts, all sorts of emotions... I came away feeling completely satisfied.

Cast

Scott Foley · Maia Reficco · Violante Placido · Giuseppe Futia

Trivia

The production filmed in Rome, eastern Lazio and Tuscany, while the villa interiors were built at Rome's Cinecittà Studios. The house on screen combines real Italian locations with purpose-built interiors.
