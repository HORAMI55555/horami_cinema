The Bucket List

2007

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5

What would make your remaining days count?

Synopsis

Billionaire Edward and mechanic Carter meet in a hospital room after receiving life-changing diagnoses. They make a list of things they want to do before they die and set off on an extravagant journey. Beyond the spectacular adventures, unfinished relationships and quieter wishes begin to reshape their idea of a life well lived.

My thoughts

I watched because it is so famous and everyone says it is wonderful! If I knew I was dying, I would definitely want to travel as much as possible too. The optimistic older guys were fun. Maybe I missed what makes this so special, though—I enjoyed it, but did not think it was exceptionally amazing.

Cast

Jack Nicholson, Morgan Freeman

Trivia

A bucket list means the things someone hopes to do before they die. Rob Reiner directs Jack Nicholson and Morgan Freeman in this unlikely travelling partnership. A useful viewing angle is the difference between the wishes on their list: some are spectacular experiences that money can arrange, while others depend on relationships no amount of money can fix. Watching how those two kinds of wish sit alongside each other adds another layer to the globe-trotting adventure—and invites you to consider your own list.
