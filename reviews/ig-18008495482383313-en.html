Tall Girl

2019

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5

For once, her crush is the one she has to look up at.

Synopsis

After years of jokes about her height, high school student Jodi tries to keep a low profile. Then Stig, a tall Swedish exchange student, walks into class. She thinks she’s finally met her match, until she learns he’s staying with the family of her longtime best friend.

My thoughts

I kept hearing good things about this and finally got to see it! The main character is a really tall girl, and I was personally so happy when the new kid turned out to be Swedish. The premise felt fresh, and there was a lot to take away from it. Definitely recommend!

Cast

Ava Michelle, Luke Eisner, Griffin Gluck

Trivia

Netflix conducted a worldwide casting search for Jodi. The part went to dancer and model Ava Michelle, making her feature film debut. There’s a lovely irony in a newcomer being picked out of a worldwide search to play someone who wishes people would stop noticing her.
