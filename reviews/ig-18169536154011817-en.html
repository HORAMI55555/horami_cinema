Bird Box

2018

Scare2/5

Two children, a river, and one rule: keep the blindfold on.

Synopsis

Something outside drives people to their deaths when they see it. Malorie blindfolds two children and takes them downriver towards a promised refuge, where sound alone must guide them past danger.

My thoughts

I really liked this. It has such a distinctive world and doesn't feel like the usual horror films. Maybe the sight-based counterpart to A Quiet Place?

Cast

Sandra Bullock, Trevante Rhodes, John Malkovich

Trivia

Susanne Bier adapted Josh Malerman's novel. The birds of the title have a practical job: their reactions give people who dare not look a portable warning that something may be close.
