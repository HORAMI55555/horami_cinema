Dare Me

2019

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★2/5

A team built on trust, and secrets that could break it.

Synopsis

An ambitious new coach arrives at a high-school cheerleading squad, unsettling the close bond between Addy and Beth. Admiration and resistance pull the friends in different directions. Behind the polished routines, loyalty, control and hidden lives intertwine, drawing the girls into increasingly dangerous circumstances.

My thoughts

A dark series! Not quite like 13 Reasons Why, but the mood stays heavy throughout. Romance, friendship and whatever is happening with the coach all blur together. The ending left me going “what?” and it is sad that it was cancelled without more. Recommended if you like dark stories—you can really sink into this atmosphere.

Cast

Willa Fitzgerald, Herizen Guardiola, Marlo Kelly

Trivia

The source is Megan Abbott’s novel, with the author’s website documenting its USA Network adaptation. Cheerleading creates an interesting contrast: teammates must physically trust and support each other even when their private loyalties are unstable. Watch how people who coordinate perfectly during a routine behave away from it. Who occupies the centre, and who watches whom? Those patterns help reveal the tension in relationships that cannot easily be labelled as friendship, admiration or something else.
