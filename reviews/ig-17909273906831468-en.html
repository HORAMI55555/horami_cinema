Old People

2022

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★2/5Scare2.5/5

A family celebration becomes a generational nightmare.

Synopsis

A woman returns home with her two children for her sister’s wedding. What should be a happy family reunion turns into a violent assault by elderly people. As the visit becomes a fight for survival, she desperately tries to keep her children alive.

My thoughts

The title says it all: this horror movie centers on old people, but I kept thinking, why are you all so gloomy and resentful of young people!? I sort of get what it’s trying to say, but you were young once too, so getting jealous of young people and trying to kill them is not okay. It made me mad. The scares are decent and the story is easy to follow. The premise is unusual, so it does feel fresh.

Cast

Melika Foroutan, Stephan Luca, Anna Unterberger

Trivia

Netflix classifies this as German horror and highlights a clash of generations. The premise turns a family reunion for a wedding into conflict between age groups. Viewing tip: look beyond the attackers to consider whom the act of protecting a family includes or excludes. The blunt title, paired with an occasion meant for celebration, can make familiar relationships feel unsettling.
