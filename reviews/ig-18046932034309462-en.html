His House

2020

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★2/5Scare3/5

They finally have a home. Something else got there first.

Synopsis

Bol and Rial escape South Sudan and are assigned a house in England. As Bol tries to settle in, something stirs behind the walls. Rial has her own idea about what followed them there.

My thoughts

I found this in the top three on an American horror list. It didn’t scare me, though the story held my interest. Spoilers: what stayed with me was the couple taking another woman’s child as they fled, losing her on the crossing, and then discovering that what they did had followed them all the way to England.

Cast

Ṣọpẹ́ Dìrísù, Wunmi Mosaku, Matt Smith

Trivia

This was writer-director Remi Weekes’s first feature. It gives the haunted-house question “Why don’t they leave?” a particularly sharp answer. The couple’s future depends on staying at their assigned address. Watch how an ordinary house key can signify both safety and confinement.
