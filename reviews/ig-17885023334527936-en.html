The Boy in the Striped Pyjamas

2008

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5Scare2/5

On either side of the fence, two boys are the same age.

Synopsis

During World War II, Bruno moves with his family when his officer father is reassigned. Beyond a fence he meets Shmuel. Their conversations grow into a friendship, though Bruno does not understand what separates them.

My thoughts

This kept coming up on must-watch lists and, strangely, even on a horror list, so I finally watched it. It’s incredibly sad. Knowing the setting, I could sense where it was heading, but the ending still shocked me. It made the war feel immediate to me. I’d recommend it.

Cast

Asa Butterfield, Jack Scanlon, Vera Farmiga

Trivia

Mark Herman both adapted John Boyne’s novel and directed the film. Listen to the adult conversations Bruno overhears: a viewer can understand the implications of a word long before the boy does. An ordinary family exchange can become one of the most uneasy moments.
