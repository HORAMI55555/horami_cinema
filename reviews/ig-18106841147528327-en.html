Heretic

A friendly welcome. A reason to leave.

Synopsis

Two young missionaries visit a man who seems eager to discuss their faith. Mr. Reed welcomes them in, but his curiosity gradually becomes a means of control. What begins as a conversation turns into a test they never agreed to take. As his questions become increasingly unsettling, the women must work out what he wants—and how to get away.

My thoughts

I was so excited for a psychological horror film about religion, but this was pretty hard to follow, and I didn't really understand the ending!! It felt like you needed to be seriously into philosophy or religion to fully enjoy it. One scene near the end really shocked me, though. The psychological unease was there throughout, and I did enjoy that part. If religious horror is your thing, this might be much more your kind of film.

Cast

Hugh Grant · Sophie Thatcher · Chloe East

Trivia

The directors traced some of the film's discomfort to a location-scouting visit. A welcoming elderly couple told them an asteroid would soon end the world, suddenly making a cozy living room feel threatening. That unsettling shift in an ordinary conversation helped inform the film.
