The Jungle Book

2016

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5

In the jungle that raised him, a boy must find where he belongs.

Synopsis

Raised by wolves, Mowgli is forced to leave his jungle home when the tiger Shere Khan threatens him. Guided by the panther Bagheera and bear Baloo, he encounters animals both friendly and dangerous. His journey challenges him to understand his humanity and his bond with the forest.

My thoughts

I saw this one at the cinema too!! I remember how well it captured the feeling of living in the jungle, and how fresh that felt. The boy’s everyday life, lovingly raised by the forest animals, is so precious!

Cast

Neel Sethi, Bill Murray (voice), Ben Kingsley (voice), Idris Elba (voice)

Trivia

Directed by Jon Favreau, the film draws on Rudyard Kipling’s stories and Disney’s animated adaptation. According to Disney, it combines live action with photorealistic CGI animals and environments. Neel Sethi plays Mowgli. Viewing tip: notice how each animal’s scale and eye level communicate its relationship with the boy. The visual depth helps convey a forest where protectors and threats share the same space.
