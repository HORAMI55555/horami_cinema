Black Mirror: Bandersnatch

2018

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5

Are you really the one making the choices?

Synopsis

In 1984, young programmer Stefan sets out to adapt a branching novel into a video game. As work progresses, everyday decisions begin to blur into questions about reality and control. This interactive Black Mirror film was built around viewers choosing actions that send its protagonist down different paths.

My thoughts

I was excited because it’s a Black Mirror film, but I finished it feeling pretty confused, so it was a mixed experience. The interesting part is making choices throughout the film. Sometimes I got sent back to try again, which gradually became frustrating. Still, it’s an unusual idea—some people will really enjoy it!

Cast

Fionn Whitehead · Will Poulter · Asim Chaudhry

Trivia

Netflix developed a tool called Branch Manager to handle the branching structure. Editors could test choices on laptops and check how one segment flowed into another. Making the film meant developing the system that made its storytelling possible, too.
