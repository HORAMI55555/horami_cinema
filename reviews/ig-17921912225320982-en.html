The Conjuring: The Devil Made Me Do It

2021

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4.5/5Scare4/5

How do you prove possession in a courtroom?

Synopsis

Ed and Lorraine Warren witness a young boy’s exorcism. When Arne, a young man who was there, later kills someone and claims possession, the Warrens retrace the events that connect him to the boy.

My thoughts

The Conjuring is one of my top three horror series. I loved the first film, the second dipped a little for me, and this one won me right back. Proper scares and a story I actually wanted to follow. Knowing there was a real case behind it made me even more curious. My horror-averse friends would be hiding behind the couch. For me it was heaven.

Cast

Patrick Wilson, Vera Farmiga, Ruairi O’Connor

Trivia

The priest’s arrival echoes The Exorcist. Before he has even crossed the threshold, the lit window and the silhouette outside have told us there’s something waiting for him. Classic horror fans get their first treat right on the doorstep.
