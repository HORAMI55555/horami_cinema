Yes Day

2021

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★2/5

One day when the parents have to say yes.

Synopsis

Realising how often they say no, two parents agree to a day of saying yes to their children’s requests within agreed boundaries. What starts as a playful family experiment becomes an unpredictable adventure. As plans spiral, parents and children discover things about one another that their normal routines tend to hide.

My thoughts

I love Yes Man, so I expected something similar, but this was a little different! Still, a fun family film that lifts your mood and passes the time nicely. I was especially happy to spot the actress who plays Wednesday!

Cast

Jennifer Garner, Edgar Ramírez, Jenna Ortega

Trivia

The film is based on Amy Krouse Rosenthal’s picture book. Jennifer Garner has also spoken to Netflix about practising Yes Days with her own family, giving the screen premise a connection to an offscreen tradition. A useful detail to watch is how the exchange runs both ways: the children also learn about their parents. Beyond the size of the outrageous requests, notice who compromises for someone else. Those choices reveal small changes in the family beneath the noisy adventure.
