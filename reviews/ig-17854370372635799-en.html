Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5

A marriage ends. The family story keeps going.

Synopsis

Actor Nicole and director Charlie separate. As they negotiate where their son will live, even questions they once settled together start passing through lawyers.

My thoughts

So deep. Honestly, some of it was too complicated for me to fully grasp. Marriage is hard. There are so many reasons things can fall apart.

Cast

Scarlett Johansson, Adam Driver, Laura Dern

Trivia

The opening pairs two accounts of what the spouses love about each other. Hearing those details first makes it painful to recognize the same traits during later arguments.
