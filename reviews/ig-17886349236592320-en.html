The concert is a trap. The exits are watched.

Synopsis

At a pop concert with his daughter, Cooper notices an unusually heavy police presence. Behind the arena’s excitement, an operation to capture a wanted person is underway. A father-daughter outing turns into a tense situation in which every exit and every movement matters. The crowds offer both cover and another obstacle.

My thoughts

This was so entertaining!!!!!! I was enjoying the sweet father-daughter outing, and then—wait, YOU’RE the killer?! From that point I couldn’t look away. Can he get out of that concert venue? What about his daughter?! Seriously recommend this one!

Cast

Josh Hartnett · Ariel Donoghue · Saleka Shyamalan · Hayley Mills

Trivia

M. Night Shyamalan wrote and directed the film, with cinematography by Sayombhu Mukdeeprom, whose credits include Call Me by Your Name. Viewing tip: look at how corridors and exits are framed alongside the spectacle onstage.
