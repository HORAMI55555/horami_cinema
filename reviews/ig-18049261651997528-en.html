Synopsis

Old friends gather for a pre-wedding party. An estranged member of the group arrives carrying a machine that lets them swap bodies, and they agree to play a game. Familiar faces suddenly hide unfamiliar motives as jealousy, desire and old secrets turn the evening into a tangled psychological nightmare.

My thoughts

This was fun!!!!! Horror? Sort of? I didn’t find it scary, but the setup felt so fresh. They use a machine to swap what’s inside their bodies and turn it into a game. The ending had me going, wait, WHAT,, but I really enjoyed it!

Cast

Brittany O’Grady, James Morosini, Alycia Debnam-Carey, Gavin Leatherwood

Trivia

Actor Colman Domingo executive-produced the film alongside his husband and creative partner, Raúl Domingo—a behind-the-camera connection you might not expect.
