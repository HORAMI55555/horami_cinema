A Nightmare on Elm Street

1984

Scare4/5

Morning might save them. Staying awake is another matter.

Synopsis

Nancy and her friends dream of the same man with blades on his glove. When injuries sustained in sleep persist after waking, Nancy starts probing a history their parents have good reason to keep quiet.

My thoughts

This is the very film that got me into horror. I was six when I saw it and it absolutely terrified me, the kind that stays with you. I want to watch it again sometime.

Cast

Heather Langenkamp, Robert Englund, Johnny Depp

Trivia

The 1984 film was Johnny Depp's feature debut. It's Nancy who works out the rules of the nightmare; the future star is at her side as her boyfriend Glen.
