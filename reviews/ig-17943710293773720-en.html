JU-ON: Origins

2020

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★2/5Scare3/5

The people move on. The house stays.

Synopsis

An actress hears footsteps at night, and a paranormal researcher follows reports of strange incidents. Seemingly separate encounters across the years lead back to the same house in Tokyo.

My thoughts

I’d pretty much given up on Japanese horror scaring me, but this turned up on a horror ranking, so I tried it. Still not scary enough for me! If you’re easing into horror, though, there’s just enough of a chill. Six episodes in roughly two and a half hours makes it an easy one to try.

Cast

Yoshiyoshi Arakawa, Yuina Kuroshima, Ririka

Trivia

Netflix tells this chapter of Ju-On in six episodes. If you watch them together, keep track of who has already encountered the house and when. A room can be brand new to the person crossing its threshold even as you’re silently begging them to stay outside.
