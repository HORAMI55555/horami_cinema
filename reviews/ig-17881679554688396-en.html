Hell Girl

2019

Scare1/5

At midnight, enter the name of the person you want sent to hell.

Synopsis

A website opens only at midnight. Enter an enemy's name and Ai Enma will offer a terrible bargain. Two girls brought together by music find out what it costs to have a grievance answered.

My thoughts

I loved this so much as a kid that I watched the anime and read the manga! It's fun, it isn't scary, and I'd definitely recommend it.

Cast

Tina Tamashiro, Nana Mori, Sawa Nimura

Trivia

Tina Tamashiro plays the anime's Ai Enma in live action. The bargain isn't sealed until the client pulls a thread from a straw doll, making one tiny movement more consequential than any spectacle that follows.
