Riverdale

2017

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★5/5

Everyone knows his name. Nobody wants to talk about that summer.

Synopsis

A new school year begins after the summer Jason Blossom disappears. Archie is drawn to music, Betty is sorting out her feelings for her old friend, and Veronica is new in town. While they try to get on with school, the disappearance keeps pulling their families’ secrets into view.

My thoughts

I had so much fun with this. I get why it’s such a big teen show. Romance, mystery, horror, sci-fi: somehow it finds room for all of them. Musical numbers pop up too, so I’d recommend it to anyone who loved The Greatest Showman. The songs are so good and the plot takes some wild turns. If you’re scrolling Netflix wondering which series to start, this is my pick.

Cast

KJ Apa, Lili Reinhart, Camila Mendes, Cole Sprouse

Trivia

One of Riverdale High’s school productions is Carrie, the stage musical based on the horror story. And who lands the lead? Cheryl Blossom. Knowing that casting gives the songs an extra charge: the girl who works so hard to look untouchable gets onstage to play someone everyone picks on.
