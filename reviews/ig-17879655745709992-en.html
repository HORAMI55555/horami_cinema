A first love changes the way she looks at the sky.

Synopsis

High school student Mika falls for Hiro, a classmate who seems nothing like her. As events beyond their control intrude on their relationship, he suddenly pulls away. Mika has to carry on without knowing why.

My thoughts

A non-horror pick. This one's right up there with my favorite romance movies!

Cast

Yui Aragaki, Haruma Miura, Keisuke Koide

Trivia

Mika's story began as a Japanese cellphone novel published under the name Mika. Before audiences watched the romance on a cinema screen, readers followed it installment by installment on the tiny screens of their phones.
