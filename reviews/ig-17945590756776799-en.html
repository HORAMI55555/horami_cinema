The Tinder Swindler

2022

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★2/5

Why does a man this rich suddenly need my credit card?

Synopsis

On Tinder, several women meet a man who presents himself as a diamond heir with a private jet. When alarming messages arrive, they send money to someone they believe is in danger. Their separate romances start to look very different once the women’s accounts are put side by side.

My thoughts

This is a documentary about a real Tinder scam. Basically, an awful guy used the app to con women. His Instagram came up in the film, so I actually searched for it when I watched. At the time he was still using it and I could even see his Stories. That was such a surreal little extension of the movie.

Cast

Cecilie Fjellhøy, Pernilla Sjöholm and Ayleen Charlotte (as themselves)

Trivia

Producer Bernadette Higgins spent hours speaking to the women over Zoom, and the team had access to their full WhatsApp archives. That gives the film two perspectives on the same exchange: how it felt to receive a message that morning, and what it sounded like once the recipient knew more.
