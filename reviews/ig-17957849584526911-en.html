Saw

2004

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5Scare2/5

They wake up in chains. The first thing they find is a saw.

Synopsis

Two strangers wake up chained at opposite ends of a filthy bathroom. A body and a gun lie between them. Each man's tape gives him instructions that put the other's life in his hands.

My thoughts

For me, Saw is all about being locked in a room with a clock ticking and trying to crack the puzzle. Ghosts and psycho-killer scares aren't really what I took away from it. I didn't find it all that scary, though it does get pretty bloody. What I love about those first eight films is how they've scattered clues all through them.

Cast

Cary Elwes, Leigh Whannell

Trivia

Adam, the captive trying to piece things together, is played by screenwriter Leigh Whannell. The man puzzling over the rules on the bathroom floor is the man who wrote them.
