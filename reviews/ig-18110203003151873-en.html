リアル鬼ごっこ

2015

Scare0/5

School uniform, wedding dress, running kit. The chase keeps changing clothes.

Synopsis

A sudden catastrophe hits Mitsuko's school bus. Elsewhere Keiko is dressed for a wedding and Izumi runs a race. Across three seemingly disconnected lives, ordinary situations turn without warning into a chase.

My thoughts

Why was this one on the horror list, again? I had fun with it.

Cast

Reina Triendl, Mariko Shinoda, Erina Mano

Trivia

Sion Sono's 2015 version gives three actresses a school uniform, a wedding dress and a running kit. Each costume change unsettles the viewer as well as the runner: whose life did we think we were following a moment ago?
