Quarantine

2008

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★2/5Scare3.5/5

The camera is rolling. The exits are gone.

Synopsis

A reporter and her cameraman join a fire crew on a routine night shift. Inside an apartment building, a resident turns violently aggressive—and the authorities seal everyone in. With no clear explanation and no way out, the crew keeps filming as an ordinary assignment becomes a desperate fight to survive.

My thoughts

This reminded me of REC! Everything unfolds through the cameraman’s lens, which honestly isn’t my favorite style. What caught my attention was the film’s rabies-like outbreak involving an unvaccinated dog. It quickly goes way beyond what feels believable, but the premise definitely got under my skin. My heart was racing the whole time; horror beginners might find it overwhelming. For seasoned horror fans, though, it feels pretty B-movie.

Cast

Jennifer Carpenter, Steve Harris, Jay Hernandez

Trivia

Brothers John Erick and Drew Dowdle co-wrote the screenplay, with John directing—a sibling collaboration behind this claustrophobic nightmare.
