Fantasy Island

2020

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5Scare1/5

The island promises your fantasy. It says nothing about getting home.

Synopsis

Guests arrive at Mr. Roarke's island resort with one fantasy apiece. What looks like an elaborate performance becomes harder to explain when the island starts reproducing moments from their private lives.

My thoughts

I picked this because it was in the Netflix top ten when I watched it, and I had such a good time! I hadn't seen a setup quite like it. The twists kept coming, and I was into it the whole way through. It didn't feel especially scary to me, so I'd recommend it even if you're not into horror. Basically, everyone who visits the island gets their wish. How it ends is another matter.

Cast

Michael Peña, Maggie Q, Lucy Hale

Trivia

Roarke and his wish-granting resort originated on television. The film keeps the idea that each guest gets a different fantasy, then asks what happens when one person's dream becomes another guest's nightmare.
