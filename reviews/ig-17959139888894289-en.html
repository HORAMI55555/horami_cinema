Beneath Paris, the way out keeps slipping away.

Synopsis

Researcher Scarlett leads a group into the catacombs beneath Paris in pursuit of a historical mystery. Beyond the passages lined with bones, they encounter spaces no map can explain. As their escape routes shift, buried memories seem to follow them underground. The expedition becomes a descent into more than the city’s hidden depths.

My thoughts

Finally, a horror film that really pulled me in! The real Paris catacombs, with all those bones and skulls, make the setting feel so convincing. Partway through I was completely absorbed. That distinctive French atmosphere works so well with horror, and the ending had me going, wait, out THERE?! If you struggle with horror, this could be a lot.

Cast

Perdita Weeks · Ben Feldman · Edwin Hodge · François Civil

Trivia

The production shot inside the real catacombs, including sequences that might have been easier on a soundstage. Director John Erick Dowdle recalled people bumping their heads on the low ceilings. The cramped conditions on screen were also a very real challenge for the crew.
