Full House

2004

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5

She comes home from holiday to find a celebrity living in her house.

Synopsis

While aspiring writer Ji-eun is away, her friends sell her house to actor Young-jae. Desperate to get her family home back, she agrees to a marriage with an expiry date.

My thoughts

This is another older K-drama I still really like! If you want the kind of romance that’s just plain fun to watch, this is exactly the one I’d put on.

Cast

Song Hye-kyo, Rain

Trivia

The series takes its name from Won Soo-yeon’s comic and from the house itself. For Ji-eun, walking through that door means coming home to her family memories. For Young-jae, it’s a brand-new purchase. Their arguments make more sense once you see the house both ways.
