Boys Over Flowers

2009

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★5/5

Everyone steps aside for him. One girl stands her ground.

Synopsis

Jan-di, the daughter of dry cleaners, lands a place at the elite Shinhwa High School. When she sees how the wealthy quartet F4 treats other students, she stands up to its leader, Jun-pyo.

My thoughts

My number one K-drama since primary school. Nothing has topped it. I went through the manga, the novels and the film too! I remember hearing about a possible Keio University connection and finding that intriguing. If you’re wondering where to start with K-dramas, seriously, watch this. Lee Min-ho is gorgeous, Ku Hye-sun is adorable, and Jan-di makes me want to go through life with a heart like hers. It’s so romantic I could burst. It stays with me for ages after it ends.

Cast

Ku Hye-sun, Lee Min-ho, Kim Hyun-joong

Trivia

This Korean series adapts the Japanese manga Hana Yori Dango. If you know Tsukushi and Domyoji, compare their early clashes with Jan-di and Jun-pyo’s. The uniforms and language have changed, but there’s the same thrill when a room falls silent and one person speaks up.
