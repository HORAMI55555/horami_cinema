Lights Out

2016

Scare3/5

Turn the light back on. Please.

Synopsis

When her brother sees something at night, Rebecca confronts the darkness she feared as a child. The shape that appears only in the dark has a connection to their mother.

My thoughts

That was fun! Moderate horror for me, but the story was interesting and genuinely tense. The ending nearly made me cry.

Cast

Teresa Palmer, Gabriel Bateman, Maria Bello

Trivia

David F. Sandberg and Lotta Losten first explored the premise in a short. A scare built around flipping one light switch became the foundation for a feature-length family story.
