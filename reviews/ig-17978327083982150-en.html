Unsolved Mysteries

2020

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3.5/5Scare2.5/5

The unanswered cases are the hardest to look away from.

Synopsis

Unexplained disappearances, mysterious deaths and bewildering sightings: this documentary series examines unresolved cases and strange phenomena through interviews, records and reconstructions. Each episode approaches a different mystery through the people who witnessed it and the families still searching for answers.

My thoughts

This is so interesting. It reconstructs real stories in a documentary style, and every episode covers something different. None of them has been solved, so you end up thinking, “What happens next?” or “Who was the culprit, then?” But the cases themselves are so fascinating that it’s really enjoyable. It covers everything from murder and kidnapping to horror-type stories and what seem to be serious UFO eyewitness accounts. Try it—for example, the story shown on this cover is pretty scary.

Cast

Families, people connected to the cases and eyewitnesses, appearing as themselves

Trivia

The series descends from the long-running show hosted by Robert Stack. Co-creator Terry Dunn Meurer explains that Raymond Burr and Karl Malden hosted early specials before Stack became its regular face. Covering disappearances and paranormal phenomena alongside murders was also deliberate: the creators wanted a wide range of intriguing mysteries. That unusual mixture has been part of the program’s identity for decades.
