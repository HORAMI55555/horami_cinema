Good Boy

2022

The perfect date has a very unusual dog.

Synopsis

Christian seems like an ideal match: wealthy, charming and interested in Sigrid. But when she visits his home, she meets a man who dresses and behaves as a dog. Trying to keep an open mind, she continues seeing Christian, while the rules of his household begin to trouble her. A Norwegian psychological thriller about attraction and uncomfortable boundaries.

My thoughts

This was absolutely unhinged, hahaha. Why is Nordic horror so often this wild?! I kept saying, wait, WHAT? Please watch without reading too much about the plot. If you want something outrageous that you don’t have to overthink, here you go. This scratches an itch nothing else does.

Cast

Gard Løkke · Katrine Lovise Øpstad Fredriksen · Nicolai Narvesen Lied

Trivia

Directed by Viljar Bøe. Viewing tip: watch not only the unusual “dog,” but also how casually the homeowner presents the arrangement. The tension begins with what someone is willing to accept—and when they decide to step away.
