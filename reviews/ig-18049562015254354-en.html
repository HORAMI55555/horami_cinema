Synopsis

A family moves into a long-abandoned smart home and reactivates Cassandra, its household assistant. At first, her help with daily life seems welcome. But her involvement grows increasingly intrusive as the house’s past and her desire for a perfect family begin to emerge.

My thoughts

It has that familiar M3GAN-like setup where something human seems to inhabit a robot and things gradually go wrong. But here, the story also follows that person’s past, which was so sad that I stayed invested the whole way through. Ahh, the ending is heartbreaking and so cruel. It’s a series, but it goes by quickly. I didn’t find it very scary, so I’d recommend it widely.

Cast

Lavinia Wilson · Mina Tander · Michael Klammer · Joshua Kantara

Trivia

The smart home has stood empty since the 1970s, giving its advanced technology a deliberately retro setting. That collision of old-fashioned machines and futuristic convenience is part of its distinctive look. Filming took place in Cologne, Germany.
