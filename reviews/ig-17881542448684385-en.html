Winchester

2018

Scare3/5

Tonight the builders add a room for the dead.

Synopsis

Sarah Winchester, heiress to a firearms fortune, keeps builders at work on her sprawling house. Sent to assess her mental health, a doctor discovers that she associates its rooms with people killed by Winchester guns.

My thoughts

Just finished it. Really enjoyed the story even though it wasn't all that scary. The house and its owner were real, and the house is still there. I'd love to visit.

Cast

Helen Mirren, Jason Clarke, Sarah Snook

Trivia

Sarah's real house stands in San Jose, California. The film takes the peculiar layout of a building repeatedly enlarged over the years and imagines a ghost story in which individual rooms have a purpose.
