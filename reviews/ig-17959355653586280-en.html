The Kissing Booth

2018

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5

The one boy she wants to kiss is the one boy their rules put off-limits.

Synopsis

Best friends Elle and Lee live by a set of rules, including one that makes Lee’s older brother Noah off-limits. When they run a kissing booth at their school carnival, a kiss leaves Elle with feelings she can’t bring herself to tell her best friend about.

My thoughts

I’d seen this one before, too! It’s exactly the kind of American high school romance you picture when someone says “teen movie.” I had fun with it and would recommend it!

Cast

Joey King, Joel Courtney, Jacob Elordi

Trivia

Beth Reekles wrote the story between homework assignments and posted it on Wattpad. Readers found it there before a publisher offered her a deal. All that school-carnival drama came from someone who still had actual assignments of her own to hand in.
