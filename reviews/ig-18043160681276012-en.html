Irish Wish

2024

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3.5/5

What if your dream wedding is the wrong dream?

Synopsis

Maddie travels to Ireland for the wedding of her best friend and the writer she secretly loves. After wishing she were the bride, she wakes up living that exact life. But getting the man she dreamed of does not necessarily mean finding the life that fits her, especially when an unexpected encounter complicates everything.

My thoughts

This was so good!!! You should watch it! She wishes she could marry the man she has always wanted, and it happens—but being with someone you want and being with someone who suits you are completely different things. It is bright and funny, but there is something genuinely thoughtful underneath. Exactly!!

Cast

Lindsay Lohan, Ed Speleers, Alexander Vlahos

Trivia

Those storybook landscapes are real Irish locations. Tourism Ireland identifies Killruddery House, Lough Tay, Westport and the Cliffs of Moher among the filming spots. That makes the film a little location-hopping escape as well as a romantic fantasy. A viewing detail worth noticing is the contrast between Maddie at the elaborate wedding and Maddie out in the landscape: which setting actually lets her look comfortable? The scenery offers a lovely way into the question of what her ideal life means.
