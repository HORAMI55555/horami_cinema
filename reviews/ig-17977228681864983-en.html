Chilling Adventures of Sabrina

2018

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★4/5

Becoming a witch should not mean giving up her own life.

Synopsis

Approaching her sixteenth birthday, Sabrina must navigate life as both a mortal teenager and a witch. Her friends and boyfriend belong to one world; her family’s traditions bind her to another. As a ceremony approaches, she tries to protect the people she loves without surrendering the right to choose her own future. Teenage life and occult danger collide in this dark fantasy.

My thoughts

I started Sabrina after her brief appearance in Riverdale, where a bittersweet reunion and goodbye with her late boyfriend caught my attention. So good! The ending left me going “Wait, what?” and wanting more, but I had such a great time. It feels like a mix of Harry Potter-style dark fantasy and an American teen high-school drama. There are four parts to get into, and seeing Sabrina turn up in Riverdale afterward makes it even more exciting!

Cast

Kiernan Shipka · Ross Lynch · Miranda Otto · Lucy Davis · Chance Perdomo

Trivia

The Riverdale connection has a creative foundation: the series draws on the Archie Comics horror comic by writer Roberto Aguirre-Sacasa and artist Robert Hack. Aguirre-Sacasa also wrote the television adaptation and served as Riverdale’s showrunner. The comic reimagines the familiar teenage witch through darker occult origins. Knowing that background adds another layer to the show’s combination of adolescent relationships and a sinister supernatural world.
