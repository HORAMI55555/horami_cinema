A Family Affair

2024

Recommended☆☆☆☆☆★★★★★3/5

Her difficult boss is dating her mother.

Synopsis

Zara is tired of working as an assistant to demanding movie star Chris Cole. Then he falls for her mother, Brooke. As their romance develops, Zara struggles to separate the boss she knows from the man her mother sees. Work frustrations, family boundaries and the possibility of a fresh start collide.

My thoughts

This was nice! A familiar kind of movie about family tensions and worries, but it left me feeling happy. A good choice when you want something easy to put on.

Cast

Nicole Kidman, Zac Efron, Joey King, Kathy Bates

Trivia

Kidman and Efron previously worked together in The Paperboy. Their existing rapport returns here in a very different setting: a romantic comedy.
